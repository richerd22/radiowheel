The short version: CircoLoco Records is a record label run as a partnership between CircoLoco, the Monday party at DC10 in Ibiza, and Rockstar Games, announced on May 24, 2021.194,195 It opened with Monday Dreamin', a 20-track compilation whose four EPs became hidden collectibles in GTA Online in July 2021, and since 2024 it has turned into a chart label for house and tech house.88,183,196 A "CircoLoco Records Radio" station for GTA VI exists only in a leak; Rockstar did not name one in its October 2026 station reveal.197,139

What is CircoLoco, and what is DC10?#

DC10 is a club on the Carretera de Salinas in Ibiza. It started in the 1990s as a music bar licensed for 80 people, opened by the brothers Deogracias and Antonio Lara Moreno.198 In 1999 two Italian promoters, Antonio Carbonaro and Andrea Pelino, joined as partners and created CircoLoco, which began as a free Monday-morning after-party.198 The venue now holds 1,500 across three rooms (La Terrace, The Main Room and The Open Air Garden), and ranked 19th in DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs in 2020.198

CircoLoco dates its own start to "The Monday Morning Sessions" in 1999.199 Carbonaro calls it the longest-running electronic music brand among its peers, a claim nobody has measured.199 He also founded No Soul For Sale, which began as the slogan for CircoLoco's 20th year and became its own platform.199 The 25th anniversary in 2024 got a "DC10 Ibiza CircoLoco Greatest Hits" release.198

The Monday regulars include Seth Troxler, The Martinez Brothers, Tania Vulcano, Loco Dice and Luciano.196 How a party like this runs as a business is its own subject, at https://lastcall.cold.fyi/.

When did CircoLoco Records launch?#

Rockstar Games and CircoLoco announced CircoLoco Records on May 24, 2021.194,201 The plan was a weekly run of EPs taken from the album Monday Dreamin', with Seth Troxler's "Lumartes" already out as a single.200 The first EP was due June 4 and the full compilation July 9, 2021.194,200

Carbonaro traces the idea to his friendship with Sam Houser and a joint CircoLoco and Rockstar event in Miami in October 2019; the label itself was put together during the pandemic.199 It was a way to reach the community while clubs were shut for COVID-19.196 A label is a reasonable thing to build when the dance floor is closed and the game is not.

The company behind the name is Rockstar Records LLC, founded May 24, 2021, with "CircoLoco Records" as its trade name - though that comes from fan and trade sites, not a company filing.202,203

Does Rockstar own CircoLoco Records?#

It is a partnership. At launch the label was billed as independent and kept separate from both companies' other businesses for now.195 Nobody has published an ownership split.195,178

Rockstar fielded founder Sam Houser and director of music Ivan Pavlovich for the launch. "Music's been part of our DNA since day one," Pavlovich told Rolling Stone.195 Houser's stated aim was to "find new ways to bring the very best underground music to the widest audience possible."201 Nick Benedetti spoke for CircoLoco, and the 2021 artists were picked jointly, all of them CircoLoco alumni.195

The work splits roughly like this:

  • Rockstar's side: placement in its games (the Media Player sticks, a CircoLoco tee in Los Santos Tuners) and the "CircoLoco / Rockstar Games presents" credit on releases.202,183,204
  • CircoLoco's side: the A&R, the DC10 floor where tracks get tested, and the visual identity, with Rockstar as partner and backer.196

How does the label pick its records?#

A&R (artists and repertoire) is the job of finding artists and choosing what gets released. Carbonaro says there is no fixed formula: signings go on instinct and on whether an act fits CircoLoco.196 His test, in four words: "It has to feel honest."196

In practice, that means:196

  • artists who shape a scene rather than fit into one
  • a mix of new and established names
  • tracks tested on the dance floor in front of the party's crowd
  • a look that is part of the record: a blood-red palette that echoes the club, and surreal digital art for Monday Dreamin'

The catalog shows the taste moving. The 2021 compilation leans on long-time CircoLoco names such as Luciano, Carl Craig and Moodymann; from 2024 on the releases tilt toward chart tech house and house, with Prospa, PAWSA, Jamback and Michael Bibi, and Prospa turning up more than anyone.205,206,204,207 That shift is plain in the release list; the label hasn't described it.

Who made "Positive" on CircoLoco Records?#

Jamback, a Dutch producer, alone. "Positive" is his CircoLoco Records debut, released November 7, 2025.234,235 Bandcamp, Crack Magazine, Chromatic Club and Beatport all credit him with no co-artist; if you remember a featured name on it, it isn't on the record.234,235,241,196 The track was shaped by the loss of his mother.235

Then it parked at the top of Beatport. By May 2026 "Positive" had spent 64 days at No. 1, the longest run in the store's history, passing FISHER's "Losing It" at 62 days in 2018.196,241 Technoairlines' 12-month chart-points ranking, as of November 2026, puts it first in the label's catalog.240

Its catalog number is probably CLR021, though the only evidence is a press image filename.235

What is Prospa's "Don't Stop"?#

"Don't Stop" is a Prospa single released on CircoLoco Records on April 11, 2025, catalog CLR015 (Discogs lists it as CLR015BP).227,228 It builds on a hook from Somethin' For The People's 1997 song "My Love Is the Shhh!".242 RaveJungle ran its release-day story on April 12, 2025; Discogs and RockstarINTEL both say April 11.242,227,204

Prospa are Harvey Blumler and Gosha Smith, a duo from Leeds.237 By the time of "Don't Stop" they were already the label's busiest act, after "If You Want My Loving" (CRL 010) and "This Rhythm" (CRL 013), which had passed 7 million streams by 2025.203,225,242 Technoairlines' November 2026 ranking has "Don't Stop" second in the catalog.240

What is "Free Your Mind" by Prospa and Cloonee?#

Two things share the name. "Free Your Mind" is the title track with Cloonee, and Beatport-derived charts also credit Sybil.237,240 Free Your Mind is Prospa's debut album, released on CircoLoco Records on June 5, 2026; deejay.de lists the 2 x 12-inch vinyl (CLR019V) for July 16, 2026.237,238

The album features Josh Baker and RAHH on "You Don't Own Me" and Kosmo Kint on "Love Songs".237 In May 2026, "Baby" and "Free Your Mind" held No. 1 and No. 2 on the Beatport Top 100, and Beatport named CircoLoco Records its Label of the Month.196 Technoairlines' November 2026 ranking puts the title track fifth in the label's 12-month chart.240

How do I get the CircoLoco Records music in GTA Online?#

Through the Media Player, the radio-wheel slot for collectible Media Sticks, added with Los Santos Tuners on July 20, 2021; the GTA Online section explains how it works.88,181 Monday Dreamin' plays at the LS Car Meet, and four EP sticks - Black, Blue, Violet and Green - are hidden around the city.183,88 Find all four and you unlock Seth Troxler's CLR Launch Party mix and a CircoLoco tee.183

Troxler built that mix by reworking the Monday Dreamin' stems, and it debuted with Los Santos Tuners.196 Rockstar's notes and GTA Base say it reworks all 20 album tracks; the fan wiki lists 16.183,181,88

The NEZ EP was spread through the game before release. "You Wanna?" came as a Media Stick in The Contract (December 2021); ScHoolboy Q's "Let's Get It", produced by NEZ, premiered on Radio Los Santos; and "Freaks" with Moodymann and Gangsta Boo was heard first in the Los Santos Tuners trailer and sits in Kenny's Backyard Boogie mix.243,184,244 Rockstar said all three would be heard only in GTA Online until the EP came out, which it did on February 25, 2022.184,244

After that the trail goes cold. The release posts from CRL 004 through CRL 019 never mention GTA Online, and no later CircoLoco Records release has been documented in the game.204

Is the Music Locker part of CircoLoco Records?#

Nothing on record ties them together. The Music Locker is the club under The Diamond Casino added with The Cayo Perico Heist in December 2020, with Moodymann, Keinemusik and Palms Trax as residents - five months before the label was announced.172 The overlap is artists, not paperwork: at the label's launch, GTA Online's nightclubs already featured CircoLoco alumni such as The Blessed Madonna, Dixon and Solomun, and Moodymann later turned up on the Monday Dreamin' Green EP.201,209 Shared DJs don't make it a CircoLoco Records room.

Does GTA VI have a CircoLoco Records radio station?#

Yes. CircoLoco Records Radio is one of the 22 stations in the game at launch, playing house and techno.3,137 The first songs logged on it are Skream's "Floral," Michael Bibi's "Rhythm of the Night" and Mind Against's "Let It Go"; GameRant adds "Deep N Sexy" by Rossi. and Demi.3,245 No host has been named.

The station was a leak first. On August 28, 2026, We Rave You reported a clip, said to come from an unfinished build, showing the name; neither Rockstar nor CircoLoco confirmed it, and Rockstar's reveal of six stations on October 8 left it out.197,139 It took the game coming out to settle it. The full dial is in the GTA VI section, and the songs are on the station's page.

Quick answers

Is CircoLoco Records owned by Rockstar Games?
Partly. Rolling Stone reported in May 2021 that CircoLoco and Rockstar Games co-own the label and would run it as a separate, independent business. The ownership split has not been published.
Who made "Positive" on CircoLoco Records?
Dutch producer Jamback, solo; no co-artist is credited anywhere it is listed. It came out November 7, 2025, and Beatport reported in May 2026 that it had spent 64 days at No. 1, the longest run in Beatport's history.
Is "Don't Stop" by Prospa on CircoLoco Records?
Yes. It came out April 11, 2025, catalog number CLR015, and builds on a hook from Somethin' For The People's 1997 song "My Love Is the Shhh!".
Who is on "Free Your Mind" by Prospa?
The title track features Cloonee, and some chart listings also credit Sybil. It opens Prospa's debut album, out on CircoLoco Records June 5, 2026, which also features Josh Baker, RAHH, Kosmo Kint and Murda Beatz.
How do I get the CircoLoco Media Sticks in GTA Online?
Since Los Santos Tuners (July 20, 2021), four Monday Dreamin' EP sticks - Black, Blue, Violet and Green - are hidden around the map and play on the Media Player. Collecting all four unlocks Seth Troxler's CLR Launch Party mix and a CircoLoco tee.
Is the Music Locker a CircoLoco Records club?
Nothing ties them together. The Music Locker opened with The Cayo Perico Heist in December 2020, five months before the label was announced, with Moodymann, Keinemusik and Palms Trax as residents.
