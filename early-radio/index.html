The short version: This section walks through GTA radio game by game, from the first GTA in 1997 to Vice City Stories in 2006. In the first two games almost everything on air was written in-house under fake band names; GTA III (2001) mixed those acts with licensed music and talk radio, Vice City (2002) went all in on licensed 1980s tracks, and San Andreas (2004) grew to 10 music stations with celebrity DJs. The two "Stories" games of 2005 and 2006 went back to Liberty City and Vice City with fresh playlists on the same formulas.

Station counts below come from fan wiki station pages, press coverage of each launch, and the station rows in this guide's own data.

Was the music in the first GTA real or made for the game?#

Made for the game. The radio was Colin Anderson's way to keep Craig Conner's demos in many genres, a story told in full at origins.5 The game shipped with seven stations covering hip hop, funk, pop, rock, country, house and drum 'n' bass, and Anderson treated avoiding licensed music as a core principle.5

Per Grand Theft Wiki, the seven were Brooklyn Underground FM (trance), The Fix FM (techno), The Fergus Buckner Show FM (country), Head Radio FM (pop and rock), It's Unleashed FM (alternative and hard rock), N-CT FM (hip hop) and Radio '76 FM, which the wiki says is announced in-game as Funk FM.33 The bands were invented: Retrograde, Rotorman, Bleeding Stump, Sideways Hank O'Malley, Da Shootaz, with songs credited to Conner, Anderson, Grant Middleton, Brian Baglow and Robert DeNegro.33 The main theme, "Gangster Friday" by the fictional Slumpussy, plays on no station. On PC the station audio is plain CD audio, so the disc plays in a stereo and you could swap in your own CD.33

Did GTA 2 have licensed music?#

A little. GTA 2 used only a few licensed songs, and its real change was the talk between them: DJs and ads finally made it in, and they set the satirical tone the series still runs on.5 Who wrote those ads is covered at lore.

Per Wikipedia, GTA 2 came out on PlayStation and Windows on October 22, 1999, followed by Dreamcast in May 2000 and Game Boy Color in December 2000.34

Per StrategyWiki, there are 11 stations and each part of the city hears five of them. Head Radio covers the whole city, and each gang gets its own station: Funami FM for the Yakuza, Heavenly Radio for the Krishnas, KGBH for the Russian mafia, Osmosis for the Scientists.35 Gang stations made the radio part of the map: what you heard told you whose turf you were on. Per gta.wiki, five tracks were licensed - Bula Matari "Taxi Drivers", Flytronix "Pendulum" and "Past Archives", Davidson "All I Wanna Do", Track 7 "I Wanna Phunk" - and the rest came from in-house writers including Conner, Anderson and Stuart Ross.36 The two wikis disagree on several names: Lo-Fi FM's DJ is "DJ Die/Dye" or "DJ Dai", KGBH's host is "Bombatumba" or "Bomba Tomba and Prodo", and Osmosis is Radio or FM depending on the page.35,36

The menu theme is E-Z Rollers' "Short Change", and gta.wiki lists a "GTA 2: The Movie Soundtrack" of eight 1998 tracks from the Moving Shadow label, mostly drum and bass.36

What was on the radio in GTA London 1969?#

London 1969 and its follow-up London 1961 were made by Rockstar Toronto without Anderson, and they brought in licensed acts such as The Upsetters and Harry J All Stars.5 Anderson's verdict: they "broke the purity of the creative vision."5

Per Wikipedia, London 1969 came out on April 30, 1999 for PC and PlayStation, and London 1961 followed on July 1, 1999 as a free PC-only download.37

Per Grand Theft Wiki, the two share 13 stations with identical playlists.38 Most of the period sound is not British pop but late-1960s and early-1970s Italian film scores by Riz Ortolani, Piero Umiliani and Francesco De Masi, next to reggae and ska from The Upsetters ("Return of Django"), Harry J Allstars ("The Liquidator") and Symarip.38 Four stations carry original Rockstar-made tracks with names like "GTA Pomp" and "Austin Allegro Drag Race", and the wiki names no DJs.38 In this guide's reading, it is the first GTA radio built on crate digging rather than a staff songwriter.

What were the radio stations in GTA III?#

Nine, and GTA III is where the modern formula clicked: in-house fictional pop, licensed specialist stations, and a talk station. Per Grand Theft Wiki, they are Head Radio, Double Clef FM, K-Jah, Rise FM, Lips 106, Game FM, MSX FM, Flashback 95.6 and Chatterbox FM.39

Head Radio and Lips 106 play fictional artists made for the game.39 The track list on gta.wiki credits Head Radio's Dil-Don't and Scatwerk, and Lips 106's Fatamarse and Boyz 2 Girlz, to Conner and Stuart Ross; Lips 106 also replays Da Shootaz "Grand Theft Auto" from the 1997 game.40 The licensed side is sharp and specific: K-Jah plays five cuts from Scientist's 1981 vampire-themed dub album, Flashback 95.6 plays Giorgio Moroder's Scarface material, MSX FM is drum and bass mixed by DJ Timecode, and Game FM (Game Radio on gta.wiki) is mixed by Stretch Armstrong with Royce Da 5'9" and Black Rob.39,40 Double Clef FM plays opera arias by Mozart, Verdi and Puccini.40

Voices per Grand Theft Wiki: Michael Hunt (Russ Mottla) on Head Radio, Andee (Shelley Miller) on Lips 106, Toni (Maria Chambers) on Flashback, with Stretch Armstrong and Lord Sear, and MC Codebreaker, playing themselves.39 Chatterbox FM is call-in talk radio hosted by Lazlow, with 33 credited guest voices including Dan Houser, Kyle MacLachlan and Michael Madsen.39

What were all the radio stations in Vice City, and who were the DJs?#

Nine: seven music, two talk. Rockstar's own exhibit for London's Design Museum, as summarized by iGrandTheftAuto, framed it as an unprecedented soundtrack of licensed 1980s music plus original talk.41 Space was tight: the sound-effect budget held under 12 seconds' worth of CD audio, so city sounds had to be very short.41

The seven music stations come to 103 songs in gta.wiki's lists, from Hall and Oates and The Buggles on Flash FM to Iron Maiden and Slayer on V-Rock.43 V-Rock also carries Love Fist, a fictional band written in-house by Allan Walker.43 Not every song stayed: gta.wiki says Michael Jackson's "Billie Jean" is missing from the 2012 mobile release and every re-release after it.43 The full list of cuts is at removed songs.

Is the Vice City soundtrack on CD?#

Yes. Rockstar partnered with Sony Music Entertainment and Epic Records on seven CDs, one per music station, each about an hour long with the DJ chatter and fake ads kept in.44 Station imaging - the IDs, links and jokes between songs - was part of the product. The volumes ran V-Rock, Wave 103, Emotion 98.3, Flash FM, Wildstyle, Fever 105, Espantoso; discs were $13.98 each or $49.98 for the set (2002, reported), and each disc worked as a key disc on the vicecityradio.com website.44 GamesFirst's December 2002 review gave an MSRP of $49.99 with most online stores at $39.99, and noted the box sold to everyone while the game was PS2-only.45

NME quotes Terry Donovan, then COO, from the release: "Every track was lovingly and laboriously chosen."11 Per Grand Theft Wiki, the set came out internationally on October 29, 2002, European editions carry extra songs, K-Chat and VCPR were never released, and a Greatest Hits disc followed on April 30, 2003.46

Who voiced the DJs in San Andreas?#

San Andreas is set in 1992 and carried 10 music stations plus the talk station WCTR, which GameSpot called the series' biggest yet.47 New were funk, rare groove, alternative, country (K-Rose) and a second hip-hop station; out went Latin, classical, public radio, drum 'n' bass and the 1980s.47 Rare groove - crate-dug funk and soul - got its own station, Master Sounds 98.3.47

The DJ booth was a casting coup. Per GameSpot: Axl Rose as K-DST's Tommy "The Nightmare" Smith, George Clinton as Bounce FM's The Funktipus, Chuck D as Playback FM's Forth Right MC, Michael Bivins as CSR's Philip "PM" Michaels, Sly Dunbar and Robbie Shakespeare on K-Jah West, and Julio G as himself on Radio Los Santos.47 Defining picks include Lynyrd Skynyrd "Free Bird" on K-DST, Public Enemy "Rebel Without a Pause" on Playback, Soundgarden "Rusty Cage" on Radio X and Mr. Fingers "Can You Feel It?" on SF-UR.48 Sources disagree on CSR's frequency: GameSpot lists 103.2, while Grand Theft Wiki gives 103.9.47,49

WCTR's shows per Grand Theft Wiki include WCTR News with Richard Burns (Wil Wheaton), Gardening with Maurice (Andy Dick), the conspiracy show Area 53, and Entertaining America, hosted by Billy Dexter and Lazlow.49 Wheaton wrote that the session was "some of the most fun I've ever had."50

What San Andreas soundtrack albums came out?#

Two. The "Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas Official Soundtrack" came out on Interscope on November 23, 2004 as two CDs (12 and 11 tracks) plus a DVD with the machinima short "The Introduction".51 An eight-disc box followed with one volume per station: Bounce FM, Playback FM and Radio Los Santos, Master Sounds, K-Rose, CSR, K-Jah West, K-DST and Radio X, leaving out SF-UR and WCTR; IGN scored it 7.3 out of 10 and NME 9 out of 10.51 The dates differ: Wikipedia gives December 7, 2004, while Gameswelt reported December 6 for Europe, with more than 80 tracks plus ads and DJ talk.51,52

What stations are in Liberty City Stories?#

Liberty City Stories (2005) returned to GTA III's city with 10 stations: nine music plus the talk station LCFR, revealed in a full list on October 25, 2005.53 Several names came back with new playlists. Head Radio and Lips 106 again play in-house tracks with writer credits including Craig Conner and Will Morton; Flashback FM is all Giorgio Moroder; Rise FM moves to 1990s house (Moloko, Ultra Naté, Josh Wink); and The Liberty Jam is mid-1990s East Coast rap from Mobb Deep, Method Man and Raekwon, hosted by DJ Clue as himself.54,53 New was Radio Del Mundo, a world music station with tracks like Ofra Haza "Im Nin'Alu" and Asha Bhosle "Dum Maro Dum".55 The nine music stations total 84 songs in GTABase's list.55

Voices per Vandal: Michael Hunt (Russ Mottla) and Andee (Shelley Miller) return, MC Codebreaker and DJ Timecode play themselves on MSX 98, and LCFR runs five shows, with Chatterbox hosted by Lazlow.53 Spellings vary between Vandal and gta.wiki (Panja or Panjit, Natalee or Natalie), and Vandal's credit of "Ed McMann" for Lips 106's Cliff is unconfirmed elsewhere.53,54

Is Phil Collins really in Vice City Stories?#

Yes. Vice City Stories (2006) features a computer-animated Phil Collins performing "In the Air Tonight", shown in the third trailer; in the story, Victor Vance gets him to his concert safely.56,57 At the July 2006 character reveal, GamesRadar listed Lazlow as confirmed voice talent.58 Per Wikipedia, he appears as the intern to his real friend Couzin Ed at V-Rock.8

There are eight music stations, and TheGTAPlace, citing IGN, reported "over 100 individual tracks", billed as the most of any GTA soundtrack, sold exclusively on the Urge download store from October 31, 2006.59 All eight reached US iTunes as an iMix on April 13, 2007.60 Per gta.wiki and GTABase, the stations are Flash FM, V-Rock, Paradise FM (no DJ), VCPR, VCFL, Wave 103, Fresh FM, Radio Espantoso and Emotion 98.3.61,62 The music lists add up to 105 songs: Paradise FM's post-disco from Change and Sister Sledge, Fresh FM's electro from Afrika Bambaataa and The Egyptian Lover, and Flash FM's Laura Branigan.61

What talk stations ran through the early games?#

Talk stations passed down a line: Chatterbox FM, then K-Chat and VCPR, then WCTR, LCFR and VCPR again.39,42,49,54,61 Lazlow is the thread: he hosted Chatterbox FM in GTA III, V-Rock in Vice City, Entertaining America on WCTR, Chatterbox again on LCFR, and showed up at V-Rock in Vice City Stories.8 His story, and the shows themselves, are at talk radio.

Quick answers

Was the music in the first GTA real or made for the game?
Made for the game. All seven stations in GTA (1997) played original music by DMA Design's own musicians, credited to invented bands, and sound designer Colin Anderson treated avoiding licensed music as a core principle.
What were all the radio stations in GTA Vice City?
Nine: Wildstyle, Flash FM, Fever 105, V-Rock, Emotion 98.3, Wave 103 and Radio Espantoso for music, plus the talk stations K-Chat and VCPR. Lazlow hosts V-Rock; per Grand Theft Wiki, Toni (Maria Chambers) hosts Flash FM.
Is the GTA Vice City soundtrack on CD?
Yes. Epic Records and Sony Music released seven CDs in 2002, one per music station, each with the DJ chatter and fake ads. They sold for $13.98 each or $49.98 as a set (reported); the talk stations were never released.
Who voiced the DJs in GTA San Andreas?
Per GameSpot's 2004 launch coverage, Axl Rose hosts K-DST, George Clinton hosts Bounce FM, Chuck D hosts Playback FM, Michael Bivins hosts CSR, and Sly Dunbar and Robbie Shakespeare host K-Jah West. Julio G plays himself on Radio Los Santos.
Did GTA 2 have licensed music?
Only a little. Per gta.wiki, five tracks were licensed, including Bula Matari's "Taxi Drivers" and two Flytronix tracks; the rest was written in-house. GTA 2's bigger change was adding DJs and satirical ads.
Is Phil Collins really in GTA Vice City Stories?
Yes. A computer-animated Phil Collins performs "In the Air Tonight" in the game, and one storyline has Victor Vance getting him safely to his concert. The song also plays on Emotion 98.3.
