Who makes the music at Rockstar

On this frequency

The short version: The habit started in a record company. Sam Houser began in BMG's London post room in 1990, BMG's games arm published the first Grand Theft Auto, and GTA's radio was pitched in Scotland by Colin Anderson of DMA Design's sound department so one musician's demos across many genres could all survive.4,5 Since then a small in-house team has picked, hosted, written and mastered the stations, with Sam Houser as the documented final word on taste. Dan Houser left in March 2020, and Lazlow later that year, on a date nobody quite agrees on.6,7,8

Did the Housers come from the music business?#

Yes, and the paper trail is dated. Sam Houser started at BMG Records in London in 1990 as a 19-year-old sorting mail, then spent 1991 to 1994 working part time on pop videos and VHS releases while at university.9,4 In 1994 he moved to BMG's interactive division, and by 1996 he was head of development at BMG Interactive, the games arm of Bertelsmann Music Group.9

BMG Interactive released Grand Theft Auto in late 1997. In March 1998 Bertelsmann had BMG sell the division to Take-Two.4 The price depends on who tells it. Sam Houser put the total consideration at $9 million; Strauss Zelnick, then BMG's worldwide CEO, called it "for a penny, essentially."4 Take-Two announced the deal on March 12, 1998, for about 1.85 million shares, roughly 16% of the company. Rockstar Games was set up in December 1998 and announced on January 22, 1999.10

The other founders had label or BMG backgrounds too. Dan Houser, Jamie King and Gary Foreman came from BMG Interactive, and Terry Donovan from BMG Entertainment's Arista Records.10 NME's Vice City retrospective draws the line: time at BMG and in music video gave the Housers label connections when the licensing era arrived.11

Dan Houser's start is thinner on paper. He took a part-time job testing CD-ROMs at BMG Interactive in 1995 and was full time until 1996, with no job title on record.12

Did Rockstar's founders come out of 1990s London raves?#

It is a popular claim with nothing behind it. No source ties Sam or Dan Houser to the UK rave or club scene of the early 1990s. What is documented is a New York club night, years later.

In 1999 Sam Houser and Terry Donovan, himself a former DJ, partnered with John Davis, promoter of the New York party Body & Soul, to launch Rockstar Loft.10 The first night ran on October 30, 1999, in Chelsea with Andi Hanley, Bob Sinclar and Dimitri from Paris. There were no game installations, only music, and it wound down in 2000.10 Fanbyte calls it short-lived and one of the company's biggest missteps.13

The clearest thread from that club night to the games runs through Andi Hanley. He spent most of 15 years as a warm-up DJ at Body & Soul and held down musical supervision at Rockstar, years unstated.14 By 2008 he was co-supervising the GTA IV soundtrack.15 For more on how New York's long-running parties worked, see https://lastcall.cold.fyi/.

Why did the first GTA have radio stations?#

Because a musician wrote too many demos. At DMA Design in Dundee, Craig Conner had written tracks across many genres. Rather than cut two-thirds of them to fit one style, Anderson proposed one station per genre, played on car radios.5 Audio was low priority at DMA, memory budgets were tight, and not everyone liked the idea.5

The soundtrack was first planned as MIDI, partly because an N64 version was in the works. When that version was cancelled, the team switched to CD-quality audio so the stations would sound like real radio.5 GTA shipped in 1997 with seven stations of original music by DMA's own musicians. DJs and ads were dreamed up early but had to wait for GTA 2, for memory and budget reasons.5 The station-by-station story is at early radio.

The music was in-house because licensing was out of reach. Conner wrote beats and rough lyrics, and rapper Jonathan Wilson, credited as "Robert De Negro," refined and performed them under fictional act names such as Da Shootaz and Slumpussy.16 More on that at the scores.

Talk radio was a budget fix too: for GTA III (2001), Dan Houser and Lazlow Jones pitched it as cheap filler.17 The full story is at talk radio.

Craig Conner (some outlets spell it Connor or Connors; credits use Conner) is the through line of the first era. With Allan Walker he oversaw music and audio on all five GTA games released by early 2005, working out of Rockstar North in Dundee and later Edinburgh.18 GamesTM, by way of a 2015 IBTimes UK piece, credits him as music director from GTA 1 through GTA V, with Red Dead Redemption and Manhunt on the side.19 On September 30, 2014, he and Will Morton left to found Solid Audioworks; Conner had been at the company since 1995, Morton since 2002 as a senior audio designer.25 Between them, over 30 man-years at Rockstar.20

Ivan Pavlovich came from Chicago's techno and house scene, where he worked with Green Velvet at Cajual Records and founded Guidance Recordings in 1996.21 He joined Rockstar in 2004 and holds the title Director of Music.21 Fact put his start at about 15 years before 2018, after he had worked on two Rockstar soundtracks from outside.26

Lazlow Jones co-wrote the radio with Dan Houser from GTA III and hosted stations from Chatterbox to Integrity 2.0. He later wrote and audio-directed Red Dead Redemption 2.8 By the time he left, his titles were Director, Producer, Writer and co-chair of the Rockstar Games Production department.7

Scores sit partly outside the building. Woody Jackson has been Rockstar's go-to composer on most of its titles since Red Dead Redemption (2010), and GTA V's score paired Tangerine Dream, Jackson, The Alchemist and Oh No.27,28 Alastair MacGregor of Rockstar North presented GTA V's audio at GDC 2014.29

How does Rockstar pick the songs for GTA radio?#

Conner's account of the GTA 1 to GTA V era: set each station's style, lock in a few certain tracks, then keep adding and dropping until the day before final submission.19 He researched each genre with help from people inside it, who often ended up as the station's DJ. Sam Houser named each station. Then the DJ links and idents were recorded, and Conner mastered everything.19 An ident is the short branding jingle or voice tag between songs.

Each game set its own brief. For GTA IV, Pavlovich and Hanley sourced music under Sam Houser's call for a contemporary Liberty City sound. Contemporary hip hop, dance rock and Russian music got much of the airtime early, alongside classic New York music from the likes of CBGB's and Studio 54, plus hip hop, jazz and afrobeat.15

GTA V shows the scale. The team planned to license over 900 tracks and narrowed them to 241, a 2013 Polygon figure; Pavlovich told Rolling Stone 240 (the full dial is at modern radio).30,28 The station concepts were regional, from Los Angeles and California to a Mexican station curated by Camilo Lara and West Coast Classics hosted by DJ Pooh. Flying Lotus was approached first, after a meeting at South by Southwest. Pavlovich called it "the most exciting soundtrack we've done," for the hosts as much as the music.28

"We've always seen our job as curators of music for the experience of GTA," Pavlovich told Rolling Stone.31 The team goes to clubs and watches sets, Boiler Room included.31 He told Fact the picks come from fandom rather than chasing what is cool, and that several staff grew up in London while he grew up in Chicago.26 The music team comes in early and sits down with Sam Houser; if a game plans a nightclub, they choose DJs who fit it.21

Nobody has described Rockstar staff doing literal crate digging - record shops, vinyl runs. What is on record is club-going, genre advisers who become hosts, artist-curated stations and unreleased material from artists' own circles.

How do they make 11 stations sound like one radio dial?#

By mastering them together. On San Andreas (2004), Conner mastered 11 stations, 155 tracks and 70 commercials plus DJ dialogue.18 Tracks play in random order, so tracks, ads and DJ segments all had to sit at one consistent level. The aim was one collection rather than a dozen separate albums.18

Mastering here means processing every track so the stations share a level and color, like a real broadcast chain. By GTA V the problem had flipped. Masters made after 2008 were so heavily compressed that Conner had to back the compression off - de-mastering - so they would sit beside older records, and he cut frequencies across the board to fight ear fatigue.20 The hardest part was the DJ lines, recorded all over the place, one actor at home.20

Speed was part of the job. Conner said Vice City was delivered in about nine months.19

What changed when Dan Houser and Lazlow left?#

Dan Houser's exit was formal and dated. A Take-Two SEC filing, reported February 4, 2020, set his last day as March 11, 2020, after an extended break that began in spring 2019. Rockstar said Sam Houser's role was unchanged.6

Lazlow's is messier. VGC reported his departure on August 14, 2020, from his LinkedIn; Wikipedia has him leaving in April 2020 after nearly 20 years.7,8 Nothing public settles it.

Nobody has said what either departure did to Rockstar's music, but curation never sat with them. It sat with Pavlovich, still giving interviews as Director of Music in late 2020, and with Tony Mesones, whom the fan wiki lists as soundtrack director from January 2023.21,22 Sam Houser remains the documented creative voice on music.21 If something was lost, it was on the writing side: Dan and Lazlow co-wrote the radio stations from GTA III onward.8 That last part is inference, not reporting.

For GTA VI, the one named music credit is outside the building: Justice are the composers, and no Rockstar music staff have been named.32 More at GTA VI.

Quick answers

Who picks the music for GTA radio stations?
Rockstar's in-house music team, led since the GTA IV era by Director of Music Ivan Pavlovich, with Sam Houser as the documented final voice on taste. Outside artists such as Flying Lotus and DJ Pooh have curated or guided whole stations.
Why did the first GTA have radio stations?
DMA Design musician Craig Conner had written demos in many genres, and audio manager Colin Anderson proposed one station per genre instead of cutting most of them. GTA shipped in 1997 with seven stations of original music.
Did Sam Houser work in the music industry?
Yes. He started at BMG Records in London in 1990, worked on pop videos and VHS releases, and was head of development at BMG Interactive by 1996.
Did the Housers come out of 1990s London raves?
Nothing on record says so. Their documented club link is Rockstar Loft, a New York club night Sam Houser and Terry Donovan started in 1999 with Body & Soul's John Davis, which wound down in 2000.
How many songs did Rockstar consider for GTA V?
The team planned to license over 900 tracks and narrowed them to 241. Ivan Pavlovich gave Rolling Stone a launch figure of 240 licensed songs in 2013.
When did Lazlow leave Rockstar?
In 2020, though the month depends on who you ask. PC Gamer put it in April, a month after Dan Houser left; VGC reported it on August 14, 2020, from his LinkedIn.
