How can you tell GTA 6 music news from a leak?#

GTA VI news moves through four channels, and they are not equal. Every claim below carries one of these labels and a date.

Label What it means here Example
Official Announced by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or Atlantic Records The November 19, 2026 release date
Reported A figure or event from a named outlet, not confirmed by Rockstar Spotify stream spikes after trailers
Leak Unreleased information surfacing through footage, a glitch or an anonymous post The 14 station names from August 2026 footage
Estimate A third party's model of numbers the company has not disclosed Sensor Tower's pre-order count

A leak that later matches an official announcement is still a leak for everything it said that has not been announced. Fitting the known facts is not confirmation. Official wording here is as the footnoted press quoted it; The Radio Wheel has no connection to Rockstar, Take-Two or Atlantic.

Officially, Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.265,3 The road there:

When Rockstar announced the second delay, it said: "We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait."265 In February 2026, Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick described the company's year as led by the November 19 release.267 Take-Two's results for its fiscal second quarter, reported on November 9, 2026, repeated the date and kept full-year net bookings guidance at $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion.3,2 Net bookings is Take-Two's measure of sales booked in a period, digital included, and it is the main number the company guides on.

What song is in the GTA 6 trailer?#

Each trailer has used one song, and each time the song became news on its own.

Trailer 1 went up on December 4, 2023, a day ahead of Rockstar's plan, after it leaked online hours earlier.266 The song is Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," from his 1989 album Full Moon Fever, which Petty's official site noted two days later.268 It drew 93 million YouTube views in its first day and 255 million by May 2025.269

Trailer 2 arrived on Tuesday, May 6, 2025, set to The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together."269 Rockstar said it drew more than 475 million views across platforms in its first day and called it the biggest video launch of all time.269 "Hot Together" is the title track of The Pointer Sisters' 1986 album, so the trailer reached back almost 40 years.270

Between those and the final trailer came The Extended Look, a roughly 26-minute preview that premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026.271,272 DJ Mag reported songs including Jayda G and Aluna's "Mine O Mine," Depeche Mode's "People Are People" and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light."271 Red Bull counted 14 tracks in total, with Birdman featuring Lil Wayne, Def Leppard, Phil Collins and Cliff Richard among them.272 Rockstar has not said any of these are on the in-game radio. A trailer edit is a trailer edit.

The Launch Trailer - Rockstar's name for it; fans call it Trailer 3 - dropped on October 15, 2026, at 9am ET with no advance notice.273 Its song is Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane," featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, a track from the official album.151

How much did the trailer songs spike on Spotify?#

The press attributes these to Spotify, and they are percentages, not stream counts.

Song Trailer Reported rise Window Source chain
Tom Petty, "Love Is a Long Road" Trailer 1, December 2023 36,979% (often rounded to 37,000%) Versus the same period a week earlier Spotify via IGN, per GameSpot274
The Pointer Sisters, "Hot Together" Trailer 2, May 2025 182,000% Two hours after the trailer, versus the same window a week before Spotify data via The Hollywood Reporter, per Insider Gaming; also reported by TechRadar275,276

Neither outlet published absolute numbers, so a giant percentage can sit on top of a modest baseline. A decades-old album track going from background catalog to a few hundred times its usual plays is still a large effect, and it is the clearest public evidence of what a GTA placement can do for a song before the game is even out.

The Extended Look produced its own set of percentages. InMusic reported on September 2, 2026, rises in Spotify streams since the August 27 premiere, with no end date given: Captain & Tennille's "But I Think It's a Dream" up 130,730% and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light" up 1,070% in global streams, and Depeche Mode's "People Are People" up 190%.277 RouteNote, citing Spotify data reported by EDM.com, gave the same 1,070% for "Inner Light" in September 2026, plus a 4,155% rise in playlist additions.278 These carry the same caveat as the table: percentages, not stream counts.

What is GTA VI: The Album?#

GTA VI: The Album is a 34-track original album made by Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records, announced by both on September 17, 2026, and set for release on November 19, 2026, the same day as the game.162 Streaming pre-saves and vinyl and CD pre-orders opened the day it was announced.162 (A pre-save adds an unreleased album to your streaming library automatically on release day.) Rockstar framed it as the next step for its in-game radio, dynamic score and other musical touchpoints.162 Rockstar has not said which album tracks play on which stations.

Six singles came out on announcement day.162,279 The album's pre-save page listed them at positions 2, 4, 5, 8, 11 and 32 - page metadata, not a Rockstar announcement, and liable to change:280

  • Yung Lean, "That's It," featuring Future and Metro Boomin
  • Travis Scott, "RHYNO," produced by Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo of Daft Punk
  • CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. and Étienne de Crécy, "Sexy Magic"
  • Morgan Wallen, "Last Thing You Need"
  • Rauw Alejandro, "Macacoa 2000"
  • Keith Richards, "Bright Lights, Big City," a cover of the Jimmy Reed song264

A seventh official track arrived with the Launch Trailer on October 15: Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane."151 That makes seven of 34 tracks officially named as of November 15, 2026. The other 27 are known only through leaks, covered below.

Atlantic's president wrote on Instagram that the label had spent "two years of our lives" on the album with Rockstar, a post picked up by fan site GTA BOOM.281

Most coverage gives November 19 as the album's release date; fan site GTABase says November 20.282 Physical editions ship on the 20th, which probably explains it.283

Did the GTA 6 liquid vinyl sell out?#

Yes, but in September, not November. The album store, run by Atlantic, offered a limited liquid-filled vinyl - two transparent magenta discs with blue liquid sealed inside - at $124.98, £108.99 or €123.99, alongside standard vinyl at $49.98, a CD at $19.98, a splatter pressing and exclusives through retailers including Blood Records, Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Target, Urban Outfitters and independent stores.283,282 Those are store prices as fan sites reported them in September 2026.

The liquid edition sold out on the UK store within minutes and on the EU store that evening, per GamingBible; at 21:30 UTC on September 17, 2026, it was still listed on the US store.283 Resale listings on eBay soon ran roughly £313 to £386, unverified.283 For how colored and novelty pressings get made and priced, see the sister guide at groove.cold.fyi.

Is the GTA 6 album tracklist leak real?#

Unconfirmed. Two leaks matter here.

The Spotify glitch (October 2026, leak). The album's Spotify page briefly showed Cardi B at track 19 and Fuerza Regida at track 10, artist names only, before the entries were removed.284 Rockstar, Atlantic and Spotify have not commented.

The full list (November 14, 2026, leak). Brazilian outlet Omelete updated its album article with a 34-track list it says was leaked through a post on X; the leaker is not named and the list is unverified.285 Fan site GTABase carries the same list without calling it a leak.282 On that list, the album opens with Justice featuring Michael McDonald and includes, among others, Playboi Carti, Dua Lipa, Young Thug, Cardi B, JENNIE, Zara Larsson, Camila Cabello, Lana Del Rey, Burna Boy, Gunna, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, Tove Lo, Arca, Rina Sawayama, Kali Uchis and Omar Apollo.285

The list is internally consistent: in its running order the six singles sit at the same positions as on the pre-save page, the Launch Trailer song is sixth, and Fuerza Regida and Cardi B are 10th and 19th, matching the Spotify glitch.285,280 That is what a real list would look like. It is also what a careful fake built from public information would look like. Until Rockstar or Atlantic publish the tracklist, every name beyond the seven official tracks is a leak.

What radio stations are in GTA 6?#

On October 8, 2026, Rockstar named the first six stations and their hosts through the Newswire and previews on the official GTA VI music page.150,286 A radio host persona here means an artist voicing a station's DJ character, often under a stage name.

Station Format Hosts (official)
Cocoteo FM Modern Latin: bachata, merengue, dembow, reggaeton El Martillo (Bad Bunny) and La Gata (RaiNao)
Back Country Radio Renegade country MW (Morgan Wallen) and Delta Dawne (Lana Del Rey), with roving reporter Mud Dog Mike; Jeremy Dufrene also appears on air
AfroBank FM African classics, amapiano, 3-step Burna (Burna Boy) and Palmsy (Palms Trax)
The Chamber 106.6 Metal from the 1980s on DJ Kerry (Kerry King) and DJ Tom (Tom Araya) of Slayer
Flash FM Pop and dance Robyn and Alex (Alex Consani)
Dirty South Classics Regional rap Trick (Trick Daddy) and Trina

Announced October 8, 2026.150,286,287

Flash FM is a returning name from GTA Vice City, which fits a game set back in Vice City, GTA's fictional Miami, now inside the fictional state of Leonida.150,162,279 Rockstar also listed further categories without station names - Classic Rock, Modern Hip-Hop, Dance, '80s, Electronic, Blues, Ballads and Talk Radio150 - and described the set as the most diverse music in any GTA game.286 New for the series: on-demand podcasts for the two leads, Jason and Lucia, playable in a car or on foot through in-game headphones.150,287

Before October, the only official hint was indirect: a V-Rock T-shirt on Jason on the official site, which Red Bull noted in September 2026.272 A shirt is not a station announcement. No more stations had been named as of November 15, 2026.

What is the leaked GTA 6 station list?#

In August 2026, footage shared by fans showed 14 station names, compiled by Kotaku and reported by German outlet Netzwelt on August 27, 2026: Back Country Radio, The Chamber 106.6, CircoLoco Records Radio (see CircoLoco Records), Cocoteo FM, Dirty South Classics, Emotion 98.3, Kaleidoscope FM, La Ola, Radio ON-U, Stockyard FM, Symphony FM, The Wipe, Worldwide FM and V-Rock.288

Four of those were later made official on October 8: Back Country Radio, The Chamber 106.6, Cocoteo FM and Dirty South Classics.150 The other 10 remain leaks as of November 15, 2026. Two official stations, AfroBank FM and Flash FM, were not on the leaked list at all - a reminder that leaked footage shows a moment in development, not a finished game.

Who is composing the GTA 6 score?#

Justice. Rockstar named the French duo as composers of GTA VI's original score, music written for the game rather than licensed, on October 15, 2026, the same day as the Launch Trailer.151 Rockstar's album announcement had already described a dynamic score, its term for music that shifts with play.162 More on that in Scores.

Quick answers

When does GTA 6 come out?
Officially, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It was delayed from a 2025 window, then from May 26, 2026, and Take-Two reiterated the November date in its November 2026 results.
What song is in the GTA 6 trailer?
Trailer 1 (December 2023) uses Tom Petty's 'Love Is a Long Road' and Trailer 2 (May 2025) uses The Pointer Sisters' 'Hot Together'. The October 15, 2026 Launch Trailer uses Justice's 'Flower in a Hurricane' with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard.
Who is on the GTA 6 album?
Officially, as of November 15, 2026: Yung Lean with Future and Metro Boomin, Travis Scott, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso with PinkPantheress, Fred again.. and Etienne de Crecy, Morgan Wallen, Rauw Alejandro, Keith Richards, and Justice with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard. That is seven of 34 tracks; other names come from leaks.
Is the GTA 6 album tracklist leak real?
It is unconfirmed. A 34-track list shared on X on November 14, 2026, matches the seven official tracks and an October Spotify glitch, but the leaker is unnamed and neither Rockstar nor Atlantic has confirmed it.
What radio stations are in GTA 6?
Rockstar officially named six on October 8, 2026: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. Other names, such as CircoLoco Records Radio and V-Rock, come from leaked footage and are unconfirmed.
Does Lana Del Rey host a radio station in GTA 6?
Yes, officially. She hosts Back Country Radio as Delta Dawne alongside Morgan Wallen, announced October 8, 2026. A Lana Del Rey track on The Album appears only in the leaked tracklist.
