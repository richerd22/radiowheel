The short version: GTA IV (2008) gave Liberty City 19 stations and handed most of the microphones to real musicians, from Iggy Pop to Karl Lagerfeld, and its two episodes added three stations of their own. GTA V (2013) launched with 15 music stations and 240 licensed songs, grew by about 165 tracks on the 2014 consoles, and GTA Online kept bolting on whole stations through MOTOMAMI Los Santos in December 2021.28,63,64,65

How many radio stations does GTA IV have?#

GTA IV has 19 radio stations: 16 play music and three are talk, with "over 360 tracks" across them.63 The PC version adds a 20th, Independence FM, a custom station that plays the player's own audio files, hosted by Gary Sheen.66,67

The music side covers a lot of ground for one city. Liberty Rock Radio 97.8 handles classic rock, Vladivostok FM does Eastern European pop and dance, K109 The Studio plays disco, Electro-Choc runs electro house, Liberty City Hardcore does punk and metal, and The Journey is ambient, hosted by a text-to-speech "computer".66 Hip-hop splits across The Beat 102.7 (current) and The Classics 104.1 (older), and reggae across Tuff Gong Radio and Massive B Soundsystem 96.9.66

The three talk stations are WKTT Radio (conservative), Public Liberty Radio (liberal) and Integrity 2.0, Lazlow's street-interview show.66,68 The shows are covered at talk radio.

Rockstar's official album, The Music of Grand Theft Auto IV, came out April 29, 2008. It was one of five GTA IV-era soundtrack albums, along with a Vladivostok FM album and Statik Selektah's The Lost and Damned EP.63

The fan wiki's station pages list 219 songs on the base game's 16 music stations, with Vladivostok FM (24) and Liberty Rock Radio (22) the biggest.67 The 360 figure counts the episodes too; add their songs and the wiki's total comes to 375.

What did The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony change on the radio?#

The two episodes re-programmed the old dial more than they built new ones. The Lost and Damned (2009) brought new shows from Funkmaster Flex and Statik Selektah on The Beat 102.7 and Max Cavalera on Liberty City Hardcore, plus new tracks for Iggy Pop's and Juliette Lewis's stations, with the new songs playing before the old ones.70

The Ballad of Gay Tony updated Vladivostok FM (now hosted by DJ Paul), K109, Electro-Choc (Crookers), San Juan Sounds (Henry Santos Jeter) and Integrity 2.0.66,63 Lazlow comes back on Integrity 2.0 with a sidekick, Jorge.68

The Lost and Damned's new songs land on four stations (Liberty Rock 18, The Beat 15, Radio Broker 12, LCHC 11), and Gay Tony's on four more (K109 16, Vladivostok 15, Electro-Choc 13, San Juan Sounds 8).67

What stations are exclusive to Episodes from Liberty City?#

Episodes from Liberty City is the 2009 standalone bundle of both episodes, and it carries three stations of its own with 48 songs not in the original game:63,66

  • Vice City FM, 1980s pop, hosted by Fernando Martinez. It replaces The Vibe 98.8.
  • RamJam FM, reggae, dub and dancehall, hosted by David Rodigan. It replaces Tuff Gong and Massive B.
  • Self-Actualization FM, ambient and chillout, hosted by Audrey. It replaces The Journey, Jazz Nation and Fusion FM.

The fan wiki's song lists match the 48 exactly: 28 on Vice City FM, 12 on RamJam FM and eight on Self-Actualization FM.67

Why did songs disappear from GTA IV?#

Licenses ran out. From April 26, 2018, licensed songs were removed from GTA IV and both episodes because the terms had expired, many of them from Vladivostok FM.71 Vice put the 2018 cut at 50 songs and said the version returning to Steam in March 2020 would have "almost 100 missing tracks" and lack the three Episodes stations.72 The fan wiki marks 49 tracks removed in 2018 and has the Episodes stations back in the PC Complete Edition from June 3, 2020.67 The competing counts, and why game licenses expire, are at licensing.

What was the radio in GTA Chinatown Wars?#

Chinatown Wars (2009) ran on a Nintendo DS, and its radio was built differently. The March 11, 2009 announcement named five producers - Deadmau5, Truth & Soul, Ticklah, Prairie Cartel and Alchemist - and stations "including" Electro-Choc, IF99, Tuff Gong, The Beat and Radio Broker, all GTA IV names, with no total and no pairing of producer to station.73,74 Pure Nintendo called the music a "mixture of original and licensed instrumentals."74 The fan wiki goes further: each station is named after a real label or artist, with about 10 tracks apiece.75 The DS station count was never officially stated. The PSP version (2009) added six more: DJ Khalil, Tortoise, Turntables on the Hudson, DFA, Anvil and Sinowav FM, a station of traditional Chinese music. The later iOS and Android versions brought back Independence FM, playing an iTunes playlist named "GTA".75

Instrumentals carry much of it. The Alchemist station (tracks credited to Gangrene) is hip-hop instrumentals, all of DJ Khalil's tracks are instrumental versions of rap songs, Ticklah's station is dub, and Prairie Cartel's 10 cuts are the band's own.75

How many songs are on the GTA V radio?#

At launch on September 17, 2013, GTA V had 15 music stations, two talk stations and 240 licensed songs. Ivan Pavlovich, then Rockstar's soundtrack supervisor, called it "the largest soundtrack that we've done."28,76 Wikipedia's article contradicts itself, giving 15, 16 and 18 music stations in different sections and both "more than 441" and 241 tracks. Pavlovich's 240 is the launch number.77

The fan wiki's station pages land on the same figure: take out the 2014 additions, the PC-era additions and the two talk stations, and 240 songs remain.64 Non-Stop-Pop FM is now the biggest station at 42 songs, with Radio Mirror Park next at 40.64,78

The Music of Grand Theft Auto V came out digitally September 24, 2013, as 59 tracks over three volumes, with a CD and vinyl release on Mass Appeal Records on December 9, 2014. It peaked at 11 on Billboard's Top Soundtracks chart.77

What did the 2014 next-gen version add to GTA V radio?#

The 2014 release for newer consoles is the biggest expansion of the GTA V dial that did not add a station. The fan wiki marks 165 tracks as new in that edition, spread across 13 stations: Non-Stop-Pop FM got 26, Worldwide FM 19, Radio Mirror Park 18, Radio Los Santos 16, and Los Santos Rock Radio and FlyLo FM 15 each.64,78,80

Not all of it was new. Twenty-two of those songs were cut from the 2013 PS3 and Xbox 360 release and restored in the new edition; Non-Stop-Pop FM alone got seven back, among them Moloko's "The Time Is Now".64 A 2014 Game Informer list credits a few songs differently from the wiki.

Take-Two's September 12, 2014 announcement set the new edition for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One on November 18, 2014, with PC to follow on January 27, 2015, and promised "over 100 additional new songs and new DJ mixes" across 17 stations.81 Game Informer repeated the figure on October 17, 2014, and said FlyLo FM gained over 30 minutes of new music, including a Flying Lotus and Krayzie Bone collaboration and a new Doom track.82 The wiki's 165, restored cuts included, clears that floor comfortably.

What did the Cayo Perico Heist and The Contract do to the radio?#

Rockstar billed the Cayo Perico Heist (December 15, 2020) as "the biggest musical update to the game to date", with over 250 new tracks.79 Besides the three new stations, Worldwide FM and FlyLo FM got new music, MF DOOM on both.79 On Kult FM, producer Zachary, voiced by David Cross, fills in for Casablancas.89

The Contract (December 15, 2021) brought MOTOMAMI Los Santos, with Rosalía and Arca switching between Spanish and English.87 It also reworked Big Boy's Radio Los Santos with classic Dr. Dre cuts and exclusive new music, released through the CircoLoco Records label.90 A Dre Day takeover on West Coast Classics later merged into its regular playlist from I-Wagen Week.65

Has GTA Online added a radio station since 2021?#

Not a whole one, as of November 2026. MOTOMAMI Los Santos (December 2021) is the latest full station on record.65 Since then the changes have been smaller: a DāM-FunK playlist for the Media Player in The Chop Shop (15 tracks on the wiki's Media Player page, 16 on its Online radio page, which counts the mix title as a track), six songs added to Radio Los Santos in I-Wagen Week, and removals.88,65 On September 16, 2026, GTABoom reported that Moloko's "The Time Is Now" - one of the songs restored in 2014 - had been pulled from Non-Stop-Pop FM in GTA V Enhanced, with consoles expected to follow; Rockstar gave no reason.91 The fan wiki tallies 954 songs across the Online radio; that count is its own, unconfirmed elsewhere.65

Quick answers

How many radio stations are in GTA IV?
GTA IV has 19 radio stations, 16 music and three talk, with a 20th custom station, Independence FM, on PC. Episodes from Liberty City adds three exclusive stations with 48 songs.
Who are the DJs on GTA IV radio?
Hosts include Iggy Pop (Liberty Rock Radio), Karl Lagerfeld (K109), Juliette Lewis (Radio Broker), DJ Premier (The Classics 104.1), Daddy Yankee (San Juan Sounds) and Femi Kuti (IF99). The Lost and Damned added shows from Funkmaster Flex, Statik Selektah and Max Cavalera.
Who are the DJs on GTA 5 radio stations?
Launch hosts include Kenny Loggins (Los Santos Rock Radio), Cara Delevingne (Non-Stop-Pop FM), Pam Grier (The Lowdown 91.1), Bootsy Collins (Space 103.2), Flying Lotus (FlyLo FM), Soulwax (Soulwax FM) and Gilles Peterson (Worldwide FM). East Los FM is listed under Don Cheto and DJ Camilo at launch, while Rolling Stone names Camilo Lara of Mexican Institute of Sound.
How many songs are on the GTA 5 radio?
Rockstar's Ivan Pavlovich gave 240 licensed songs on 15 music stations at the 2013 launch. The 2014 next-gen edition added about 165 more, and GTA Online updates have added hundreds since, including over 250 in the 2020 Cayo Perico Heist.
Why were songs removed from GTA IV?
Music licenses expired. From April 26, 2018, licensed songs were patched out of GTA IV and its episodes, many from Vladivostok FM, and Vice reported almost 100 missing from the 2020 Steam Complete Edition.
What radio stations were added in GTA Online and when?
blonded Los Santos (2017), Los Santos Underground Radio (2018), iFruit Radio (2019), Kult FM, Still Slipping Los Santos and The Music Locker (2020), and the Media Player and MOTOMAMI Los Santos (2021). There has been no new station since December 2021.
