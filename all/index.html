Everything on one page

Steal a car in Grand Theft Auto and the first thing it does is play you a song. Hold the button and a wheel of stations fans out: punk, country, reggaeton, a call-in show about gardening, an ad for a soda you will never drink. For almost 30 years Rockstar Games has run that wheel like a small media company inside the game, with music directors, licensing deals, celebrity DJs, writers, original scores and, lately, its own record label and club residencies.

Here is how it works: who digs the records, how a station gets built, the talk radio fans still quote, the fake bands and fake ads, the club era of GTA Online, CircoLoco Records, and why songs quietly vanish from games people already own. GTA V alone had sold nearly 235 million units by November 2026, and it has outlived some of the music licenses on its own soundtrack.1,2 Grand Theft Auto VI arrived on November 19, 2026, with 23 stations on the dial.3

Further down is the part built for digging: a page for every station in every game, every song with a source behind it, the songs that were pulled and when, every talk show and host. Every fact links to where it came from. When two sources tell it differently, you get both. Leaks are labelled as leaks.

88.1 FM

Who makes the music at Rockstar

The short version: The habit started in a record company. Sam Houser began in BMG's London post room in 1990, BMG's games arm published the first Grand Theft Auto, and GTA's radio was pitched in Scotland by Colin Anderson of DMA Design's sound department so one musician's demos across many genres could all survive.4,5 Since then a small in-house team has picked, hosted, written and mastered the stations, with Sam Houser as the documented final word on taste. Dan Houser left in March 2020, and Lazlow later that year, on a date nobody quite agrees on.6,7,8

Did the Housers come from the music business?#

Yes, and the paper trail is dated. Sam Houser started at BMG Records in London in 1990 as a 19-year-old sorting mail, then spent 1991 to 1994 working part time on pop videos and VHS releases while at university.9,4 In 1994 he moved to BMG's interactive division, and by 1996 he was head of development at BMG Interactive, the games arm of Bertelsmann Music Group.9

BMG Interactive released Grand Theft Auto in late 1997. In March 1998 Bertelsmann had BMG sell the division to Take-Two.4 The price depends on who tells it. Sam Houser put the total consideration at $9 million; Strauss Zelnick, then BMG's worldwide CEO, called it "for a penny, essentially."4 Take-Two announced the deal on March 12, 1998, for about 1.85 million shares, roughly 16% of the company. Rockstar Games was set up in December 1998 and announced on January 22, 1999.10

The other founders had label or BMG backgrounds too. Dan Houser, Jamie King and Gary Foreman came from BMG Interactive, and Terry Donovan from BMG Entertainment's Arista Records.10 NME's Vice City retrospective draws the line: time at BMG and in music video gave the Housers label connections when the licensing era arrived.11

Dan Houser's start is thinner on paper. He took a part-time job testing CD-ROMs at BMG Interactive in 1995 and was full time until 1996, with no job title on record.12

Did Rockstar's founders come out of 1990s London raves?#

It is a popular claim with nothing behind it. No source ties Sam or Dan Houser to the UK rave or club scene of the early 1990s. What is documented is a New York club night, years later.

In 1999 Sam Houser and Terry Donovan, himself a former DJ, partnered with John Davis, promoter of the New York party Body & Soul, to launch Rockstar Loft.10 The first night ran on October 30, 1999, in Chelsea with Andi Hanley, Bob Sinclar and Dimitri from Paris. There were no game installations, only music, and it wound down in 2000.10 Fanbyte calls it short-lived and one of the company's biggest missteps.13

The clearest thread from that club night to the games runs through Andi Hanley. He spent most of 15 years as a warm-up DJ at Body & Soul and held down musical supervision at Rockstar, years unstated.14 By 2008 he was co-supervising the GTA IV soundtrack.15 For more on how New York's long-running parties worked, see https://lastcall.cold.fyi/.

Why did the first GTA have radio stations?#

Because a musician wrote too many demos. At DMA Design in Dundee, Craig Conner had written tracks across many genres. Rather than cut two-thirds of them to fit one style, Anderson proposed one station per genre, played on car radios.5 Audio was low priority at DMA, memory budgets were tight, and not everyone liked the idea.5

The soundtrack was first planned as MIDI, partly because an N64 version was in the works. When that version was cancelled, the team switched to CD-quality audio so the stations would sound like real radio.5 GTA shipped in 1997 with seven stations of original music by DMA's own musicians. DJs and ads were dreamed up early but had to wait for GTA 2, for memory and budget reasons.5 The station-by-station story is at early radio.

The music was in-house because licensing was out of reach. Conner wrote beats and rough lyrics, and rapper Jonathan Wilson, credited as "Robert De Negro," refined and performed them under fictional act names such as Da Shootaz and Slumpussy.16 More on that at the scores.

Talk radio was a budget fix too: for GTA III (2001), Dan Houser and Lazlow Jones pitched it as cheap filler.17 The full story is at talk radio.

How does Rockstar pick the songs for GTA radio?#

Conner's account of the GTA 1 to GTA V era: set each station's style, lock in a few certain tracks, then keep adding and dropping until the day before final submission.19 He researched each genre with help from people inside it, who often ended up as the station's DJ. Sam Houser named each station. Then the DJ links and idents were recorded, and Conner mastered everything.19 An ident is the short branding jingle or voice tag between songs.

Each game set its own brief. For GTA IV, Pavlovich and Hanley sourced music under Sam Houser's call for a contemporary Liberty City sound. Contemporary hip hop, dance rock and Russian music got much of the airtime early, alongside classic New York music from the likes of CBGB's and Studio 54, plus hip hop, jazz and afrobeat.15

GTA V shows the scale. The team planned to license over 900 tracks and narrowed them to 241, a 2013 Polygon figure; Pavlovich told Rolling Stone 240 (the full dial is at modern radio).30,28 The station concepts were regional, from Los Angeles and California to a Mexican station curated by Camilo Lara and West Coast Classics hosted by DJ Pooh. Flying Lotus was approached first, after a meeting at South by Southwest. Pavlovich called it "the most exciting soundtrack we've done," for the hosts as much as the music.28

"We've always seen our job as curators of music for the experience of GTA," Pavlovich told Rolling Stone.31 The team goes to clubs and watches sets, Boiler Room included.31 He told Fact the picks come from fandom rather than chasing what is cool, and that several staff grew up in London while he grew up in Chicago.26 The music team comes in early and sits down with Sam Houser; if a game plans a nightclub, they choose DJs who fit it.21

Nobody has described Rockstar staff doing literal crate digging - record shops, vinyl runs. What is on record is club-going, genre advisers who become hosts, artist-curated stations and unreleased material from artists' own circles.

How do they make 11 stations sound like one radio dial?#

By mastering them together. On San Andreas (2004), Conner mastered 11 stations, 155 tracks and 70 commercials plus DJ dialogue.18 Tracks play in random order, so tracks, ads and DJ segments all had to sit at one consistent level. The aim was one collection rather than a dozen separate albums.18

Mastering here means processing every track so the stations share a level and color, like a real broadcast chain. By GTA V the problem had flipped. Masters made after 2008 were so heavily compressed that Conner had to back the compression off - de-mastering - so they would sit beside older records, and he cut frequencies across the board to fight ear fatigue.20 The hardest part was the DJ lines, recorded all over the place, one actor at home.20

Speed was part of the job. Conner said Vice City was delivered in about nine months.19

What changed when Dan Houser and Lazlow left?#

Dan Houser's exit was formal and dated. A Take-Two SEC filing, reported February 4, 2020, set his last day as March 11, 2020, after an extended break that began in spring 2019. Rockstar said Sam Houser's role was unchanged.6

Lazlow's is messier. VGC reported his departure on August 14, 2020, from his LinkedIn; Wikipedia has him leaving in April 2020 after nearly 20 years.7,8 Nothing public settles it.

Nobody has said what either departure did to Rockstar's music, but curation never sat with them. It sat with Pavlovich, still giving interviews as Director of Music in late 2020, and with Tony Mesones, whom the fan wiki lists as soundtrack director from January 2023.21,22 Sam Houser remains the documented creative voice on music.21 If something was lost, it was on the writing side: Dan and Lazlow co-wrote the radio stations from GTA III onward.8 That last part is inference, not reporting.

For GTA VI, the one named music credit is outside the building: Justice are the composers, and no Rockstar music staff have been named.32 More at GTA VI.

The short version: In the first two games almost everything on air was written in-house under fake band names. GTA III (2001) mixed those acts with licensed music and talk radio, Vice City (2002) went all in on licensed 1980s tracks, and San Andreas (2004) grew to 10 music stations with celebrity DJs. Was the music in the first GTA real or made for the game?#

Made for the game. The radio was Colin Anderson's way to keep Craig Conner's demos in many genres, a story told in full at origins.5 The game shipped with seven stations covering hip hop, funk, pop, rock, country, house and drum 'n' bass, and Anderson treated avoiding licensed music as a core principle.5

The seven were Brooklyn Underground FM (trance), The Fix FM (techno), The Fergus Buckner Show FM (country), Head Radio FM (pop and rock), It's Unleashed FM (alternative and hard rock), N-CT FM (hip hop) and Radio '76 FM, which is announced in-game as Funk FM.33 The bands were invented: Retrograde, Rotorman, Bleeding Stump, Sideways Hank O'Malley, Da Shootaz, with songs credited to Conner, Anderson, Grant Middleton, Brian Baglow and Robert DeNegro.33 The main theme, "Gangster Friday" by the fictional Slumpussy, plays on no station. On PC the station audio is plain CD audio, so the disc plays in a stereo and you could swap in your own CD.33

Did GTA 2 have licensed music?#

A little. GTA 2 used only a few licensed songs. Its real change was the talk between them: DJs and ads finally made it in, and they set the satirical tone the series still runs on.5 Who wrote those ads is covered at lore.

GTA 2 came out on PlayStation and Windows on October 22, 1999, followed by Dreamcast in May 2000 and Game Boy Color in December 2000.34

There are 11 stations, and each part of the city hears five of them. Head Radio covers the whole city, and each gang gets its own station: Funami FM for the Yakuza, Heavenly Radio for the Krishnas, KGBH for the Russian mafia, Osmosis for the Scientists.35 Gang stations made the radio part of the map: what you heard told you whose turf you were on. Five tracks were licensed - Bula Matari "Taxi Drivers", Flytronix "Pendulum" and "Past Archives", Davidson "All I Wanna Do", Track 7 "I Wanna Phunk" - and the rest came from in-house writers including Conner, Anderson and Stuart Ross.36 The fan wikis can't agree on several names. Lo-Fi FM's DJ is "DJ Die/Dye" on one and "DJ Dai" on the other, KGBH's host is "Bombatumba" or "Bomba Tomba and Prodo", and Osmosis is Radio or FM depending on the page.35,36

The menu theme is E-Z Rollers' "Short Change", and there is a "GTA 2: The Movie Soundtrack" of eight 1998 tracks from the Moving Shadow label, mostly drum and bass.36

What was on the radio in GTA London 1969?#

London 1969 and its follow-up London 1961 were made by Rockstar Toronto without Anderson, and they brought in licensed acts such as The Upsetters and Harry J All Stars.5 Anderson's verdict: they "broke the purity of the creative vision."5

London 1969 came out on April 30, 1999, for PC and PlayStation, and London 1961 followed on July 1, 1999, as a free PC-only download.37

The two share 13 stations with identical playlists.38 Most of the period sound is not British pop but late-1960s and early-1970s Italian film scores by Riz Ortolani, Piero Umiliani and Francesco De Masi, next to reggae and ska from The Upsetters ("Return of Django"), Harry J Allstars ("The Liquidator") and Symarip.38 Four stations carry original Rockstar-made tracks with names like "GTA Pomp" and "Austin Allegro Drag Race", and no DJs are on record.38 It reads like the first GTA radio built on crate digging rather than a staff songwriter.

What were the radio stations in GTA III?#

Nine, and GTA III is where the modern formula clicked: in-house fictional pop, licensed specialist stations, and a talk station. They are Head Radio, Double Clef FM, K-Jah, Rise FM, Lips 106, Game FM, MSX FM, Flashback 95.6 and Chatterbox FM.39

Head Radio and Lips 106 play fictional artists made for the game.39 Head Radio's Dil-Don't and Scatwerk, and Lips 106's Fatamarse and Boyz 2 Girlz, are credited to Conner and Stuart Ross; Lips 106 also replays Da Shootaz "Grand Theft Auto" from the 1997 game.40 The licensed side is sharp and specific. K-Jah plays five cuts from Scientist's 1981 vampire-themed dub album, Flashback 95.6 plays Giorgio Moroder's Scarface material, MSX FM is drum and bass mixed by DJ Timecode, and Game FM (Game Radio on one wiki) is mixed by Stretch Armstrong with Royce Da 5'9" and Black Rob.39,40 Double Clef FM plays opera arias by Mozart, Verdi and Puccini.40

On the mic: Michael Hunt (Russ Mottla) on Head Radio, Andee (Shelley Miller) on Lips 106, Toni (Maria Chambers) on Flashback, with Stretch Armstrong and Lord Sear, and MC Codebreaker, playing themselves.39 Chatterbox FM is call-in talk radio hosted by Lazlow, with 33 credited guest voices including Dan Houser, Kyle MacLachlan and Michael Madsen.39

Is the Vice City soundtrack on CD?#

Yes. Rockstar partnered with Sony Music Entertainment and Epic Records on seven CDs, one per music station, each about an hour long with the DJ chatter and fake ads kept in.44 Station imaging - the IDs, links and jokes between songs - was part of the product. The volumes ran V-Rock, Wave 103, Emotion 98.3, Flash FM, Wildstyle, Fever 105, Espantoso. Discs were $13.98 each or $49.98 for the set in 2002, and each one doubled as a key disc on the vicecityradio.com website.44 GamesFirst's December 2002 review put the MSRP at $49.99, with most online stores at $39.99, and pointed out that the box sold to everyone while the game was PS2-only.45

Terry Donovan, then COO, said in the release: "Every track was lovingly and laboriously chosen."11 The set came out internationally on October 29, 2002. European editions carry extra songs, K-Chat and VCPR were never released, and a Greatest Hits disc followed on April 30, 2003.46

Who voiced the DJs in San Andreas?#

San Andreas is set in 1992 and carried 10 music stations plus the talk station WCTR, which GameSpot called the series' biggest yet.47 In came funk, rare groove, alternative, country (K-Rose) and a second hip-hop station; out went Latin, classical, public radio, drum 'n' bass and the 1980s.47 Rare groove - crate-dug funk and soul - got its own station, Master Sounds 98.3.47

The DJ booth was a casting coup: Axl Rose as K-DST's Tommy "The Nightmare" Smith, George Clinton as Bounce FM's The Funktipus, Chuck D as Playback FM's Forth Right MC, Michael Bivins as CSR's Philip "PM" Michaels, Sly Dunbar and Robbie Shakespeare on K-Jah West, and Julio G as himself on Radio Los Santos.47 The defining picks include Lynyrd Skynyrd "Free Bird" on K-DST, Public Enemy "Rebel Without a Pause" on Playback, Soundgarden "Rusty Cage" on Radio X and Mr. Fingers "Can You Feel It?" on SF-UR.48 Even CSR's frequency has two answers: GameSpot lists 103.2, Grand Theft Wiki 103.9.47,49

WCTR's shows include WCTR News with Richard Burns (Wil Wheaton), Gardening with Maurice (Andy Dick), the conspiracy show Area 53, and Entertaining America, hosted by Billy Dexter and Lazlow.49 Wheaton wrote that the session was "some of the most fun I've ever had."50

Two. What stations are in Liberty City Stories?#

Liberty City Stories (2005) returned to GTA III's city with 10 stations, nine music plus the talk station LCFR, and the full list went public on October 25, 2005.53 Several names came back with new playlists. Head Radio and Lips 106 again play in-house tracks, with writer credits including Craig Conner and Will Morton. Flashback FM is all Giorgio Moroder, Rise FM moves to 1990s house (Moloko, Ultra Naté, Josh Wink), and The Liberty Jam is mid-1990s East Coast rap from Mobb Deep, Method Man and Raekwon, hosted by DJ Clue as himself.54,53 The new one was Radio Del Mundo, a world music station with tracks like Ofra Haza "Im Nin'Alu" and Asha Bhosle "Dum Maro Dum".55 The nine music stations run to 84 songs.55

Michael Hunt (Russ Mottla) and Andee (Shelley Miller) return, MC Codebreaker and DJ Timecode play themselves on MSX 98, and LCFR runs five shows, with Chatterbox hosted by Lazlow.53 Spellings wander (Panja or Panjit, Natalee or Natalie), and Vandal's credit of "Ed McMann" for Lips 106's Cliff turns up nowhere else.53,54

The radio, game by game: GTA IV and GTA V

The short version: GTA IV (2008) gave Liberty City 19 stations and handed most of the microphones to real musicians, from Iggy Pop to Karl Lagerfeld, and its two episodes added three stations of their own. GTA V (2013) launched with 15 music stations and 240 licensed songs, grew by about 165 tracks on the 2014 consoles, and GTA Online kept bolting on whole stations through MOTOMAMI Los Santos in December 2021.28,63,64,65

How many radio stations does GTA IV have?#

GTA IV has 19 radio stations: 16 play music and three are talk, with "over 360 tracks" across them.63 The PC version adds a 20th, Independence FM, a custom station that plays the player's own audio files, hosted by Gary Sheen.66,67

The music side covers a lot of ground for one city. Liberty Rock Radio 97.8 handles classic rock, Vladivostok FM does Eastern European pop and dance, K109 The Studio plays disco, Electro-Choc runs electro house, Liberty City Hardcore does punk and metal, and The Journey is ambient, hosted by a text-to-speech "computer".66 Hip-hop splits across The Beat 102.7 (current) and The Classics 104.1 (older), and reggae across Tuff Gong Radio and Massive B Soundsystem 96.9.66

The three talk stations are WKTT Radio (conservative), Public Liberty Radio (liberal) and Integrity 2.0, Lazlow's street-interview show.66,68 The shows are covered at talk radio.

Rockstar's official album, The Music of Grand Theft Auto IV, came out April 29, 2008. It was one of five GTA IV-era soundtrack albums, along with a Vladivostok FM album and Statik Selektah's The Lost and Damned EP.63

The fan wiki's station pages list 219 songs on the base game's 16 music stations, with Vladivostok FM (24) and Liberty Rock Radio (22) the biggest.67 The 360 figure counts the episodes too; add their songs and the wiki's total comes to 375.

The two episodes re-programmed the old dial more than they built new ones. Why did songs disappear from GTA IV?#

Licenses ran out. From April 26, 2018, licensed songs were removed from GTA IV and both episodes because the terms had expired, many of them from Vladivostok FM.71 Vice put the 2018 cut at 50 songs and said the version returning to Steam in March 2020 would have "almost 100 missing tracks" and lack the three Episodes stations.72 The fan wiki marks 49 tracks removed in 2018 and has the Episodes stations back in the PC Complete Edition from June 3, 2020.67 The competing counts, and why game licenses expire, are at licensing.

What was the radio in GTA Chinatown Wars?#

Chinatown Wars (2009) ran on a Nintendo DS, and its radio was built differently. The March 11, 2009 announcement named five producers - Deadmau5, Truth & Soul, Ticklah, Prairie Cartel and Alchemist - and stations "including" Electro-Choc, IF99, Tuff Gong, The Beat and Radio Broker, all GTA IV names, with no total and no pairing of producer to station.73,74 Pure Nintendo called the music a "mixture of original and licensed instrumentals."74 The fan wiki goes further: each station is named after a real label or artist, with about 10 tracks apiece.75 The DS station count was never officially stated. The PSP version (2009) added six more: DJ Khalil, Tortoise, Turntables on the Hudson, DFA, Anvil and Sinowav FM, a station of traditional Chinese music. Instrumentals carry much of it. The Alchemist station (tracks credited to Gangrene) is hip-hop instrumentals, all of DJ Khalil's tracks are instrumental versions of rap songs, Ticklah's station is dub, and Prairie Cartel's 10 cuts are the band's own.75

Talk radio: Chatterbox to Chakra Attack

The short version: Talk radio exists in Grand Theft Auto because, by Dan Houser's own account, it was the cheapest way to fill hours of driving in GTA III (2001). Written mostly by Houser and Lazlow Jones, it became the series' sharpest satire: one call-in station in Liberty City, two in Vice City, a whole network of shows on San Andreas's WCTR, three political stations in GTA IV and a left/right split by county in GTA V. Lazlow, who played a steadily sadder version of himself, left Rockstar in 2020 after nine GTA games, in a month that depends on who you read.17,8,92,93

Why does GTA have talk radio at all?#

Houser has said the GTA III radio was a two-year project, and that Rockstar's Scotland team under Leslie Benzies spent the first year just getting the system to work.17 With hours of driving to fill and no budget, Houser and Lazlow pitched talk radio as the cheap fix.17 Houser later gave IGN the brief in one line: "how do we fill this with absolutely no money?"17 Someone then suggested letting the game's own characters call in, which Houser said made the whole thing feel magical.17

The budget hack stuck. Music stations sell a mood; talk stations build a society. Each game, Lazlow told Kinda Funny Games, sets out to satirize a specific place and time, and the callers, sponsors and panel guests tell you what kind of city you are stealing cars in.94

It pays off in plot too: WCTR's news reports the player's missions, and the station's content changes as the story advances, so some segments only play once.95,96

What was Chatterbox FM in GTA III?#

Chatterbox FM is GTA III's talk station and the template for everything after it: Lazlow, playing himself, taking calls from a city of cranks.97 He plays the only sane man in the building, answering each caller with caustic sarcasm and a stock line about the call being a lesson to us all.97 The station sits at 109, which is outside the real FM band of 87.5 to 108 MHz, so no real receiver could tune it (the real version of that dial lives at https://airwaves.cold.fyi/).97

The callers are Liberty City's oddest residents: the Survivalist caller, the SPANK caller, Freddy, the C.R.A.P. caller, Bob from Pike Creek, Barry Stark and Toni Cipriani.97 Two longer interviews break up the calls: one with Reed Tucker, and one with Fernando Martinez, who uses the airtime to plug his marriage-rescue business, Fernando's New Beginnings.97 The station belongs to Donald Love's Love Media, which runs self-congratulating public service announcements between segments.97

The targets are talk radio and its callers, consumerism, self-help, pharmaceutical ads, reality TV, conspiracy theories and anti-technology groups.97 A caller also complains that a show called Gardening with Maurice has been taken off the air - a throwaway that pays off three years later in San Andreas.95

What are WKTT, PLR and Integrity 2.0 in GTA IV?#

GTA IV (2008) is where the talk radio turned openly political, with one station for each side of the aisle and one for Lazlow's ego.101,102,103

WKTT ("We Know The Truth") is right-wing talk.101 Its anchor is The Richard Bastion Show, with Jason Sudeikis as a Rush Limbaugh-style host.101 Some of his callers were real people. On July 13, 2007, Rockstar opened the teaser site WKTTRadio.com with a New York phone number where fans could leave a message about what was wrong with Liberty City, America, liberals or their health.114,101 Some of those calls ended up on Bastion's show.101 The rest of the schedule: Just or Unjust, where Judge Grady replaces verdicts with audience-picked contests, and Fizz!, an Entertainment Tonight parody.101 The episodes added two more: The Martin Serious Show in The Lost and Damned, a Howard Stern parody with Smithy the Sidekick, Lisa Lynn and Mark the Manager, and Conspire in The Ballad of Gay Tony, John Smith's Alex Jones-style conspiracy hour.101

PLR (Public Liberty Radio) is the public-radio answer:102

  • The Séance - psychic Beatrix Fontaine (Ilyana Kadushin), who keeps steering callers toward paying her.
  • Pacemaker - health show hosted by Ryan McFallon (Bryan Tucker), with pharma rep Sheila Stafford (Rachel Allen), HMO chief Wilson Taylor Sr. (Bill Hader) and Mason Waylon (Rick Shapiro).
  • Intelligent Agenda - Mike Riley's (Brian Sack) left-wing call-in, with actor Brandon Roberts, candidate John Hunter and child prodigy Zachary Tyler.

Integrity 2.0 is a station of its own, not part of PLR, and the best single joke about Lazlow in the series.103 He walks around Algonquin interviewing strangers: one runs away, one calls him an asshole, and he ends up assaulting a hot dog vendor.103 The show is pre-recorded and loops because, in the fiction, he cannot afford live shows.103 ZiT sponsors it in GTA IV; in the episodes the sponsor becomes his stepfather's funeral home, he gains a sidekick named Jorge, and the pair visit the gay club Hercules.103

Who is Lazlow, and when did he leave Rockstar?#

Lazlow Jones is the one constant. He met one of Rockstar's founders while surfing, and in 2001 Lazlow's production company was hired for GTA III.123 He has co-written the stations with Houser since then, and by 2024 called them writing, directing and producing partners of 23 years.8,123 Off mic he wrote and directed dialogue and voice actors, and worked on trailers, streams and the in-game animated shows.124

He has appeared in every main-line GTA, and not only on talk stations: he was head DJ on Vice City's V-Rock and hosted GTA V's Fame or Shame TV show.123,125 GTA V was also the first time he appeared on screen as a character, in the missions "Fame or Shame", "Reuniting the Family" and "Meltdown".125

The decline was deliberate. Lazlow told IGN at San Diego Comic-Con that Houser made the in-game Lazlow more pathetic with every game, because the joke depends on the gap between the man and the character.126 When he asked about his GTA V material, Houser told him, in GTA BOOM's paraphrase, that he would get to dance in his underwear.126

How was GTA talk radio written and recorded?#

The early method was a two-person, low-cost operation. Lazlow and Houser's first writing session was pizza and Diet Coke at Houser's apartment.123 Houser set the big picture and the main characters; in the room, he threw out ideas and Lazlow typed, graduating from a legal pad to a laptop because he could not keep up.123

Chatterbox FM was recorded in Lazlow's home studio, the studio he used for his real show, Technofile.123 He recorded the voices and the fake GTA III ads there and sent the files to Houser by FTP; Houser listened to them live over a phone line.123 Lazlow produced GTA III's radio and commercials, and his company Radio Lazlow co-wrote and produced the Vice City stations.97,125

The cast was whoever Lazlow knew. Most of the Chatterbox voices were friends, radio contacts and neighbors; neighborhood kids did voices too, and his own father is the caller who loves eating squirrels.123 Callers read their lines to him over the phone, and the test for a bit was simple. "I knew we were golden if he would break up laughing," Lazlow told ShortList in 2024.123

By GTA IV, fans were supplying material through the WKTTRadio.com line, and Rockstar's audio team said in 2008 that Weazel News bulletins would update through the story.114,132

For GTA V, former Rockstar audio staff Craig Conner and Will Morton, two of the three senior members of Rockstar North's audio team in 2008, gave AudioTechnology a rough picture of the process.133,20 The in-car radio's talkback shows, spoof DJs and phone-in guests sat alongside about 150,000 lines of in-game dialogue voiced by about 700 actors, recorded, Morton said, in three studios running at once, each with a Rockstar director.20 Conner called the DJ dialogue the biggest radio job, recorded in so many locations that one actor worked from home.20

What did critics say about GTA talk radio?#

The critics split along the games' own line, local weirdness versus national politics.

  • PC Gamer, 2020. Samuel Roberts named Chatterbox his favorite GTA talk station, weird residents calling Lazlow about nonsense, and found its target funnier and more specific than the later games' politicians and technology companies. Joe Donnelly named Chakra Attack the GTA V standout, an exception among skits that can feel forced.134,118
  • PopMatters, 2013. G. Christopher Williams called the ads and talk shows central to the series' satire, found the targets inconsistent, and suggested the wall of media noise may be the message. He also noted that the broadcasts often contain misogynistic, homophobic and anti-religious statements.135
  • The Guardian (undated). Aleks Krotoski called Lazlow's Chatterbox "Howard Stern-esque anarchy" and said a Chris Evans Virgin Radio stunt was "kid's play" by comparison.136

Where did GTA's fake radio ads come from?#

The idea is older than the jokes. Radio went into the first GTA (1997) to save Craig Conner's genre-hopping demos, but memory and budget kept DJs and ads out until GTA 2, where Michael Keillor, a film and TV director working at DMA Design, wrote spots for "3rd World Bank" and a "Zoom Zoom" nasal spray.5 PC Gamer credits those ads and DJs with setting the satirical tone the series kept.5 The early games are covered at /early-radio/.

GTA III is where the format matured. Its commercials were written by Houser and Lazlow and produced by Lazlow.39 Lazlow cut them in his home studio and sent them to Houser for review, and his test was blunt: "I knew we were golden if he would break up laughing."123

Each game, Lazlow told Kinda Funny Games, sets out to satirize one place at one moment in time.94 GamesRadar's 2010 roundup of favorite ads boiled the approach down to parodying the most twisted parts of real advertising.141

Is Love Fist a real band?#

No. Love Fist is a fictional rock band written for Vice City (2002), heard on V-Rock with "Dangerous Bastard" and "Fist Fury", both written for the game.104 Radio Espantoso, Vice City's Latin station, also mixes in made-up acts: Alpha Banditos, Tres Apenas como eso and Unaesta.104

A fictional artist is the oldest trick in GTA radio. Even the first game's main theme, "Gangster Friday", is credited to Craig Conner as the fictional band Slumpussy.33 GTA III stocked Head Radio and Lips 106 with invented acts such as Dil-Don't, Scatwerk, Fatamarse and Boyz 2 Girlz, with Conner and Stuart Ross writing the original tracks.39 Liberty City Stories (2005) brought both stations back with writer credits including Conner and Will Morton.54

Because no label owns it, in-house music is the one part of the radio no license can expire on. The rest is covered at /licensing/.

Who is Lazlow, and why does his character keep getting worse?#

Lazlow Jones is a real radio host who co-wrote and produced all the radio in Vice City and San Andreas and also plays a version of himself on air, from Chatterbox FM in GTA III to Chattersphere in GTA V.125 The joke is that the character fails upward and then just fails. Speaking to IGN at San Diego Comic-Con, Lazlow said Houser deliberately made the in-game Lazlow worse as a person and less like the real Lazlow with every game; for GTA V, Houser's answer to his script question included dancing in his underwear.126

He left Rockstar in 2020 after nine GTA games and joined Houser at Absurd Ventures.93 Asked in 2025 whether he would turn up in GTA VI, he said he doubted it.127 His full on-air run, his exit and the talk shows he wrote are covered at /talk-radio/.

How does the radio wheel work, and how did station switching change?#

Station switching has been a design choice since the first game. On the PC version of GTA (1997) the stations are plain CD audio, so you can swap in your own CD.33 GTA 2 tied radio to territory: each area hears five of its 11 stations, including gang stations such as Funami FM for the Yakuza.35

Before GTA V, the control was a linear selector: a looping next and previous button that could only step to the neighboring station. The radio wheel, a radial picker, arrived with GTA V and GTA Online: hold D-Pad Left (Q on PC) and hover on a station for about a second to tune it.151 The 2021 Definitive Editions of GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas got a wheel too. Fans claim GTA VI goes back to a linear selector; Rockstar hasn't said so.151

In GTA Online the dial also gains the Media Player, a customizable station that only plays music from Media Sticks found around Los Santos.88 For how real stations get a signal into a car, the sister guide https://airwaves.cold.fyi/ covers the transmitters.

The radio rewards close listening and long memories.

  • The host who never shows. On GTA Online's Kult FM, Julian Casablancas never reaches the booth, so producer Zachary (David Cross) fills in and calls back to Zero, the San Andreas RC shop owner. On Still Slipping, artists' answering-machine messages cut in between tracks.89
  • A robot DJ. GTA IV's ambient station, The Journey, is "hosted" by a computer voice made with Mac text-to-speech.66
  • A broken guest spot. Skepta's playlist on iFruit Radio has not played since the Los Santos Drug Wars update, leaving only Danny Brown's half.65
  • Cheat codes in a CD box. Each disc in the 2002 Vice City box set unlocked a cheat on vicecityradio.com.44
  • The station that never aired (rumor). Fans say Kavinsky was reported in early 2012 as host of a planned GTA V station called "Nightride FM". It never appeared and the original report hasn't turned up, so file it under rumor.115

The scores: music written for the games

The short version: For its first decade, Grand Theft Auto's original music was mostly in-house songs credited to fictional bands, played on the radio rather than under the action. GTA V (2013) brought a full original score, which Rockstar called its first fully collaborative score, built by Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, The Alchemist and Oh No as layered stems that react to play.28 Red Dead Redemption, Red Dead Redemption 2, Max Payne 3 and GTA Online all got scores of their own, and Justice scored GTA VI, with Overmono and 17 more acts credited beside them.154

Did early GTA have original music?#

Yes, but it went on the radio. GTA 1 (1997) shipped with seven stations of entirely original music by DMA Design's own musicians.5 Craig Conner wrote the songs with rapper Jonathan Wilson, credited as "Robert De Negro", under invented acts such as Da Shootaz, Slumpussy and CCC.16 The main theme, "Gangster Friday", plays on no station at all.33

The habit lasted. Only five of GTA 2's 30 songs were licensed; the rest were written in-house.36 GTA London kept four stations of Rockstar-made tracks.38 GTA III and Liberty City Stories filled Head Radio and Lips 106 with fictional artists made for the game.39,54 Conner also wrote original music for Manhunt (2003),155 mostly on a Korg Mono/Poly synth.18 The in-house stations are covered at /early-radio/.

Who composed the GTA V score?#

Four acts: Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, The Alchemist and Oh No.28 Rockstar soundtrack supervisor Ivan Pavlovich called it the first fully collaborative score Rockstar had made, and described the 2013 package as "the largest soundtrack that we've done, and the largest score that we've done."28

It was a relay more than a band. Tangerine Dream's Edgar Froese went first: his first eight months on the job produced 62 hours of music, recorded with the band in Austria.77 Jackson, Rockstar's go-to composer since Red Dead Redemption,27 first scored Trevor's missions, then The Alchemist and Oh No sampled and layered the material.77 DJ Shadow mixed the result and matched it to gameplay.77

The team made more than 20 hours of music in all.77 Rolling Stone put it at roughly 20 movies' worth of score.28

Two of the composers then got their own radio station. The Lab, built on their album Welcome to Los Santos, carries the record, which came out April 14, 2015 with the PC version of the game and at retail on April 21.77 PC Gamer dates the station to July 2015; other accounts say April, and /modern-radio/ has both.83

Froese also put out his own version. The Cinematographic Score - GTA 5 came out March 24, 2014 on Eastgate: 12 tracks, limited to 2,000 copies.77 Pressing runs that small are a crate-digger's problem; https://groove.cold.fyi/ covers how vinyl editions get made and priced.

How does a dynamic score work in GTA V?#

A dynamic score reacts to what the player is doing instead of playing a fixed track.28 GTA V's runs on Rockstar's interactive stem system.28 A stem is one layer of a cue - drums, bass, a synth line - that the game can bring in or drop out. Each GTA V mission used eight stems, with Pavlovich doing the mix-downs.77

That lets the music build or thin out with the action without restarting. The cost is volume: every layer has to work in combination with the others, which helps explain hour counts that read like box sets. Woody Jackson told Mix that the stem count grew from eight to 15 by Red Dead Redemption 2.27

Who made the Red Dead Redemption soundtrack?#

Bill Elm, a member of the band Friends of Dean Martinez, and Woody Jackson, a former member, scored Red Dead Redemption (2010).156 They made over 14 hours of music across 15 months, all recorded at 130 BPM in A minor, with Ennio Morricone's Dollars Trilogy as the touchstone.156 Tommy Morgan played harmonica.156

The game also placed four vocal songs: José González's "Far Away", Jamie Lidell's "Compass", Ashtar Command's "Deadman's Gun" and William Elliott Whitmore's "Bury Me Not on the Lone Prairie".156 "Far Away" won Best Song in a Game at the Spike Video Game Awards.156 The album came out digitally May 18, 2010 and physically August 23, 2010, with David Holmes selecting its instrumental tracks.156

IBTimes UK, drawing on a 2015 GamesTM interview, says Craig Conner produced Red Dead Redemption soundtracks too.19 That may be a different job from Elm and Jackson's; nobody has spelled out the split.

Who made the Red Dead Redemption 2 soundtrack, and is D'Angelo on it?#

Woody Jackson composed the score, and Daniel Lanois produced the vocal performances.157 The singers were D'Angelo, Willie Nelson, Nas, Rhiannon Giddens and Josh Homme, with Arca, Colin Stetson, Senyawa and Duane Eddy among the other contributors.157 D'Angelo's song "Unshaken" was his first new material since 2014's Black Messiah.157 It reached number 6 on US R&B Digital Song Sales.158

Willie Nelson's song is "Cruel Cruel World," and Josh Homme recorded his own version of it.159

Who did the Max Payne 3 soundtrack?#

Los Angeles band HEALTH composed and recorded the Max Payne 3 Official Soundtrack.161 The single "Tears" went on iTunes for the May 15, 2012 console launch, and the full soundtrack followed digitally on May 23, 2012.161 HEALTH's John Famiglietti said Rockstar gave the band freedom to bring its own sound to the game.161

Handing a whole game's score to a working band, with no film composer in sight, was an unusual hire. HEALTH came back for GTA Online, appearing among the contributors to its 2023 original score album.162

For the game's 10th anniversary, Rockstar announced Max Payne 3 - The Official Soundtrack (Anniversary Edition) on May 13, 2022, in digital and limited-edition vinyl formats, with tracks made for the game that had never been released.163 No release date came with the announcement.163

Does GTA Online have its own score?#

It does, though it arrives in pieces with updates rather than as one release. Who composed the GTA VI score?#

Justice. Rockstar named the duo as composers of GTA VI's original score on October 15, 2026, the same day it released the Launch Trailer.154 That trailer's song, Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, belongs to Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the separate 34-track record made with Atlantic Records (see /gta-6/), not the score.154,165

The score has its own release track. What was GTA Online's music before the nightclubs?#

The shift started with a radio station. The Doomsday Heist update on December 12, 2017 added blonded Los Santos 97.8 FM, an artist-curated station built around Frank Ocean.171 Ocean did not host it alone: his blonded RADIO co-hosts Vegyn, Roof Access and Federico Aliprandi run the show, with Ocean talking with them and choosing the music.171 The playlist mixes his own songs with picks like JAY-Z, Aphex Twin and Burial.171 It runs to 28 songs and landed in GTA Online and GTA V's story mode at the same time.65 Epic Games' 2025 retrospective lists blonded among the stations later folded back into single-player.89

Two years later, The Diamond Casino Heist brought iFruit Radio, hosted and curated by Danny Brown, which launched on December 12, 2019 with 28 tracks including Skepta, Megan Thee Stallion and Burna Boy.172,173 The 28 split into a 15-track Danny Brown playlist and a 13-track Skepta playlist, and fans report the Skepta half has been broken since Los Santos Drug Wars, leaving only Brown's.65

Both stations worked the same way: hand a real artist a frequency and let them program it. After Hours took that idea and gave it a dance floor. Every Online station, with dates and track counts, is listed at /modern-radio/.

How did the After Hours nightclubs work?#

Announced for Tuesday, July 24, 2018, After Hours was the update that made GTA Online a club venue.174 Players buy one of 10 nightclub locations and run it, from the lighting to the DJ bookings.26 The booked DJs then play custom mixes inside whichever club the player owns.26

The four residents were Solomun, Tale Of Us, Dixon and The Black Madonna.174 In July 2020 she renamed herself The Blessed Madonna, after a petition started by DJ Monty Luke gathered over 1,000 signatures.175 She played Los Santos in 2018 as The Black Madonna, so that name stays wherever the 2018 game comes up.

Rockstar rolled the residents out over four weeks, Solomun first.31 Each DJ also got in-game missions: Solomun's involves a pilotless jet, and The Black Madonna asked for one where she punches a fake cop.31 Rockstar approached her management, and she agreed on the condition that she appear as herself rather than as a generic DJ.31

The picks came from the team's own fandom, not market research. Ivan Pavlovich, Rockstar's music director, says he was following Dixon in 1996, and he once ran the label Guidance Recordings.26 How a set like this gets built offline is its own story, at https://showfile.cold.fyi/.

What did The Cayo Perico Heist add to the music?#

What did DJs say about working with Rockstar?#

In public, the residents talked about reach. Tale Of Us said in 2018 the game "will reach a huge new audience for us," and The Black Madonna praised the attention to detail.176 She went further with Rolling Stone, saying the game "changed my fanbase within a month."31 She gave no numbers.

Pavlovich, speaking in 2020, described the game as a platform for introducing players to new music, with nightclub DJs chosen to fit the world and sometimes written into story missions.180 The riders, friends and tour managers went into the capture along with the sets.26

Did being in GTA Online boost the DJs' streams?#

Nobody has published the numbers. The one large figure is Rockstar's own: 75 billion minutes of music listened to across GTA V and GTA Online, counted over 18 stations and five years in 2018 and repeated in 2020 as "since 2013."31,180 It is game-wide and not broken down by artist.

The rest is anecdote. In January 2021 Beatportal found a fan who discovered Keinemusik through Music Locker radio and a former DJ who called in-game venues a good chance at new exposure.190

CircoLoco Records: Rockstar's label with a Monday party

The short version: CircoLoco Records is a record label run as a partnership between CircoLoco, the Monday party at DC10 in Ibiza, and Rockstar Games, announced on May 24, 2021.195,196 It opened with Monday Dreamin', a 20-track compilation whose four EPs became hidden collectibles in GTA Online in July 2021, and since 2024 it has turned into a chart label for house and tech house.88,184,197 GTA VI has a CircoLoco Records Radio station, hosted by Seth Troxler and Antonio Carbonaro.198,139

What is CircoLoco, and what is DC10?#

DC10 is a club on the Carretera de Salinas in Ibiza. It started in the 1990s as a music bar licensed for 80 people, opened by the brothers Deogracias and Antonio Lara Moreno.199 In 1999 two Italian promoters, Antonio Carbonaro and Andrea Pelino, joined as partners and created CircoLoco, which began as a free Monday-morning after-party.199 The venue now holds 1,500 across three rooms (La Terrace, The Main Room and The Open Air Garden), and ranked 19th in DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs in 2020.199

CircoLoco dates its own start to "The Monday Morning Sessions" in 1999.200 Carbonaro calls it the longest-running electronic music brand among its peers, a claim nobody has measured.200 He also founded No Soul For Sale, which began as the slogan for CircoLoco's 20th year and became its own platform.200 The 25th anniversary in 2024 got a "DC10 Ibiza CircoLoco Greatest Hits" release.199

The Monday regulars include Seth Troxler, The Martinez Brothers, Tania Vulcano, Loco Dice and Luciano.197 How a party like this runs as a business is its own subject, at https://lastcall.cold.fyi/.

When did CircoLoco Records launch?#

Rockstar Games and CircoLoco announced CircoLoco Records on May 24, 2021.195,202 The plan was a weekly run of EPs taken from the album Monday Dreamin', with Seth Troxler's "Lumartes" already out as a single.201 The first EP was due June 4 and the full compilation July 9, 2021.195,201

Carbonaro traces the idea to his friendship with Sam Houser and a joint CircoLoco and Rockstar event in Miami in October 2019; the label itself was put together during the pandemic.200 It was a way to reach the community while clubs were shut for COVID-19.197 A label is a reasonable thing to build when the dance floor is closed and the game is not.

The company behind the name is Rockstar Records LLC, founded May 24, 2021, with "CircoLoco Records" as its trade name - though that comes from fan and trade sites, not a company filing.203,204

Does Rockstar own CircoLoco Records?#

It is a partnership. At launch the label was billed as independent and kept separate from both companies' other businesses for now.196 Nobody has published an ownership split.196,179

Rockstar fielded founder Sam Houser and director of music Ivan Pavlovich for the launch. "Music's been part of our DNA since day one," Pavlovich told Rolling Stone.196 Houser's stated aim was to "find new ways to bring the very best underground music to the widest audience possible."202 Nick Benedetti spoke for CircoLoco, and the 2021 artists were picked jointly, all of them CircoLoco alumni.196

The work splits roughly like this:

  • Rockstar's side: placement in its games (the Media Player sticks, a CircoLoco tee in Los Santos Tuners) and the "CircoLoco / Rockstar Games presents" credit on releases.203,184,205
  • CircoLoco's side: the A&R, the DC10 floor where tracks get tested, and the visual identity, with Rockstar as partner and backer.197

How does the label pick its records?#

A&R (artists and repertoire) is the job of finding artists and choosing what gets released. Carbonaro says there is no fixed formula: signings go on instinct and on whether an act fits CircoLoco.197 His test, in four words: "It has to feel honest."197

In practice, that means:197

  • artists who shape a scene rather than fit into one
  • a mix of new and established names
  • tracks tested on the dance floor in front of the party's crowd
  • a look that is part of the record: a blood-red palette that echoes the club, and surreal digital art for Monday Dreamin'

The catalog shows the taste moving. The 2021 compilation leans on long-time CircoLoco names such as Luciano, Carl Craig and Moodymann; from 2024 on the releases tilt toward chart tech house and house, with Prospa, PAWSA, Jamback and Michael Bibi, and Prospa turning up more than anyone.206,207,205,208 That shift is plain in the release list; the label hasn't described it.

Why songs disappear: licenses, expiry and removals

The short version: A song on a GTA radio station is rented, not owned. Each licensed track needs permission for both the composition and the recording, usually for a fixed term, and when those permissions run out Rockstar patches the song out of the versions it still sells.246,247 That has happened to Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and, in September 2026, GTA V - a game Take-Two says has sold nearly 235 million units (official, November 2026).91,248 GTA VI arrived on November 19, 2026, with a commissioned 34-track album beside 829 licensed songs, and nobody has said how its music deals are built.165

How does music licensing for a video game work?#

Every licensed song carries two separate copyrights. The composition (or publishing) is the song itself, owned by a music publisher. The master is one specific recording of it, usually owned by a label. A game needs a sync license from the publisher and a master use license from the label, and clearing one does not clear the other.246

Each deal is priced on its scope. Term is how long it lasts, and a perpetual license costs more than a fixed window. Territory is where it applies, and worldwide costs more than a handful of countries. Media covers which uses are included - in-game playback on each platform, trailers and store pages are separate decisions. A buyout is a flat one-time fee, and an option lets a publisher extend a license to sequels, DLC or ports at a rate agreed in advance.246

The complication is headcount. EA's Lori Cromwell-Charron told Digital Music News that each track has at least two, and sometimes six or more, rights holders, and every one of them has to sign off. Early Guitar Hero games used cover versions to avoid paying for masters at all. Perpetual licenses exist, but they are rare.247

What is actually known about Rockstar's music deals?#

Very little. Rockstar has never published a license length, fee, territory or deal structure. Everything known about its terms comes from removal timing or from artists' accounts.251

The most-cited account is Heaven 17's. In a September 7, 2024 post, Martyn Ware said his publishers had relayed a Rockstar offer of $7,500 to use "Temptation" (1983) in GTA VI, framed as a buyout of future royalties from the game in perpetuity. He turned it down. That is Ware's account alone: Rockstar did not comment, and Mixmag's report doesn't say whether the offer covered the master, the publishing or both.251

In the same exchange, Simon Raymonde of Cocteau Twins argued the fee was normal for one song, given that the last game carried about 440 songs - his number, and nobody has checked it.251 Between them, the two accounts establish one thing with some confidence: at least one GTA VI approach was pitched as a one-off payment rather than a royalty stream. Whether that is typical, and whether it was perpetual for in-game use, is not public.

How does GTA VI's commissioned album change the equation?#

GTA VI launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.2,3 Beside its radio it has GTA VI: The Album, 34 original tracks made with Atlantic Records and announced September 17, 2026, including Keith Richards' cover of Jimmy Reed's "Bright Lights, Big City."165,268 Justice composed the original score, and the game shipped with 22 radio stations, six of them with artist hosts named in advance.154,153,3 All three are covered in GTA VI.

Rockstar's vice president of music, Ivan Pavlovich, told Billboard in November 2026 that the team licenses about three times as many songs as it ends up using, and that one of them, The Cars' "Drive," was a song Rockstar had failed to clear for Vice City in 2002.167 He did not discuss terms.

What changes is the starting point. Licensed radio rents songs that already exist; a commissioned album and score are music made for the game. The open questions matter more: who owns the album's masters, what terms cover the stations' licensed tracks, and whether anything is perpetual. The Heaven 17 account suggests at least some GTA VI approaches were buyouts, and that is one artist's word.251 A cover like the Keith Richards track still involves someone else's composition.

How was the GTA 6 soundtrack put together?#

By licensing far more than it used. Ivan Pavlovich, Rockstar's vice president of music, told Billboard the game has more than 800 songs and that the team licenses about three times as many as end up in it: "We're probably licensing three times the amount of songs we end up using."167 The credits list 829.137

Some of it took decades. Rockstar tried and failed to clear The Cars' "Drive" for GTA Vice City in 2002; in GTA VI, Billboard reports, it is the first song a player hears on the radio.167 The credits put it on Emotion 98.3, the soft rock station that also dates to Vice City.137

Pavlovich described clearing the international records as "detective work," naming the songs from Thailand and Vietnam on Kaleidoscope FM.167 Clearances ran through the music, licensing and legal teams, with Sam Houser involved.167 For some missions Rockstar built songs in as separate stems, among them Travis Scott and Justice tracks.167 How the deals are structured, and what any of it cost, is not in the interview. More on that in Why songs disappear.

Who composed the GTA 6 score?#

Justice. Rockstar named the French duo, Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay, as composers of GTA VI's original score, music written for the game rather than licensed, on October 15, 2026, the same day as the Launch Trailer.154,168

It took about four years. The job arrived as a text from their managers asking whether they wanted to score the game, and Justice had never scored one.167 "When we started working, I had one kid," Xavier de Rosnay told Billboard; he now has three.167 They spent a week at Rockstar's New York office and otherwise worked in near-total secrecy with Pavlovich, Sam Houser and Rob Nelson of Rockstar North, writing much of it on their 2024 tour.167 The brief was to be fun, to avoid sounding like video game music, and to draw on their 2011 album Audio, Video, Disco.167 Each scene got 20 to 25 minutes of music, to cover however fast or slowly a player moves through it, and the duo say they do not know how many hours it adds up to.167 "There was nobody else who could do this but Justice," Pavlovich said.167

The credits show how much help they had. The main score is "composed, produced, performed by Justice," featuring Boys Noize, Gesaffelstein, King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard and Phil Collen.137 The British duo Overmono has a separate credit for additional score. Then comes a supplemental score from 17 acts, including 100 gecs, A. G. Cook, Bobby Krlic, Boys Noize, Idles, DJ Python, Nick León, Oh No and Take a Daytrip.137 Oh No was one of the four composers of GTA V's score.

Four Justice songs with guest singers are credited with the score: "Starting From Zero" with Michael McDonald, "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard, "The Night's Coming" with Djo, and "My Only Vice" with The Weeknd.137 The first two are also on The Album.270

The score gets its own record. Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score is due on December 11, 2026, three weeks after the game: 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, on streaming, CD and vinyl, including a triple clear-vinyl edition.168 Billboard counts 50 pieces, which may be the same record with the bonus tracks included.167 Two pieces came out before launch, "Flower in a Hurricane" and "Pusher," featuring Gesaffelstein, which arrived with the album announcement on October 29.168,270 "Pusher" runs seven minutes and Gesaffelstein co-wrote it; "Starting From Zero," the opening credits song, was recorded with Michael McDonald in a home studio in Los Feliz.167 "Flower in a Hurricane" debuted at No. 62 on the Hot 100 and No. 2 on Hot Dance/Electronic Songs, and its US streams rose 153.4% the day the game came out.167

Rockstar calls the score adaptive, with compositions "dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with the world."168 The credits back that up with job titles: Brady Custis is "Music Director & Adaptive Score Composer."137 Ivan Pavlovich is credited as Vice President of Music and Tony Mesones as Director: Soundtrack & Artist Relations, and one line covers the paperwork: "All music licensing by DMG Clearances."137 More on how GTA scores work in Scores.
