Every song pulled from Grand Theft Auto

Music licenses run out, and when they do, songs leave the games - sometimes from copies people already own. Here is every song that went, and when. The why is in Why songs disappear.

Pulled from the dial

87 songs that left the games

  1. 2012-11-16Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (PC digital) The whole game, temporarily pulled from digital PC stores; the trigger was reported to be Michael Jackson's "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'", with Sony Music as claimant (unconfirmed) source
  2. 2012-12-08Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (PC digital re-release incl. Steam; mobile 10th anniversary release) Billie Jean and Wanna Be Startin' Somethin' (Michael Jackson), Bark at the Moon (Ozzy Osbourne), Wow (Kate Bush), Rockit (Herbie Hancock), Looking for the Perfect Beat (Afrika Bambaataa), Running with the Night (Lionel Richie), plus "a couple of songs from Radio Espantoso" (fan-compiled list) nearly 10 (7 named) source
  3. 2014-11-09Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (Steam PC update; matches mobile and Xbox 360 HD versions) 17 licensed songs (plus high-resolution options); same 17 already absent from the mobile version and the Xbox 360 HD port of it 17 source
  4. 2018Grand Theft Auto V Kano, "Can't Hold Back (Your Lovin')" 1 source
  5. 2018-04-26Grand Theft Auto IV and Episodes from Liberty City Tracks across 10 stations; Vladivostok FM hit hardest (15 tracks), also Liberty Rock Radio, The Journey, Vice City FM and others; 11 replacements added to Vladivostok FM 43 listed by Game Informer; Destructoid counts 51, including Episodes tracks and one unused track source
  6. 2021-11-10Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy - The Definitive Edition (Vice City and San Andreas) Launch track list missing 20 Vice City and 18 San Andreas songs, including Billie Jean and Wanna Be Startin' Somethin' (Michael Jackson), Killing in the Name (Rage Against the Machine) and Express Yourself (N.W.A.); later in November 2021 Rockstar sent an amended list with "a number of tracks reinstated", naming none 38 at first report; how many stayed missing after the amended list is not public source
  7. 2026-09-15Grand Theft Auto V Enhanced (PC; consoles expected) Moloko, "The Time Is Now" (2000), from Non-Stop-Pop FM, via hotfix spotted by dataminers 1 source
