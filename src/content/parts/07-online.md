## GTA Online: the club, the label and the residency

**The short version:** From late 2017, GTA Online stopped being a game that licensed music and started acting like a venue, a radio network and a record label. The After Hours update (July 2018) let players own nightclubs where four real DJs held motion-captured residencies, and Cayo Perico (December 2020) added a second club, The Music Locker, plus three new stations. In 2021 came CircoLoco Records, the Media Player and six new Dr. Dre tracks that premiered inside the game weeks before streaming.

[[MODULE:online]]

### What was GTA Online's music before the nightclubs?

The shift started with a radio station. The Doomsday Heist update on December 12, 2017 added **blonded Los Santos 97.8 FM**, an artist-curated station built around Frank Ocean.[^onl-fader-2017] Ocean did not host it alone: his blonded RADIO co-hosts Vegyn, Roof Access and Federico Aliprandi run the show, with Ocean talking with them and choosing the music.[^onl-fader-2017] The playlist mixes his own songs with picks like JAY-Z, Aphex Twin and Burial.[^onl-fader-2017] It runs to 28 songs and landed in GTA Online and GTA V's story mode at the same time.[^onl-gtawiki-stations] Epic Games' 2025 retrospective lists blonded among the stations later folded back into single-player.[^onl-epic-2025]

Two years later, The Diamond Casino Heist brought **iFruit Radio**, hosted and curated by Danny Brown, which launched on December 12, 2019 with 28 tracks including Skepta, Megan Thee Stallion and Burna Boy.[^onl-gtaboom-ifruit][^onl-djmag-2020] The 28 split into a 15-track Danny Brown playlist and a 13-track Skepta playlist, and fans report the Skepta half has been broken since Los Santos Drug Wars, leaving only Brown's.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

Both stations worked the same way: hand a real artist a frequency and let them program it. After Hours took that idea and gave it a dance floor. Every Online station, with dates and track counts, is listed at /modern-radio/.

### How did the After Hours nightclubs work?

Announced for Tuesday, July 24, 2018, **After Hours** was the update that made GTA Online a club venue.[^onl-pcgamer-2018] Players buy one of 10 nightclub locations and run it, from the lighting to the DJ bookings.[^onl-fact-2018] The booked DJs then play custom mixes inside whichever club the player owns.[^onl-fact-2018]

The four residents were Solomun, Tale Of Us, Dixon and The Black Madonna.[^onl-pcgamer-2018] In July 2020 she renamed herself The Blessed Madonna, after a petition started by DJ Monty Luke gathered over 1,000 signatures.[^onl-mixmag-2020] She played Los Santos in 2018 as The Black Madonna, so that name stays wherever the 2018 game comes up.

Rockstar rolled the residents out over four weeks, Solomun first.[^onl-rollingstone] Each DJ also got in-game missions: Solomun's involves a pilotless jet, and The Black Madonna asked for one where she punches a fake cop.[^onl-rollingstone] Rockstar approached her management, and she agreed on the condition that she appear as herself rather than as a generic DJ.[^onl-rollingstone]

The picks came from the team's own fandom, not market research. Ivan Pavlovich, Rockstar's music director, says he was following Dixon in 1996, and he once ran the label Guidance Recordings.[^onl-fact-2018] How a set like this gets built offline is its own story, at https://showfile.cold.fyi/.

### How do you motion-capture a DJ set?

You throw a party and film it. Rolling Stone puts each resident in a motion-capture studio on Long Island, playing a live set to invited friends, family, managers and fans, with Rockstar copying their tech riders and drinks orders.[^onl-rollingstone] Pavlovich put it plainly: "We literally threw a party in the mo-cap studio and captured it all."[^onl-rollingstone] Mixmag puts the sessions at Rockstar's New York headquarters instead, with the DJs in suits covered in tracking dots.[^onl-mixmag-2018]

The in-game booths were matched to each DJ's real habits:[^onl-mixmag-2018]

| Resident | In-game setup |
|---|---|
| Solomun | Pioneer DJM-2000 mixer |
| Dixon | Rotary mixer |
| Tale Of Us | Laptops running Traktor |
| The Black Madonna | Allen & Heath mixer |

A licensed track list gets a DJ's music into a game. Rockstar copied the mixer, the rider and the room as well.[^onl-rollingstone]

### What is Los Santos Underground Radio?

**Los Santos Underground Radio** (LSUR) is the station After Hours added to carry the residents' music outside the club. It opened with an exclusive Solomun mix recorded live, and the Tale Of Us residency followed the week of July 31, 2018, debuting tracks from their album Afterlight, which was also due on Apple Music and Spotify.[^onl-worthplaying-2018] Tale Of Us made an 18-track album for the game, and the station loops the DJs' mixes and selections.[^onl-mixmag-2018]

Fan tracklists put LSUR at 65 songs, grouped by resident:[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

| LSUR playlist | Tracks |
|---|---|
| Solomun | 18 |
| Tale Of Us | 18 |
| Dixon | 15 |
| The Black Madonna | 14 |
| Total | 65 |

The club also left the game once. On August 1, 2018, Tale Of Us played a 30-minute set from a virtual Los Santos club, streamed on Resident Advisor's Facebook page, which DJ Mag described as the first virtual DJ livestream of its kind.[^onl-djmag-2018]

### What did The Cayo Perico Heist add to the music?

The Cayo Perico Heist arrived December 15, 2020, with what Rockstar described as "well over 250 brand new tracks" across three new stations and fresh mixes on FlyLo FM and Worldwide FM.[^onl-gamesradar-2020] The three new stations were Still Slipping Los Santos (Joy Orbison), Kult FM (Julian Casablancas) and Music Locker radio.[^onl-gamesradar-2020]

**The Music Locker** is an underground club beneath The Diamond Casino, with three residents: Moodymann from Detroit, Keinemusik from Berlin, and Palms Trax.[^onl-djmag-2020] The in-game cover charge is about $150.[^onl-epic-2025] Keinemusik also played the tropical beach party on Cayo Perico, the island where the heist takes place.[^onl-mbw-2021]

The capture sessions happened mid-pandemic. Moodymann travelled from Detroit with his vinyl and his dancers to be motion-captured during quarantine, while Keinemusik played with custom effects gear.[^onl-complex-2020] By January 2021 the virtual sets by all three residents were up on YouTube, watchable outside the game.[^onl-dancingastronaut-2021]

The other two stations leaned odd. Still Slipping's reach is extended by an antenna collectible, and Kult FM's on-air voice is a producer called Zachary, played by David Cross.[^onl-epic-2025] Until December 22, 2020, Still Slipping could only be heard in Mirror Park.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

### What is CircoLoco Records, and how does it show up in the game?

On May 24, 2021, CircoLoco and Rockstar Games announced **CircoLoco Records**, a record label launched in partnership, with a first EP on June 4 and a compilation on July 9, 2021.[^onl-mbw-2021] Its releases and ownership are at /circoloco/. Inside GTA Online, it lives mostly on the Media Player.

### What is the Media Player in GTA Online?

The **Media Player** is a slot on the radio wheel that plays collectible **media sticks**, in-world USB drives that unlock a mix or EP. It arrived with Los Santos Tuners on July 20, 2021.[^onl-gtabase-mediaplayer][^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

That update brought Moodymann back as a character: "Kenny," a contact in the street-racing scene, dressed in Born x Raised.[^onl-djmag-2021] His **Kenny's Backyard Boogie** mix covers house, soul, hip hop and funk, with exclusive collaborations featuring Channel Tres, Nez with Gangsta Boo, and Jesse Johnson of The Time; players get the stick by visiting Kenny at the LS Car Meet.[^onl-djmag-2021][^onl-worthplaying-2021-07] It runs to 35 tracks.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

The same update hid four CircoLoco Records EP sticks around the map; collecting all four unlocks Seth Troxler's **CLR Launch Party** mix.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-07] GTA Base counts 20 tracks in that mix; the fan wiki lists 16.[^onl-gtabase-mediaplayer][^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

The Contract added a six-track Dr. Dre stick and a two-track "Singles" stick with Nez and ScHoolboy Q. Fans say three sticks came with that update but list only those two.[^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer][^onl-gtawiki-stations]

### What happened to the music in The Contract?

The Contract launched December 15, 2021, and brought back GTA V's Franklin; the storyline has players helping track down unreleased Dr. Dre music through Record A Studios.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12] Six new Dre tracks premiered in-game, including "Gospel" with Eminem and "ETA" with Snoop Dogg and Anderson .Paak, and Rockstar billed them as exclusive to the update at launch.[^onl-djmag-dre] Epic Games later called them effectively his first new album since Compton in 2015.[^onl-epic-2025] The six songs reached streaming services in early February 2022, ahead of Dre's February 13 Super Bowl halftime show, which made for an **exclusive window** of about seven weeks.[^onl-fader-2022]

The update rebuilt three stations around the Dre storyline:

- **MOTOMAMI Los Santos**, hosted by ROSALÍA and Arca, a longtime Rockstar collaborator, with an exclusive Bad Gyal song, "A La Mía," produced by Keinemusik, plus Caroline Polachek, Daddy Yankee, Mr. Fingers and Camarón de la Isla.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12][^onl-rockstarintel-2021] It runs to 33 tracks.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]
- **Radio Los Santos** relaunched with host Big Boy and exclusives from Hit-Boy, Freddie Gibbs with Pusha T, and others, and premiered CircoLoco Records' ScHoolboy Q single "Let's Get It," produced by Nez.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12] From I-Wagen Week on, it also carries the Dre tracks.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]
- **West Coast Classics** got a "Dre Day" takeover hosted by DJ Pooh, with call-ins from Dre collaborators, while DāM-FunK scored the update.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12] Dre Day ran 17 tracks, one repeated from the regular playlist, and all were later merged into it.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

Nez's tracks stayed GTA-exclusive until an EP in early 2022.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12]

### What is the DāM-FunK mix from The Chop Shop?

DāM-FunK's work for GTA Online did not stop with scoring The Contract. Los Santos Drug Wars (2022) got its own adaptive score from Soulwax and Nick Hook,[^onl-gtawiki-drugwars] and in December 2023 Rockstar released a DāM-FunK score album for GTA Online.[^onl-rockstarintel-2023] Both are covered at /scores/.

Inside the game there is a matching media stick. The Chop Shop added DāM-FunK's "Even the Score," billed as a recording of West Coast Classics' 10-year anniversary party and unlocked by calling DJ Pooh at the station.[^fc-gtawiki-mediasticks] Its 15 tracks are named after GTA Online updates, from "Bikers" to "Smuggler's Run" to five Contract cues.[^onl-gtawiki-stations] All of this is fan-documented.[^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer] It is the only stick tied to DJ Pooh; no separate DJ Pooh mix is documented.

### What did DJs say about working with Rockstar?

In public, the residents talked about reach. Tale Of Us said in 2018 the game "will reach a huge new audience for us," and The Black Madonna praised the attention to detail.[^onl-mixmag-2018] She went further with Rolling Stone, saying the game "changed my fanbase within a month."[^onl-rollingstone] She gave no numbers.

Pavlovich, speaking in 2020, described the game as a platform for introducing players to new music, with nightclub DJs chosen to fit the world and sometimes written into story missions.[^onl-complex-2020] The riders, friends and tour managers went into the capture along with the sets.[^onl-fact-2018]

### Did being in GTA Online boost the DJs' streams?

Nobody has published the numbers. The one large figure is Rockstar's own: 75 billion minutes of music listened to across GTA V and GTA Online, counted over 18 stations and five years in 2018 and repeated in 2020 as "since 2013."[^onl-rollingstone][^onl-complex-2020] It is game-wide and not broken down by artist.

The rest is anecdote. In January 2021 Beatportal found a fan who discovered Keinemusik through Music Locker radio and a former DJ who called in-game venues a good chance at new exposure.[^onl-beatportal-2021]

### What music came with the 2024 to 2025 GTA Online updates?

Mostly score work. The Bottom Dollar Bounties patch notes (June 25, 2024) list no new songs or stations, only a Music Locker menu change and audio previews in the Race Creator.[^gf-b-rockstarintel-2024] Agents of Sabotage brought a four-track Nick Hook score on December 10, 2024, each track with eight stems the game mixes live.[^gf-b-gtawiki-sabotage] Money Fronts (June 17, 2025) has a 15-track soundtrack, and A Safehouse in the Hills (December 10, 2025) has a 13-track score credited to Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, James Curd and Nick Hook, mostly reused cues.[^gf-b-gtawiki-moneyfronts][^gf-b-gtawiki-safehouse] None of them brought in a new artist or label partnership.
