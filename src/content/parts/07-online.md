## GTA Online: the club, the label and the residency

**The short version:** From late 2017, GTA Online stopped being a game that licensed music and started acting like a venue, a radio network and a record label. The After Hours update (July 2018) let players own nightclubs where four real DJs held motion-captured residencies, and Cayo Perico (December 2020) added a second club, The Music Locker, plus three new stations. In 2021 came CircoLoco Records, the Media Player and six new Dr. Dre tracks that premiered inside the game weeks before streaming.

[[MODULE:online]]

### What was GTA Online's music before the nightclubs?

The shift started with a radio station. The Doomsday Heist update on December 12, 2017 added **blonded Los Santos 97.8 FM**, an artist-curated station built around Frank Ocean.[^onl-fader-2017] Ocean did not host it alone: his blonded RADIO co-hosts Vegyn, Roof Access and Federico Aliprandi run the show, with Ocean talking with them and choosing the music.[^onl-fader-2017] The playlist mixes his own songs with picks like JAY-Z, Aphex Twin and Burial.[^onl-fader-2017] Per the GTA wiki's tracklist, it runs to 28 songs and was added to GTA Online and GTA V's story mode at the same time.[^onl-gtawiki-stations] Epic Games' 2025 retrospective counts blonded among the stations later folded back into single-player.[^onl-epic-2025]

Two years later, The Diamond Casino Heist brought **iFruit Radio**, hosted and curated by Danny Brown, launched on December 12 with 28 tracks including Skepta, Megan Thee Stallion and Burna Boy.[^onl-gtaboom-ifruit] The fan wiki page that describes it is undated; DJ Mag's 2020 coverage places the Danny Brown station in 2019.[^onl-djmag-2020] Per the GTA wiki, the 28 songs split into a 15-track Danny Brown playlist and a 13-track Skepta playlist, though the wiki also reports that since Los Santos Drug Wars the Skepta playlist is broken, leaving only Brown's half.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

Both stations worked the same way: hand a real artist a frequency and let them program it. After Hours took that idea and gave it a dance floor. Every Online station, with dates and track counts, is listed at /modern-radio/.

### How did the After Hours nightclubs work?

Announced for Tuesday, July 24, 2018, **After Hours** was the update that made GTA Online a club venue.[^onl-pcgamer-2018] Players buy one of 10 nightclub locations, and run it (the lighting, the DJ bookings).[^onl-fact-2018] The booked DJs then play custom mixes inside whichever club the player owns.[^onl-fact-2018]

The four residents were Solomun, Tale Of Us, Dixon and The Black Madonna.[^onl-pcgamer-2018] Mixmag reported on July 20, 2020 that the last of those had renamed herself The Blessed Madonna, after a petition started by DJ Monty Luke gathered over 1,000 signatures, so later coverage uses the new name.[^onl-mixmag-2020] This guide uses the name she performed under in 2018 where it describes the 2018 game.

Rockstar rolled the residents out over four weeks, Solomun first.[^onl-rollingstone] Each DJ also got in-game missions: Solomun's involves a pilotless jet, and The Black Madonna asked for one where she punches a fake cop.[^onl-rollingstone] Rolling Stone reports Rockstar approached The Black Madonna's management, and she agreed on the condition that she appear as herself rather than as a generic DJ.[^onl-rollingstone]

How the residents were picked says a lot about the whole project. Ivan Pavlovich, Rockstar's music director, told FACT the choices came from the team's own fandom rather than market research; he says he was following Dixon in 1996, and he previously headed the label Guidance Recordings.[^onl-fact-2018] For anyone curious how a set like this gets built offline, the sister guide https://showfile.cold.fyi/ covers that side.

### How do you motion-capture a DJ set?

Rolling Stone describes each resident playing a live set in a motion-capture studio on Long Island, with invited friends, family, managers and fans in the room, and Rockstar copying their tech riders and drinks orders.[^onl-rollingstone] Pavlovich put it plainly: "We literally threw a party in the mo-cap studio and captured it all."[^onl-rollingstone]

Mixmag's account differs on the location: it says the DJs were captured at Rockstar's New York headquarters, in suits covered in tracking dots.[^onl-mixmag-2018] The two sources disagree and neither is the final word.

The in-game booths were matched to each DJ's real habits, per Mixmag:[^onl-mixmag-2018]

| Resident | In-game setup |
|---|---|
| Solomun | Pioneer DJM-2000 mixer |
| Dixon | Rotary mixer |
| Tale Of Us | Laptops running Traktor |
| The Black Madonna | Allen & Heath mixer |

That level of detail is the point. A licensed track list gets a DJ's music into a game; this approach recreated the DJ, the gear and the party around them.[^onl-rollingstone]

### What is Los Santos Underground Radio?

**Los Santos Underground Radio** (LSUR) is the station After Hours added to carry the residents' music outside the club. It opened with an exclusive Solomun mix recorded live, and the Tale Of Us residency followed the week of July 31, 2018, debuting tracks from their album Afterlight, which was also due on Apple Music and Spotify.[^onl-worthplaying-2018] Mixmag reports Tale Of Us made an 18-track album for the game, and that the station loops the DJs' mixes and selections.[^onl-mixmag-2018]

The GTA wiki's tracklist for LSUR has 65 songs, grouped by resident:[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

| LSUR playlist | Tracks (per GTA wiki) |
|---|---|
| Solomun | 18 |
| Tale Of Us | 18 |
| Dixon | 15 |
| The Black Madonna | 14 |
| Total | 65 |

The club also left the game once. On August 1, 2018, Tale Of Us played a 30-minute set from a virtual Los Santos club, streamed on Resident Advisor's Facebook page, which DJ Mag described as the first virtual DJ livestream of its kind.[^onl-djmag-2018]

### What did The Cayo Perico Heist add to the music?

The Cayo Perico Heist arrived December 15, 2020, with what Rockstar described as "well over 250 brand new tracks" across three new stations and fresh mixes on FlyLo FM and Worldwide FM.[^onl-gamesradar-2020] The three new stations were Still Slipping Los Santos (Joy Orbison), Kult FM (Julian Casablancas) and Music Locker radio.[^onl-gamesradar-2020]

**The Music Locker** is an underground club beneath The Diamond Casino, with three residents: Moodymann from Detroit, Keinemusik from Berlin, and Palms Trax.[^onl-djmag-2020] The in-game cover charge is about $150.[^onl-epic-2025] Keinemusik also played the tropical beach party on Cayo Perico, the island where the heist takes place.[^onl-mbw-2021]

The capture sessions happened mid-pandemic. Complex reports Moodymann travelled from Detroit with his vinyl and his dancers to be motion-captured during quarantine, while Keinemusik played with custom effects gear.[^onl-complex-2020] In January 2021 Dancing Astronaut embedded YouTube videos of the virtual DJ sets by all three residents, so the sets can be watched outside the game.[^onl-dancingastronaut-2021]

The other two stations leaned odd. Still Slipping's reach is extended by an antenna collectible, and Kult FM's on-air voice is a producer called Zachary, played by David Cross.[^onl-epic-2025] Per the GTA wiki, Still Slipping was only audible in Mirror Park until December 22, 2020.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

That is 108 rows for Music Locker Radio.[^onl-gtawiki-stations] The same Complex interview says the music team joins development early and works directly with Rockstar founder Sam Houser, and that FlyLo FM got unreleased material including a Mac Miller verse.[^onl-complex-2020]

### What is CircoLoco Records, and how does it show up in the game?

On May 24, 2021, CircoLoco and Rockstar Games announced **CircoLoco Records**, a record label launched in partnership, with a first EP on June 4 and a compilation on July 9, 2021.[^onl-mbw-2021] Its releases and ownership are covered at /circoloco/; what follows is how it plays inside GTA Online.

### What is the Media Player in GTA Online?

The **Media Player** is a slot on the radio wheel that plays collectible **media sticks**, in-world USB drives that unlock a mix or EP. It arrived with Los Santos Tuners on July 20, 2021.[^onl-gtabase-mediaplayer][^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

That update brought Moodymann back as a character: "Kenny," a contact in the street-racing scene, dressed in Born x Raised.[^onl-djmag-2021] His **Kenny's Backyard Boogie** mix covers house, soul, hip hop and funk, with exclusive collaborations featuring Channel Tres, Nez with Gangsta Boo, and Jesse Johnson of The Time; players get the stick by visiting Kenny at the LS Car Meet.[^onl-djmag-2021][^onl-worthplaying-2021-07] The GTA wiki lists 35 tracks on it.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

The same update hid four CircoLoco Records EP sticks around the map; collecting all four unlocks Seth Troxler's **CLR Launch Party** mix.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-07] The sources disagree on that mix's track count (20 or 16), as the CircoLoco section sets out.[^onl-gtabase-mediaplayer][^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

Per the GTA wiki, The Contract added a Dr. Dre stick with six tracks and a two-track "Singles" stick with Nez and ScHoolboy Q; the wiki's Media Player page says three sticks came with that update but lists only those two.[^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer][^onl-gtawiki-stations]

### What happened to the music in The Contract?

The Contract launched December 15, 2021, and brought back GTA V's Franklin; the storyline has players helping track down unreleased Dr. Dre music through Record A Studios.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12] Six new Dre tracks premiered in-game, including "Gospel" with Eminem and "ETA" with Snoop Dogg and Anderson .Paak, and Rockstar billed them as exclusive to the update at launch.[^onl-djmag-dre] Epic's 2025 piece calls them effectively his first new album since Compton in 2015.[^onl-epic-2025] The six songs reached streaming services in early February 2022, ahead of Dre's February 13 Super Bowl halftime show - an **exclusive window** of about seven weeks, estimated from those dates.[^onl-fader-2022]

The update rebuilt three stations around the Dre storyline:

- **MOTOMAMI Los Santos**, hosted by ROSALÍA and Arca (described as a longtime Rockstar collaborator), with an exclusive Bad Gyal song, "A La Mía," produced by Keinemusik, plus Caroline Polachek, Daddy Yankee, Mr. Fingers and Camarón de la Isla.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12][^onl-rockstarintel-2021] The GTA wiki lists 33 tracks.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]
- **Radio Los Santos** relaunched with host Big Boy and exclusives from Hit-Boy, Freddie Gibbs with Pusha T, and others, and premiered CircoLoco Records' ScHoolboy Q single "Let's Get It," produced by Nez.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12] Per the GTA wiki, the Dre tracks moved onto the station from I-Wagen Week.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]
- **West Coast Classics** got a "Dre Day" takeover hosted by DJ Pooh, with call-ins from Dre collaborators, while DāM-FunK scored the update.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12] The wiki lists 17 Dre Day tracks, one a repeat from the regular playlist, all later merged into it.[^onl-gtawiki-stations]

Nez's tracks stayed GTA-exclusive until an EP in early 2022.[^onl-worthplaying-2021-12]

### What is the DāM-FunK mix from The Chop Shop?

DāM-FunK's work for GTA Online did not stop with scoring The Contract. Los Santos Drug Wars (2022) got its own adaptive score from Soulwax and Nick Hook,[^onl-gtawiki-drugwars] and in December 2023 Rockstar released a DāM-FunK score album for GTA Online.[^onl-rockstarintel-2023] Both are covered at /scores/.

Inside the game, a matching media stick exists. Per the GTA wiki, The Chop Shop added DāM-FunK's "Even the Score," billed as a recording of West Coast Classics' 10-year anniversary party and unlocked by calling DJ Pooh at the station.[^fc-gtawiki-mediasticks] Its 15 tracks are named after GTA Online updates, from "Bikers" to "Smuggler's Run" to five Contract cues.[^onl-gtawiki-stations] The wiki's Media Player page also links the stick to The Chop Shop; treat the details as fan-documented.[^onl-gtawiki-mediaplayer] In the sources used here, this is the only media stick tied to DJ Pooh; no separate DJ Pooh mix turned up.

### What did DJs say about working with Rockstar?

In public, the residents talked about reach. Tale Of Us told Mixmag in 2018 the game "will reach a huge new audience for us," and The Black Madonna praised the attention to detail.[^onl-mixmag-2018] Rolling Stone's feature has the strongest claim: The Black Madonna said the game "changed my fanbase within a month."[^onl-rollingstone] She gave no numbers.

Pavlovich's version, to Complex in 2020, is that Rockstar sees the game as a platform for introducing players to new music, and that nightclub DJs are chosen to fit the world and sometimes written into story missions.[^onl-complex-2020] FACT's interview adds the human side: riders, friends and tour managers were captured along with the sets.[^onl-fact-2018]

### Did being in GTA Online boost the DJs' streams?

Nobody has published the numbers. The one large figure is Rockstar's own estimate: 75 billion minutes of music listened to across GTA V and GTA Online, which Rolling Stone reported against 18 stations and five years since launch, and which Complex repeated in 2020 as "since 2013."[^onl-rollingstone][^onl-complex-2020] That is official, game-wide and not broken down by artist.

The rest is anecdote. Beatportal in January 2021 found a fan who discovered Keinemusik through Music Locker radio and a former DJ who called in-game venues a good chance at new exposure.[^onl-beatportal-2021]

### What music came with the 2024 to 2025 GTA Online updates?

Mostly score work, on the evidence found. The Bottom Dollar Bounties patch notes (June 25, 2024), as Rockstar Intel published them, list no new songs or stations, only a Music Locker menu change and audio previews in the Race Creator.[^gf-b-rockstarintel-2024] Agents of Sabotage brought a four-track Nick Hook score on December 10, 2024, each track with eight stems the game mixes live, per the GTA wiki.[^gf-b-gtawiki-sabotage] Money Fronts (June 17, 2025) has a 15-track soundtrack per the same wiki, and A Safehouse in the Hills (December 10, 2025) has a 13-track score credited to Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, James Curd and Nick Hook, mostly reused cues.[^gf-b-gtawiki-moneyfronts][^gf-b-gtawiki-safehouse] None of these pages describes a new artist or label partnership.
