## Why songs disappear: licenses, expiry and removals

**The short version:** A song on a GTA radio station is rented, not owned. Each licensed track needs permission for both the composition and the recording, usually for a fixed term, and when those permissions run out Rockstar patches the song out of the versions it still sells.[^lic-legalmoves-2026][^lic-dmn-2022] That has happened to Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and, in September 2026, GTA V - a game Take-Two says has sold nearly 235 million units (official, November 2026).[^lic-gtaboom-2026-09][^lic-gamingbolt-2026] GTA VI arrived on November 19, 2026, with a commissioned 34-track album beside 829 licensed songs, and nobody has said how its music deals are built.[^lic-mbw-2026]

### How does music licensing for a video game work?

Every licensed song carries two separate copyrights. The **composition** (or publishing) is the song itself, owned by a music publisher. The **master** is one specific recording of it, usually owned by a label. A game needs a **sync license** from the publisher and a **master use license** from the label, and clearing one does not clear the other.[^lic-legalmoves-2026]

Each deal is priced on its scope. **Term** is how long it lasts, and a perpetual license costs more than a fixed window. **Territory** is where it applies, and worldwide costs more than a handful of countries. **Media** covers which uses are included - in-game playback on each platform, trailers and store pages are separate decisions. A **buyout** is a flat one-time fee, and an **option** lets a publisher extend a license to sequels, DLC or ports at a rate agreed in advance.[^lic-legalmoves-2026]

The complication is headcount. EA's Lori Cromwell-Charron told Digital Music News that each track has at least two, and sometimes six or more, **rights holders**, and every one of them has to sign off. Early Guitar Hero games used cover versions to avoid paying for masters at all. Perpetual licenses exist, but they are rare.[^lic-dmn-2022]

### How long does a game music license last?

Nobody publishes their contracts, so the best evidence is when songs vanish. The known cases cluster between seven and 10 years:[^lic-dmn-2022][^lic-avclub-2025][^lic-gameinformer-2018-04-10]

| Game | Reported term | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Alan Wake (2010) | Seven years | Pulled from sale in 2017 |
| Alpha Protocol | Nine years | Still missing from Steam as of 2022 |
| GTA IV (2008) | 10 years (inferred from timing) | Songs removed April 26, 2018 |
| Rock Band 4 (2015) | 10 years (Harmonix) | Delisted in 2025; each DLC song goes at its own 10-year mark |

Game Informer worked out the GTA IV figure: the removals landed three days before the game's 10th anniversary, and Rockstar had confirmed licensing restrictions. It is an inference, not a published term.[^lic-gameinformer-2018-04-10] The A.V. Club's piece on Rock Band 4 mentions in passing that Rockstar has had to pull songs from earlier GTA titles - by now the habit is familiar enough to be shorthand.[^lic-avclub-2025]

### What is actually known about Rockstar's music deals?

Very little. Rockstar has never published a license length, fee, territory or deal structure. Everything known about its terms comes from removal timing or from artists' accounts.[^lic-mixmag-2024]

The most-cited account is Heaven 17's. In a September 7, 2024 post, Martyn Ware said his publishers had relayed a Rockstar offer of $7,500 to use "Temptation" (1983) in GTA VI, framed as a buyout of future royalties from the game in perpetuity. He turned it down. That is Ware's account alone: Rockstar did not comment, and Mixmag's report doesn't say whether the offer covered the master, the publishing or both.[^lic-mixmag-2024]

In the same exchange, Simon Raymonde of Cocteau Twins argued the fee was normal for one song, given that the last game carried about 440 songs - his number, and nobody has checked it.[^lic-mixmag-2024] Between them, the two accounts establish one thing with some confidence: at least one GTA VI approach was pitched as a one-off payment rather than a royalty stream. Whether that is typical, and whether it was perpetual for in-game use, is not public.

### Why did songs disappear from GTA Vice City?

Vice City has lost music three times over. The first case is small and muddled. The fan wiki has Lionel Richie's "Running with the Night" on Flash FM surviving only in the original PS2 version and the 2021 Definitive Edition.[^lic-gtawiki-vc] CinemaBlend puts it among the 2012 Steam removals, and VGC's pre-launch list had it missing from the Definitive Edition.[^lic-cinemablend-2012][^lic-vgc-2021-11-10]

The second case was loud. In November 2012 Rockstar pulled the PC version from digital stores, saying: "Due to some music licensing issues, we've had to temporarily remove Vice City PC from digital stores." Press linked it to Michael Jackson's "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'," with CinemaBlend naming Sony Music Entertainment as the likely cause - reported, never confirmed.[^lic-pcgamer-2012]

By December 8, 2012, Vice City was back on Steam without both Michael Jackson songs ("Billie Jean" and "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'"), Ozzy Osbourne's "Bark at the Moon," Kate Bush's "Wow," Herbie Hancock's "Rockit," Afrika Bambaataa's "Looking for the Perfect Beat," "Running with the Night" and a couple of Radio Espantoso tracks. The headline said "nearly 10"; seven are named.[^lic-cinemablend-2012] Some of the same songs were also missing from the mobile and PS Classics re-releases.[^lic-vgc-2021-11-10] The fan wiki brings the Vice City total to nine, adding Tyrone Brunson's "The Smurf" on Wildstyle and Irakere's "Añunga Ñunga" on Radio Espantoso.[^lic-gtawiki-vc]

### How many songs were removed from GTA San Andreas?

The count is 17. A Steam update in November 2014 removed 17 songs from the PC version, along with its high-resolution options, so that it matched the mobile version, which had already lost the same songs. PC Gamer read it as expired licenses; Rockstar said nothing.[^lic-pcgamer-2014][^lic-engadget-2014] The 2014 Xbox 360 "HD" re-release was a port of the mobile version, so it shares the cut soundtrack, and the original Xbox version was taken off Xbox Live.[^lic-pcgamer-2014][^lic-gameranx-2015]

The cuts spread across the dial: Tom Petty's "Runnin' Down a Dream" and Joe Cocker's "Woman to Woman" on K-DST (The Dust), Rage Against the Machine's "Killing in the Name" and Ozzy Osbourne's "Hellraiser" on Radio X, N.W.A's "Express Yourself" on Radio Los Santos, The Gap Band's "You Dropped a Bomb on Me" on Bounce FM, and James Brown's "The Payback" on Master Sounds 98.3.[^lic-gtabase-sa] A second James Brown track, "Funky President," appears in the removed-songs table below only as missing from the 2021 Definitive Edition, though GTABase gives it the same post-2014 removal mark as the other 17. Whether the Definitive Edition brought back Ultramagnetic MCs' "Critical Beatdown," another 2014 cut, depends on which list you read.[^lic-gtabase-sa]

### Why did songs disappear from GTA IV in 2018?

In April 2018 Rockstar said that "due to music licensing restrictions, we are required to remove certain songs," with a large part of Vladivostok FM affected, and that some would be replaced with new songs on that station.[^lic-engadget-2018] The update went live on April 26, 2018. Players on PC, PS3 and Xbox 360 could keep the old soundtrack by not updating, and console disc copies were untouched.[^lic-gameinformer-2018-04-26][^lic-gtawiki-gta4]

How many songs went depends on who is counting and what they count:

| Count | Who | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Over 30 | LevelUp (2026) | Passing figure for all GTA IV losses since launch, no year or list |
| 43 | Game Informer (2018) | Tally of its station-by-station list across 10 stations; the article gives no total |
| 49 | Removed-songs table below | Tracks tagged to the April 2018 update across GTA IV and both episodes; 44 match GTABase's April 26 list, five appear only on the fan wiki |
| Over 50 | Destructoid (2018) | 51 entries, including 19 from Episodes from Liberty City and a previously unused track |

Sources: [^lic-levelup-2026][^lic-gameinformer-2018-04-26][^lic-gtabase-gta4][^lic-gtawiki-gta4][^lic-destructoid-2018]

The spread is about scope, not error: fold in the Episodes from Liberty City tracks and the unused files and the total passes 50. Game Informer's list has Liberty Rock Radio losing eight songs, The Journey six and Vice City FM five.[^lic-gameinformer-2018-04-26] The casualties include The Smashing Pumpkins' "1979" and Stevie Nicks' "Edge of Seventeen" on Liberty Rock Radio and Terry Riley's "A Rainbow in Curved Air" on The Journey. Drive By Audio's "Jailbait" on Liberty City Hardcore is its own case: it was only ever in the original Xbox Live release of The Lost and Damned, and later vanished from every version.[^lic-gtawiki-gta4]

### What happened to Vladivostok FM?

It took the hardest hit. Game Informer's list has 15 removals, 12 from the base game plus three from The Ballad of Gay Tony, and 11 replacement songs, all on that station.[^lic-gameinformer-2018-04-26] The departed include Kino's "Gruppa Krovi," Leningrad's "Nikogo ne Zhalko," Ruslana's "Wild Dances" and, from The Ballad of Gay Tony, David Guetta and Kelly Rowland's "When Love Takes Over."[^lic-gtawiki-gta4]

Rockstar's April 2018 statement singled the station out but gave no reason beyond licensing.[^lic-engadget-2018] The removed-songs table below tags the same 15 Vladivostok FM tracks to the 2018 update.[^lic-gtawiki-gta4] The station's own story is in [Modern radio](/modern-radio/).

### Which songs are missing from the GTA Trilogy Definitive Edition?

At launch in November 2021, VGC counted 20 tracks missing from Vice City and 18 from San Andreas, among them both Michael Jackson songs, "Killing in the Name" and N.W.A's "Express Yourself." Rockstar confirmed the game would be missing some music, and dataminers said the PC files still held the songs that are no longer licensed.[^lic-vgc-2021-11-10]

A day later Rockstar sent VGC an updated list that, VGC said, included practically all the missing songs; it gave no count. The Michael Jackson and Rage Against the Machine songs stayed out.[^lic-vgc-2021-11-11] Nobody stated a final number, but the remaster clearly did not undo the 2012 and 2014 cuts.

### Has GTA V lost any songs?

Two that anyone has documented. The first is Kano's "Can't Hold Back (Your Lovin')" on Space 103.2, and its date is contested. LevelUp puts the loss in 2018;[^lic-levelup-2026] the fan wiki says it went in the Los Santos Tuners update,[^lic-gtawiki-space] which went live on July 20, 2021.[^fc-gameinformer-2021-07-20]

The second is recent. Around September 15, 2026, a hotfix for GTA V Enhanced removed Moloko's "The Time Is Now" (2000) from Non-Stop-Pop FM. Dataminers spotted it, and it was expected to reach consoles. Rockstar said nothing; the press blames an expired license, which nobody has confirmed.[^lic-gtaboom-2026-09][^lic-levelup-2026] The song was never in the original PS3 and Xbox 360 release - it first appeared in the enhanced versions.[^lic-gtawiki-nonstop]

GTA BOOM did the arithmetic: a typical radio license of about 10 years, set against a game roughly 13 years old.[^lic-gtaboom-2026-09] That is press reasoning, not a Rockstar statement.

### Why does a game outliving its licenses matter?

Because GTA V keeps selling. Take-Two's results for the quarter to September 30, 2026, released November 9, put GTA V at nearly 235 million units,[^lic-gamingbolt-2026][^lic-kitguru-2026] counted as **sold-in** units.[^lic-gamesbeat-2026]

| Report | GTA V units | Status |
|---|---|---|
| November 2025 | 225 million | Official, reported |
| May 2026 (CEO on earnings call) | About 230 million | Official, reported |
| August 2026 (quarter to June 30) | Over 230 million, unchanged on the prior quarter | Official, quoted |
| November 2026 (quarter to September 30) | Nearly 235 million | Official, reported |

Sources: [^lic-gtaboom-2026-05][^lic-gamesbeat-2026][^lic-gamingbolt-2026]

That works out to about 5 million between July and September 2026 - subtraction between rounded numbers, so an estimate.[^lic-gamingbolt-2026][^lic-gamesbeat-2026] Every one of those buyers downloads the current build, and the current build carries only the songs whose licenses are still live. A licensed soundtrack on a fixed term was a sensible bet for a game expected to sell for a few years. Applied to a game that moved millions more copies in its 13th year, the same term means the radio slowly thins while the box stays on the shelf. Harmonix's answer for Rock Band 4 was to delist the game.[^lic-avclub-2025] Rockstar's has been to patch songs out and keep selling, with two exceptions: the temporary Vice City delisting of 2012 and the original Xbox San Andreas leaving Xbox Live.[^lic-pcgamer-2012][^lic-pcgamer-2014]

### How does GTA VI's commissioned album change the equation?

GTA VI launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.[^lic-taketwo-2026][^six-keengamer-all-2026] Beside its radio it has GTA VI: The Album, 34 original tracks made with Atlantic Records and announced September 17, 2026, including Keith Richards' cover of Jimmy Reed's "Bright Lights, Big City."[^lic-mbw-2026][^fc-ucr-2026] Justice composed the original score, and the game shipped with 22 radio stations, six of them with artist hosts named in advance.[^lic-stereogum-2026][^lic-keengamer-2026][^six-keengamer-all-2026] All three are covered in [GTA VI](/gta-6/).

What changes is the starting point. Licensed radio rents songs that already exist; a commissioned album and score are music made for the game. The open questions matter more: who owns the album's masters, what terms cover the stations' licensed tracks, and whether anything is perpetual. The Heaven 17 account suggests at least some GTA VI approaches were buyouts, and that is one artist's word.[^lic-mixmag-2024] A cover like the Keith Richards track still involves someone else's composition.

### Is the viral post about GTA song removals accurate?

A widely shared post on November 15, 2026 made five claims about GTA song removals and sales. Here they are against the record:

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 50+ songs pulled from GTA IV in 2018 | Partly supported - depends on counting | Destructoid says over 50 including Episodes and an unused track; Game Informer's list has 43 entries, with no total stated; LevelUp says over 30 since launch[^lic-destructoid-2018][^lic-gameinformer-2018-04-26][^lic-levelup-2026] |
| 17 songs pulled from San Andreas in 2014 | Supported, with nuance | 17 cut from the Steam PC version in November 2014 to match mobile; Rockstar gave no reason at the time[^lic-pcgamer-2014][^lic-engadget-2014] |
| Moloko pulled from GTA V this year | Supported | "The Time Is Now," Non-Stop-Pop FM, hotfix around September 15, 2026; no Rockstar statement[^lic-gtaboom-2026-09] |
| GTA V at about 235 million units | Supported | Take-Two, November 9, 2026: nearly 235 million (official)[^lic-gamingbolt-2026][^lic-taketwo-2026] |
| 5 million sold last quarter | Roughly right, loosely | Over 230 million in August to nearly 235 million in November; rounded figures, so about 5 million is an estimate[^lic-gamesbeat-2026][^lic-gamingbolt-2026] |

The post's direction is right. Its precision is borrowed: the GTA IV number is the high end of three counts, and the quarterly figure is subtraction between two rounded totals.
