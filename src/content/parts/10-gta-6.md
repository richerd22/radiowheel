## GTA VI: the music at launch

**The short version:** Grand Theft Auto VI came out on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after two delays.[^six-engadget-2025][^six-keengamer-all-2026] Launch-day lists count 22 radio stations - 20 music, two talk - with hosts including Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Robyn and two members of Slayer, plus five on-demand podcast series, a series first.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-vgc-podcasts-2026] Rockstar and Atlantic's 34-track original album arrived the same day, and Justice's score follows as its own album on December 11.[^six-complex-album-2026][^six-inven-score-2026] Rockstar has not published full station playlists, so the song lists here come from the press, and they do not fully agree.

### How can you tell GTA 6 music news from a leak?

GTA VI news moves through four channels, and they are not equal. Every claim below carries one of these labels and a date.

| Label | What it means here | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Official** | Announced by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or Atlantic Records | The November 19, 2026 release date |
| **Reported** | A figure or event from a named outlet, not confirmed by Rockstar | Spotify stream spikes after trailers |
| **Leak** | Unreleased information surfacing through footage, a glitch or an anonymous post | The album tracklist photo of November 14, 2026 |
| **Estimate** | A third party's model of numbers the company has not disclosed | Sensor Tower's pre-order count |

Two of the big pre-launch leaks, the 14 station names and the album tracklist, turned out to be right. That did not make them official on the day they surfaced, and the tracker keeps the label each item had when it appeared. Official wording here is as the footnoted press quoted it; The Radio Wheel has no connection to Rockstar, Take-Two or Atlantic.

### What song is in the GTA 6 trailer?

Each trailer has used one song, and each time the song became news on its own.

**Trailer 1** went up on December 4, 2023, a day ahead of Rockstar's plan, after it leaked online hours earlier.[^six-nme-2023] The song is Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," from his 1989 album Full Moon Fever, which Petty's official site noted two days later.[^six-tompetty-2023] It drew 93 million YouTube views in its first day and 255 million by May 2025.[^six-vgc-2025]

**Trailer 2** arrived on Tuesday, May 6, 2025, set to The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together."[^six-vgc-2025] Rockstar said it drew more than 475 million views across platforms in its first day and called it the biggest video launch of all time.[^six-vgc-2025] "Hot Together" is the title track of The Pointer Sisters' 1986 album, so the trailer reached back almost 40 years.[^gf-c-nme-2025]

Between those and the final trailer came **The Extended Look**, a roughly 26-minute preview that premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026.[^six-djmag-2026][^six-redbull-2026] DJ Mag reported songs including Jayda G and Aluna's "Mine O Mine," Depeche Mode's "People Are People" and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light."[^six-djmag-2026] Red Bull counted 14 tracks in total, with Birdman featuring Lil Wayne, Def Leppard, Phil Collins and Cliff Richard among them.[^six-redbull-2026] Most of them turned out to be radio songs. Launch-day lists put "People Are People" and Prefab Sprout's "Cars and Girls" on Vice City FM, Cliff Richard's "Devil Woman" and Phil Collins' "Against All Odds" on Emotion 98.3, and "Pop Bottles" on Dirty South Classics.[^six-keengamer-all-2026]

The **Launch Trailer** - Rockstar's name for it; fans call it Trailer 3 - dropped on October 15, 2026, at 9am ET with no advance notice.[^six-gtaintel-launch-2026] Its song is Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane," featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, a track from the official album.[^six-stereogum-2026]

The two headline trailer songs are a different story. Neither "Love Is a Long Road" nor "Hot Together" sits on a station in the launch-day lists; GTABase files both under a general playlist instead.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-gtabase-radio]

### How much did the trailer songs spike on Spotify?

The press attributes these to Spotify, and they are percentages, not stream counts.

| Song | Trailer | Reported rise | Window | Source chain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tom Petty, "Love Is a Long Road" | Trailer 1, December 2023 | 36,979% (often rounded to 37,000%) | Versus the same period a week earlier | Spotify via IGN, per GameSpot[^six-gamespot-2023] |
| The Pointer Sisters, "Hot Together" | Trailer 2, May 2025 | 182,000% | Two hours after the trailer, versus the same window a week before | Spotify data via The Hollywood Reporter, per Insider Gaming; also reported by TechRadar[^six-techradar-2025][^six-insidergaming-2025] |

Neither outlet published absolute numbers, so a giant percentage can sit on top of a modest baseline. A decades-old album track going from background catalog to a few hundred times its usual plays is still a large effect, and it is the clearest public evidence of what a GTA placement can do for a song before the game is even out.

The Extended Look produced its own set of percentages. InMusic reported on September 2, 2026, rises in Spotify streams since the August 27 premiere, with no end date given: Captain & Tennille's "But I Think It's a Dream" up 130,730% and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light" up 1,070% in global streams, and Depeche Mode's "People Are People" up 190%.[^gf-c-inmusic-2026] RouteNote, citing Spotify data reported by EDM.com, gave the same 1,070% for "Inner Light" in September 2026, plus a 4,155% rise in playlist additions.[^gf-c-routenote-2026] These carry the same caveat as the table: percentages, not stream counts.

Rockstar framed it as the next step for its in-game radio, dynamic score and other musical touchpoints.[^six-mbw-2026]

Six singles came out on announcement day, and a seventh track, Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane," arrived with the Launch Trailer on October 15.[^six-mbw-2026][^six-complex-2026][^six-stereogum-2026] Rockstar published the full tracklist on November 17, 2026.[^six-complex-album-2026]

### Did the GTA 6 liquid vinyl sell out?

Yes, but in September, not November. The album store, run by Atlantic, offered a limited **liquid-filled vinyl** - two transparent magenta discs with blue liquid sealed inside - at $124.98, £108.99 or €123.99, alongside standard vinyl at $49.98, a CD at $19.98, a splatter pressing and exclusives through retailers including Blood Records, Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Target, Urban Outfitters and independent stores.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026][^six-gtabase-album] Those are store prices as fan sites reported them in September 2026.

The liquid edition sold out on the UK store within minutes and on the EU store that evening, per GamingBible; at 21:30 UTC on September 17, 2026, it was still listed on the US store.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026] Resale listings on eBay soon ran roughly £313 to £386, unverified.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026] For how colored and novelty pressings get made and priced, see the sister guide at [groove.cold.fyi](https://groove.cold.fyi/).

### Was the GTA 6 album tracklist leak real?

Yes. Two leaks came before the official list, and both held up.

**The Spotify glitch (October 2026).** The album's Spotify page briefly showed Cardi B at track 19 and Fuerza Regida at track 10, artist names only, before the entries were removed.[^six-allthingshow-2026] Both are in exactly those slots on the official list.[^six-complex-album-2026]

**The full list (November 14, 2026).** A photo said to show the back cover of a vinyl edition surfaced with all 34 tracks.[^six-complex-album-2026] Brazilian outlet Omelete carried the list the same day, crediting a post on X.[^six-omelete-2026] Rockstar published its tracklist three days later, on November 17, and KeenGamer found no additions or cuts between the leak and the album as released.[^six-complex-album-2026][^six-keengamer-all-2026]

For three days the list was a leak that fit every known fact, which is also what a careful fake would have looked like. The tracker above still marks it as a leak on the date it appeared.

### How many songs are on the GTA 6 radio?

Nobody outside Rockstar has a final number. Rockstar has previewed stations but not published full playlists, so the counts come from people listening.

KeenGamer logged 249 songs across 20 music stations on launch day, the same set GTABase carries.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-gtabase-radio] GameRant's list, published the evening before, runs longer on several stations - 59 songs on Flash FM against KeenGamer's 32, 28 on Vice City FM against 13 - and says more will be added as they are found.[^six-gamerant-2026] Where only GameRant lists a song, the station pages here say so. GameRant also files a run of VCRR rap tracks under Radio On-U, the reggae station; those are left on VCRR here.[^six-gamerant-2026][^six-keengamer-all-2026]

Launch-day counts for a game this size run low. The lists will grow as players sit in traffic.

### Was the leaked GTA 6 station list right?

All of it. In August 2026, footage shared by fans showed 14 station names, compiled by Kotaku and reported by German outlet Netzwelt on August 27, 2026: Back Country Radio, The Chamber 106.6, CircoLoco Records Radio (see [CircoLoco Records](/circoloco/)), Cocoteo FM, Dirty South Classics, Emotion 98.3, Kaleidoscope FM, La Ola, Radio ON-U, Stockyard FM, Symphony FM, The Wipe, Worldwide FM and V-Rock.[^six-netzwelt-2026]

Four became official on October 8.[^six-keengamer-2026] The other 10 are all in the launch-day lists, La Ola under the fuller name Radio La Ola.[^six-keengamer-all-2026] What the footage missed is the other eight: AfroBank FM, Flash FM, Vice City FM, VCRR, Honey FM, Gloriana Blues Radio, Leonida Jazz Radio and WKTT.

### Does GTA 6 have talk radio and podcasts?

Both. The talk stations are **WKTT**, the conservative talk station from GTA IV whose letters stand for We Know The Truth, and **The Wipe**, a looser, podcast-style station.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-cultr-2026] The one show named so far is on The Wipe: Ask Dr. Nita, an advice show about sex and relationships hosted by Dr. Nita, voiced by the Brazilian singer Anitta, with actor Barbie Ferreira as a guest and stretches in Portuguese.[^six-infobae-2026] It surfaced the day before launch in a clip released by Brazilian outlet VOXL.[^six-infobae-2026] No launch-day list names WKTT's shows or hosts.[^six-keengamer-all-2026]

The **on-demand podcasts** are new to the series. Rockstar announced them on October 8 as listening for the two leads, Jason and Lucia, in a car or on foot through in-game headphones.[^six-keengamer-2026][^six-gfinity-2026] VGC lists five series at launch:[^six-vgc-podcasts-2026]

- F\*ck, Marry, Murder
- The Safe Trip
- Vice City True Stories
- Drunk Kings
- Final Act

A few are unlocked from the start and more episodes open up as the story goes on.[^six-vgc-podcasts-2026] In a car they sit below the radio wheel: hold left on the D-pad, then push the right stick down.[^six-vgc-podcasts-2026] VGC says some episodes refer back to events and characters from earlier GTA games, and that F\*ck, Marry, Murder may have the most explicit dialogue Rockstar has put in a game.[^six-vgc-podcasts-2026] Nobody has published the podcast casts. For 25 years of what came before, see [Talk radio](/talk-radio/).

### Who composed the GTA 6 score?

Justice. Rockstar named the French duo, Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay, as composers of GTA VI's **original score**, music written for the game rather than licensed, on October 15, 2026, the same day as the Launch Trailer.[^six-stereogum-2026][^six-inven-score-2026]

The score gets its own record. Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score is due on December 11, 2026, three weeks after the game: 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, on streaming, CD and vinyl, including a triple clear-vinyl edition.[^six-inven-score-2026] Two pieces are out already, "Flower in a Hurricane" and "Pusher," featuring Gesaffelstein, which arrived with the album announcement on October 29.[^six-inven-score-2026][^six-complex-album-2026]

Rockstar calls the score adaptive, with compositions "dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with the world."[^six-inven-score-2026] More on how GTA scores work in [Scores](/scores/).

### What is Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival?

A one-night show Rockstar set for November 21, 2026, at Mana Wynwood in Miami, two days after launch.[^six-complex-album-2026] Complex lists Travis Scott, Kodak Black, Cardi B, Don Toliver and Justice as performers, all of them on the album or the score.[^six-complex-album-2026] Tickets went by lottery, which had closed by launch day.[^six-keengamer-all-2026]
