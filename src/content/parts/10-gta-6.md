## GTA VI: the music

**The short version:** Grand Theft Auto VI came out on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after two delays.[^six-engadget-2025][^six-keengamer-all-2026] It has 23 radio stations - 21 music, two talk - and Rockstar's credits list 741 songs on them, plus three DJ mixes and five on-demand podcast series, a series first.[^six-credits-2026] The hosts run from Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and Robyn to Keith Richards, "Weird Al" Yankovic and Jeremy Irons.[^six-credits-2026] Rockstar and Atlantic's 34-track original album arrived the same day, and Justice's score follows as its own album on December 11.[^six-complex-album-2026][^six-inven-score-2026] The station pages here follow the credits.

### How can you tell GTA 6 music news from a leak?

GTA VI news moves through four channels, and they are not equal. Every claim below carries one of these labels and a date.

| Label | What it means here | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Official** | Announced by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or Atlantic Records | The November 19, 2026 release date |
| **Reported** | A figure or event from a named outlet, not confirmed by Rockstar | Spotify stream spikes after trailers |
| **Leak** | Unreleased information surfacing through footage, a glitch or an anonymous post | The album tracklist photo of November 14, 2026 |
| **Estimate** | A third party's model of numbers the company has not disclosed | Sensor Tower's pre-order count |

Two of the big pre-launch leaks, the 14 station names and the album tracklist, turned out to be right. That did not make them official on the day they surfaced, and the tracker keeps the label each item had when it appeared. Official wording here is as the footnoted press quoted it; The Radio Wheel has no connection to Rockstar, Take-Two or Atlantic.

### What song is in the GTA 6 trailer?

Each trailer has used one song, and each time the song became news on its own.

**Trailer 1** went up on December 4, 2023, a day ahead of Rockstar's plan, after it leaked online hours earlier.[^six-nme-2023] The song is Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," from his 1989 album Full Moon Fever, which Petty's official site noted two days later.[^six-tompetty-2023] It drew 93 million YouTube views in its first day and 255 million by May 2025.[^six-vgc-2025]

**Trailer 2** arrived on Tuesday, May 6, 2025, set to The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together."[^six-vgc-2025] Rockstar said it drew more than 475 million views across platforms in its first day and called it the biggest video launch of all time.[^six-vgc-2025] "Hot Together" is the title track of The Pointer Sisters' 1986 album, so the trailer reached back almost 40 years.[^gf-c-nme-2025]

Between those and the final trailer came **The Extended Look**, a roughly 26-minute preview that premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026.[^six-djmag-2026][^six-redbull-2026] DJ Mag reported songs including Jayda G and Aluna's "Mine O Mine," Depeche Mode's "People Are People" and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light."[^six-djmag-2026] Red Bull counted 14 tracks in total, with Birdman featuring Lil Wayne, Def Leppard, Phil Collins and Cliff Richard among them.[^six-redbull-2026] Most of them turned out to be radio songs. Launch-day lists put "People Are People" and Prefab Sprout's "Cars and Girls" on Vice City FM, Cliff Richard's "Devil Woman" and Phil Collins' "Against All Odds" on Emotion 98.3, and "Pop Bottles" on Dirty South Classics.[^six-keengamer-all-2026]

The **Launch Trailer** - Rockstar's name for it; fans call it Trailer 3 - dropped on October 15, 2026, at 9am ET with no advance notice.[^six-gtaintel-launch-2026] Its song is Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane," featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, a track from the official album.[^six-stereogum-2026]

The two headline trailer songs are a different story. Neither is on a station in the press lists, and GTABase files both under a general playlist.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-gtabase-radio] The fan-run GTA Wiki puts "Hot Together" on Vice City FM; nobody has logged "Love Is a Long Road" on the radio yet.[^six-gtawiki-vi]

Neither outlet published absolute numbers, so a giant percentage can sit on top of a modest baseline. The Extended Look produced its own set of percentages. InMusic reported on September 2, 2026, rises in Spotify streams since the August 27 premiere, with no end date given: Captain & Tennille's "But I Think It's a Dream" up 130,730% and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light" up 1,070% in global streams, and Depeche Mode's "People Are People" up 190%.[^gf-c-inmusic-2026] RouteNote, citing Spotify data reported by EDM.com, gave the same 1,070% for "Inner Light" in September 2026, plus a 4,155% rise in playlist additions.[^gf-c-routenote-2026] These carry the same caveat as the table: percentages, not stream counts.

### Did the GTA 6 liquid vinyl sell out?

Yes, but in September, not November. The album store, run by Atlantic, offered a limited **liquid-filled vinyl** - two transparent magenta discs with blue liquid sealed inside - at $124.98, £108.99 or €123.99, alongside standard vinyl at $49.98, a CD at $19.98, a splatter pressing and exclusives through retailers including Blood Records, Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Target, Urban Outfitters and independent stores.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026][^six-gtabase-album] Those are store prices as fan sites reported them in September 2026.

The liquid edition sold out on the UK store within minutes and on the EU store that evening, per GamingBible; at 21:30 UTC on September 17, 2026, it was still listed on the US store.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026] Resale listings on eBay soon ran roughly £313 to £386, unverified.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026] For how colored and novelty pressings get made and priced, see the sister guide at [groove.cold.fyi](https://groove.cold.fyi/).

### Was the GTA 6 album tracklist leak real?

Yes. Two leaks came before the official list, and both held up.

**The Spotify glitch (October 2026).** The album's Spotify page briefly showed Cardi B at track 19 and Fuerza Regida at track 10, artist names only, before the entries were removed.[^six-allthingshow-2026] Both are in exactly those slots on the official list.[^six-complex-album-2026]

**The full list (November 14, 2026).** A photo said to show the back cover of a vinyl edition surfaced with all 34 tracks.[^six-complex-album-2026] Brazilian outlet Omelete carried the list the same day, crediting a post on X.[^six-omelete-2026] Rockstar published its tracklist three days later, on November 17, and KeenGamer found no additions or cuts between the leak and the album as released.[^six-complex-album-2026][^six-keengamer-all-2026]

For three days the list was a leak that fit every known fact, which is also what a careful fake would have looked like. The tracker above still marks it as a leak on the date it appeared.

### How many songs are on the GTA 6 radio?

The credits list 741 songs across the 21 music stations.[^six-credits-2026] Flash FM is the biggest at 80, then Vice City FM at 71 and V-Rock at 70; Symphony FM and Radio On-U are the smallest at 16 each.[^six-credits-2026]

Three mixes sit on top of that. Bodega the DJ has a 45-song mix and DJ Stevie J a 24-song one, both of which AllHipHop places on VCRR, the rap station Stevie J co-hosts.[^six-credits-2026][^six-allhiphop-2026] The third is [The Sniper of La Perle](/stations/gta6-the-sniper-of-la-perle/), 22 songs hosted by Kodak Black and presented by the Haitian-American producer Michaël Brun, mixing Kodak's own tracks with Haitian acts like Barikad Crew, Carimi and T-Vice.[^six-credits-2026][^six-allhiphop-2026] It is the game's other hidden playlist, unlocked by scanning four posters; the locations are below.[^six-pcgamer-playlists-2026] With the mixes, the count is 832 credited plays of 829 different songs.[^six-credits-2026]

The station pages on this site now follow the credits, not the press. On launch day the best lists were far short: KeenGamer had 249 songs, and GameRant and the fan wiki added about 120 more between them.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-gamerant-2026][^six-gtawiki-vi] Almost all of those turned out to be right. Fifteen did not make the credits and have been dropped here, among them two Taylor Swift songs, Mariah Carey's "Fantasy," Britney Spears' "Gimme More" and Metallica's "Battery" (The Chamber has "Master of Puppets" and "Seek & Destroy" instead).[^six-gamerant-2026][^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-credits-2026] A handful had been filed on the wrong station.

The credits also list music that is not on a station: five songs played by a bar band at a place called the Rusty Anchor (Hall & Oates, Foreigner, Loverboy, Steve Miller Band and A Flock of Seagulls covers), and 14 "additional songs" that include Korn's "Freak on a Leash," Linkin Park's "In the End," Barber's Adagio for Strings and a Travis Scott track called "Burn This Shit Down."[^six-credits-2026] Where each one plays is for players to find.

### Does GTA 6 have talk radio and podcasts?

Both, and the credits name everyone on them.[^six-credits-2026]

**The Wipe**, credited as The Wipe Talk Radio, has three shows. Ask Dr. Nita is an advice show about sex and relationships hosted by Dr. Nita, voiced by the Brazilian singer Anitta, with actor Barbie Ferreira in the cast and stretches in Portuguese.[^six-infobae-2026][^six-credits-2026] El Otro Lado is hosted by Enrique "El Enigma" Romero, played by John Leguizamo. She Got Assets is hosted by Tish, played by Janelle James, who also has a writing credit.[^six-credits-2026]

**WKTT**, the conservative talk station from GTA IV whose letters stand for We Know The Truth, has two.[^six-keengamer-all-2026] Jason Sudeikis plays Richard Bastion on The Richard Bastion Show. The comedian Stavros Halkias plays Dominic "The Brick" Brizzolato on Hit the Bricks, with Sarah Sherman and Carl Tart among the callers.[^six-credits-2026]

The **on-demand podcasts** are new to the series. Rockstar announced them on October 8 as listening for the two leads, Jason and Lucia, in a car or on foot through in-game headphones.[^six-keengamer-2026][^six-gfinity-2026] There are five series:[^six-credits-2026]

### Which musicians are in GTA 6?

A lot of them, and not only on the radio. Billboard counted the music cameos on November 21 and ran past 50 names.[^six-billboard-cameos-2026]

In the story cast, Sexyy Red and Kodak Black play themselves. Bad Bunny has a second role beyond his DJ, a character named Mitico Mario. The producer Scott Storch plays The Pianist, the soca star Machel Montano plays Kenton Rees, and Bodega the DJ, DJ Stevie J and Freddie Gibbs all have parts.[^six-credits-2026][^six-billboard-cameos-2026] Billboard also reports an uncredited Juice WRLD easter egg, a non-player character, and a D'Angelo song it calls unreleased; the credits list it on Worldwide FM as "Kamikazi."[^six-billboard-cameos-2026][^six-credits-2026]

The game has its own rappers. **Real Dimez** is the in-game duo of Bae-Luxe and Roxy Rashad, played by Hood Brat and Krissy Celess, and the credits give them five songs made for the game, including "Cooties," a "Cooties" remix with Sexyy Red, and "Money Don't Lie," produced by Take a Daytrip.[^six-credits-2026][^six-billboard-cameos-2026] **DWNPLY** is Denzel Curry, in the main cast and on three songs, one of them produced by Murda Beatz.[^six-credits-2026] A Pawsa remix of the Real Dimez song "FTH" is on The Vault Radio.[^six-credits-2026]

Zack Fox is in the radio ads. The credits list the comedian and rapper in the ad cast, among the 34 people who made original music for the ads, and under additional writing; Billboard says he worked on the TV ads as well.[^six-credits-2026][^six-billboard-cameos-2026] "Weird Al" Yankovic, Lil Duval and DJ Pooh wrote ad music too, and the ads sample five records, among them Juvenile's "Slow Motion" and War's "Low Rider."[^six-credits-2026]

Tommy Richman's "Million Dollar Baby" is on Stockyard FM.[^six-credits-2026] The credits do not list him in the cast, and they do not list Riff Raff anywhere under that name.[^six-credits-2026]

### Who composed the GTA 6 score?

Justice. Rockstar named the French duo, Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay, as composers of GTA VI's **original score**, music written for the game rather than licensed, on October 15, 2026, the same day as the Launch Trailer.[^six-stereogum-2026][^six-inven-score-2026]

The credits show how much help they had. The main score is "composed, produced, performed by Justice," featuring Boys Noize, Gesaffelstein, King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard and Phil Collen.[^six-credits-2026] The British duo Overmono has a separate credit for additional score. Then comes a supplemental score from 17 acts, including 100 gecs, A. G. Cook, Bobby Krlic, Boys Noize, Idles, DJ Python, Nick León, Oh No and Take a Daytrip.[^six-credits-2026] Oh No was one of the four composers of GTA V's score.

Four Justice songs with guest singers are credited with the score: "Starting From Zero" with Michael McDonald, "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard, "The Night's Coming" with Djo, and "My Only Vice" with The Weeknd.[^six-credits-2026] The first two are also on The Album.[^six-complex-album-2026]

The score gets its own record. Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score is due on December 11, 2026, three weeks after the game: 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, on streaming, CD and vinyl, including a triple clear-vinyl edition.[^six-inven-score-2026] Two pieces came out before launch, "Flower in a Hurricane" and "Pusher," featuring Gesaffelstein, which arrived with the album announcement on October 29.[^six-inven-score-2026][^six-complex-album-2026]

Rockstar calls the score adaptive, with compositions "dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with the world."[^six-inven-score-2026] The credits back that up with job titles: Brady Custis is "Music Director & Adaptive Score Composer."[^six-credits-2026] Ivan Pavlovich is credited as Vice President of Music and Tony Mesones as Director: Soundtrack & Artist Relations, and one line covers the paperwork: "All music licensing by DMG Clearances."[^six-credits-2026] More on how GTA scores work in [Scores](/scores/).

### What was Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival?

A one-night show Rockstar put on November 21, 2026, at Mana Wynwood in Miami, two days after launch.[^six-complex-album-2026] Complex lists Travis Scott, Kodak Black, Cardi B, Don Toliver and Justice as performers, all of them on the album or the score.[^six-complex-album-2026] Tickets went by lottery, which had closed by launch day.[^six-keengamer-all-2026] Rockstar has published no recap, set lists or attendance. Secondhand reports have Cardi B performing her album track "Don't Chart" and Travis Scott and Don Toliver closing the night.[^six-stupiddope-2026]

### How many copies did GTA 6 sell?

Take-Two put out numbers the day after launch: 32.5 million units sold before release, which it calls a record across all platforms, and $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales, "based on company estimates through end of day November 19th worldwide."[^six-taketwo-dayone-2026] GTA V's first day in 2013 was about $800 million.[^six-reuters-launch-2026]

The album had a day of its own. Push Square, citing the chart account Pop Base, reports 29.85 million Spotify streams on its first day, which would be the biggest opening day for a soundtrack on the service; Spotify has not put out the figure itself.[^six-pushsquare-2026]

Before launch, Take-Two gave no number. Officially, Zelnick said in November 2026: "with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year."[^six-finviz-2026]

The figures in circulation are **estimates** from Sensor Tower, an analytics firm that models digital sales. They are not company data and they exclude physical copies.
