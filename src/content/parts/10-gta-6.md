## GTA VI: what is known about the music

**The short version:** As of November 15, 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI is set to launch on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after two delays.[^six-engadget-2025][^six-finviz-2026] Rockstar and Atlantic Records have announced a 34-track original album out the same day, Justice are composing the score, and six radio stations have been officially named with hosts including Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Robyn and two members of Slayer.[^six-mbw-2026][^six-stereogum-2026][^six-keengamer-2026] Everything else in circulation - a full album tracklist, a longer station list, pre-order totals - is either a leak or an estimate, and this page labels each one.

[[MODULE:gta6]]

### How can you tell GTA 6 music news from a leak?

GTA VI news moves through four channels, and they are not equal. This page sorts every claim into one of them, with a date.

| Label | What it means here | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Official** | Announced by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or Atlantic Records | The November 19, 2026 release date |
| **Reported** | A figure or event from a named outlet, not confirmed by Rockstar | Spotify stream spikes after trailers |
| **Leak** | Unreleased information surfacing through footage, a glitch or an anonymous post | The 14 station names from August 2026 footage |
| **Estimate** | A third party's model of numbers the company has not disclosed | Sensor Tower's pre-order count |

A leak that later matches an official announcement is still a leak for everything it said that has not been announced. Consistency with known facts is not confirmation. Nothing on this page comes from Rockstar directly to this guide - official wording is as quoted by the press outlets footnoted, and this guide has no connection to Rockstar, Take-Two or Atlantic.

### What song is in the GTA 6 trailer?

Each trailer has used one song, and each time the song became news on its own.

**Trailer 1** went up on December 4, 2023, a day ahead of Rockstar's plan, after it leaked online hours earlier.[^six-nme-2023] The song is Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," from his 1989 album Full Moon Fever, which Petty's official site noted two days later.[^six-tompetty-2023] Per VGC, the trailer drew 93 million YouTube views in its first day; by May 2025 it had been viewed 255 million times.[^six-vgc-2025]

**Trailer 2** arrived on Tuesday, May 6, 2025, set to The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together."[^six-vgc-2025] Rockstar said it drew more than 475 million views across platforms in its first day and called it the biggest video launch of all time.[^six-vgc-2025] "Hot Together" is the title track of The Pointer Sisters' 1986 album, so the trailer reached back almost 40 years.[^gf-c-nme-2025]

Between those and the final trailer came **The Extended Look**, a roughly 26-minute preview that premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026.[^six-djmag-2026][^six-redbull-2026] DJ Mag reported songs including Jayda G and Aluna's "Mine O Mine," Depeche Mode's "People Are People" and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light."[^six-djmag-2026] Red Bull counted 14 tracks in total, with Birdman featuring Lil Wayne, Def Leppard, Phil Collins and Cliff Richard among them.[^six-redbull-2026] Rockstar has not said any of these are on the in-game radio. A trailer edit is a trailer edit.

The **Launch Trailer** - Rockstar's name for it; fans call it Trailer 3 - dropped on October 15, 2026, at 9am ET with no advance notice.[^six-gtaintel-launch-2026] Its song is Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane," featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, a track from the official album.[^six-stereogum-2026]

### How much did the trailer songs spike on Spotify?

These are reported figures, attributed to Spotify by the outlets below, and they are percentages, not stream counts.

| Song | Trailer | Reported rise | Window | Source chain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tom Petty, "Love Is a Long Road" | Trailer 1, December 2023 | 36,979% (often rounded to 37,000%) | Versus the same period a week earlier | Spotify via IGN, per GameSpot[^six-gamespot-2023] |
| The Pointer Sisters, "Hot Together" | Trailer 2, May 2025 | 182,000% | Two hours after the trailer, versus the same window a week before | Spotify data via The Hollywood Reporter, per Insider Gaming; also reported by TechRadar[^six-techradar-2025][^six-insidergaming-2025] |

Neither outlet published absolute numbers, so a giant percentage can sit on top of a modest baseline. A decades-old album track going from background catalog to a few hundred times its usual plays is still a large effect, and it is the clearest public evidence of what a GTA placement can do for a song before the game is even out.

The Extended Look produced its own set of percentages. InMusic reported on September 2, 2026, rises in Spotify streams since the August 27 premiere, with no end date given: Captain & Tennille's "But I Think It's a Dream" up 130,730% and Elderbrook and Bob Moses' "Inner Light" up 1,070% in global streams, and Depeche Mode's "People Are People" up 190%.[^gf-c-inmusic-2026] RouteNote, citing Spotify data reported by EDM.com, gave the same 1,070% for "Inner Light" in September 2026, plus a 4,155% rise in playlist additions.[^gf-c-routenote-2026] These carry the same caveat as the table: percentages, not stream counts.

### What is GTA VI: The Album?

**GTA VI: The Album** is a 34-track original album made by Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records, announced by both on September 17, 2026, and set for release on November 19, 2026, the same day as the game.[^six-mbw-2026] Streaming pre-saves and vinyl and CD pre-orders opened the day it was announced.[^six-mbw-2026] (A **pre-save** adds an unreleased album to your streaming library automatically on release day.) Rockstar framed it as the next step for its in-game radio, dynamic score and other musical touchpoints, per Music Business Worldwide.[^six-mbw-2026] Rockstar has not said which album tracks play on which stations.

Six singles came out on announcement day.[^six-mbw-2026][^six-complex-2026] The album's pre-save page listed them at positions 2, 4, 5, 8, 11 and 32, per GTA Intel, which notes this is page metadata rather than a Rockstar announcement and could change:[^fc-gtaintel-order-2026]

- Yung Lean, "That's It," featuring Future and Metro Boomin
- Travis Scott, "RHYNO," produced by Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo of Daft Punk
- CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. and Étienne de Crécy, "Sexy Magic"
- Morgan Wallen, "Last Thing You Need"
- Rauw Alejandro, "Macacoa 2000"
- Keith Richards, "Bright Lights, Big City," a cover of the Jimmy Reed song[^fc-ucr-2026]

A seventh official track arrived with the Launch Trailer on October 15: Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane."[^six-stereogum-2026] That makes seven of 34 tracks officially named as of November 15, 2026. The other 27 are known only through leaks, covered below.

Atlantic's president said in an Instagram post that the label had spent "two years of our lives" on the album with Rockstar, as reported by fan site GTA BOOM.[^six-gtaboom-2026]

One small discrepancy: most coverage gives November 19 as the album's release date, while fan site GTABase lists November 20.[^six-gtabase-album] GTA Intel reports that physical editions ship November 20, which is the likely source of the difference.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026]

### Did the GTA 6 liquid vinyl sell out?

Yes, but in September, not November. The album store, run by Atlantic, offered a limited **liquid-filled vinyl** - two transparent magenta discs with blue liquid sealed inside - at $124.98, £108.99 or €123.99, alongside standard vinyl at $49.98, a CD at $19.98, a splatter pressing and exclusives through retailers including Blood Records, Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Target, Urban Outfitters and independent stores.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026][^six-gtabase-album] These are store prices as reported by fan sites in September 2026.

Per GTA Intel, citing GamingBible, the liquid edition sold out on the UK store within minutes and on the EU store that evening; GTA Intel's own check at 21:30 UTC on September 17, 2026 found it still listed on the US store.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026] GTA Intel credits wider sellout reporting to GamingBible and Dexerto, and passes on eBay resale listings of roughly £313 to £386, which it does not verify.[^six-gtaintel-album-2026] For the economics of colored and novelty pressings, the sister guide https://groove.cold.fyi/ covers how vinyl gets made and priced.

### Is the GTA 6 album tracklist leak real?

Unconfirmed. Two leaks matter here.

**The Spotify glitch (October 2026, leak).** The album's Spotify page briefly showed Cardi B at track 19 and Fuerza Regida at track 10, artist names only, before the entries were removed, per All Things How.[^six-allthingshow-2026] Rockstar, Atlantic and Spotify have not commented.

**The full list (November 14, 2026, leak).** Brazilian outlet Omelete updated its album article with a 34-track list it says was leaked through a post on X; the leaker is not named and the list is unverified.[^six-omelete-2026] Fan site GTABase carries the same list without calling it a leak.[^six-gtabase-album] Per that leaked list, the album opens with Justice featuring Michael McDonald and includes, among others, Playboi Carti, Dua Lipa, Young Thug, Cardi B, JENNIE, Zara Larsson, Camila Cabello, Lana Del Rey, Burna Boy, Gunna, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, Tove Lo, Arca, Rina Sawayama, Kali Uchis and Omar Apollo.[^six-omelete-2026]

The list is internally consistent: in its running order the six singles sit at the same positions as on the pre-save page, the Launch Trailer song is sixth, and Fuerza Regida and Cardi B are 10th and 19th, matching the Spotify glitch.[^six-omelete-2026][^fc-gtaintel-order-2026] That is what a real list would look like. It is also what a careful fake built from public information would look like. Until Rockstar or Atlantic publish the tracklist, every name beyond the seven official tracks is a leak.

### What radio stations are in GTA 6?

On October 8, 2026, Rockstar named the first six stations and their hosts through the Newswire and previews on the official GTA VI music page.[^six-keengamer-2026][^six-shacknews-2026] A **radio host persona** here means an artist voicing a station's DJ character, often under a stage name.

| Station | Format | Hosts (official) |
|---|---|---|
| Cocoteo FM | Modern Latin: bachata, merengue, dembow, reggaeton | El Martillo (Bad Bunny) and La Gata (RaiNao) |
| Back Country Radio | Renegade country | MW (Morgan Wallen) and Delta Dawne (Lana Del Rey), with roving reporter Mud Dog Mike; Jeremy Dufrene also appears on air |
| AfroBank FM | African classics, amapiano, 3-step | Burna (Burna Boy) and Palmsy (Palms Trax) |
| The Chamber 106.6 | Metal from the 1980s on | DJ Kerry (Kerry King) and DJ Tom (Tom Araya) of Slayer |
| Flash FM | Pop and dance | Robyn and Alex (Alex Consani) |
| Dirty South Classics | Regional rap | Trick (Trick Daddy) and Trina |

Sources for the table: KeenGamer, Shacknews and Gfinity, all October 8, 2026.[^six-keengamer-2026][^six-shacknews-2026][^six-gfinity-2026]

Flash FM is a returning name from GTA Vice City, which fits a game set back in Vice City, GTA's fictional Miami, now inside the fictional state of **Leonida**.[^six-keengamer-2026][^six-mbw-2026][^six-complex-2026] Rockstar also listed further categories without station names - Classic Rock, Modern Hip-Hop, Dance, '80s, Electronic, Blues, Ballads and Talk Radio[^six-keengamer-2026] - and described the set as the most diverse music in any GTA game.[^six-shacknews-2026] New for the series: **on-demand podcasts** for the two leads, Jason and Lucia, playable in a car or on foot through in-game headphones.[^six-keengamer-2026][^six-gfinity-2026]

Before October, the only official hint was indirect: a V-Rock T-shirt on Jason on the official site, which Red Bull noted in September 2026.[^six-redbull-2026] A shirt is not a station announcement. This guide found no further official station reveals between October 8 and November 15, 2026.

### What is the leaked GTA 6 station list?

In August 2026, footage shared by fans showed 14 station names, compiled by Kotaku and reported by German outlet Netzwelt on August 27, 2026: Back Country Radio, The Chamber 106.6, CircoLoco Records Radio (see [CircoLoco Records](/circoloco/)), Cocoteo FM, Dirty South Classics, Emotion 98.3, Kaleidoscope FM, La Ola, Radio ON-U, Stockyard FM, Symphony FM, The Wipe, Worldwide FM and V-Rock.[^six-netzwelt-2026]

Four of those were later made official on October 8: Back Country Radio, The Chamber 106.6, Cocoteo FM and Dirty South Classics.[^six-keengamer-2026] The other 10 remain leaks as of November 15, 2026. Two official stations, AfroBank FM and Flash FM, were not on the leaked list at all - a reminder that leaked footage shows a moment in development, not a finished game.

### Who is composing the GTA 6 score?

Justice. Rockstar named the French duo as composers of GTA VI's **original score**, music written for the game rather than licensed, on October 15, 2026, the same day as the Launch Trailer.[^six-stereogum-2026] Rockstar's album announcement had already described a **dynamic score**, its term for music that shifts with play.[^six-mbw-2026] [The scores section](/scores/) covers what is known about it.
