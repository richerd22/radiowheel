## CircoLoco Records: Rockstar's label with a Monday party

**The short version:** CircoLoco Records is a record label run as a partnership between CircoLoco, the Monday party at DC10 in Ibiza, and Rockstar Games, announced on May 24, 2021.[^clr-mixmag-2021][^clr-rollingstone-2021] It opened with *Monday Dreamin'*, a 20-track compilation whose four EPs became hidden collectibles in GTA Online in July 2021, and since 2024 it has turned into a chart label for house and tech house.[^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer][^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-beatportal-2026] A "CircoLoco Records Radio" station for GTA VI exists only in a leak; Rockstar did not name one in its October 2026 station reveal.[^clr-weraveyou-2026-leak][^clr-gamesradar-2026]

### What is CircoLoco, and what is DC10?

**DC10** is a club on the Carretera de Salinas in Ibiza. Per Wikipedia, it started in the 1990s as a music bar licensed for 80 people, opened by the brothers Deogracias and Antonio Lara Moreno.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10] In 1999 two Italian promoters, Antonio Carbonaro and Andrea Pelino, joined as partners and created **CircoLoco**, which began as a free Monday-morning after-party.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10] Wikipedia now gives the venue a capacity of 1,500 across three rooms (La Terrace, The Main Room and The Open Air Garden), and reports it ranked 19th in DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs in 2020.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10]

CircoLoco dates its own start to "The Monday Morning Sessions" in 1999.[^clr-nss-2021] In a 2021 interview Carbonaro called it the longest-running electronic music brand among its peers, which is his claim rather than a measured one.[^clr-nss-2021] He also founded No Soul For Sale, which began as the slogan for CircoLoco's 20th year and became its own platform.[^clr-nss-2021] For the 25th anniversary in 2024, a "DC10 Ibiza CircoLoco Greatest Hits" release marked the date, per Wikipedia.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10]

The regulars Beatport names read like a who's-who of the Monday floor: Seth Troxler, The Martinez Brothers, Tania Vulcano, Loco Dice and Luciano.[^clr-beatportal-2026] For how a party like this runs as a business at night, the sister guide https://lastcall.cold.fyi/ covers nightlife.

### When did CircoLoco Records launch?

Rockstar Games and CircoLoco announced **CircoLoco Records** on May 24, 2021.[^clr-mixmag-2021][^clr-gamesradar-2021] The plan was a weekly run of EPs taken from the album *Monday Dreamin'*, with Seth Troxler's "Lumartes" already out as a single.[^clr-djmag-2021] The first EP was due June 4 and the full compilation July 9, 2021.[^clr-mixmag-2021][^clr-djmag-2021]

Carbonaro traces the idea to his friendship with Sam Houser and a joint CircoLoco and Rockstar event in Miami in October 2019; the label itself was put together during the pandemic.[^clr-nss-2021] Beatport frames the 2021 launch as a way to reach the community while clubs were shut for COVID-19.[^clr-beatportal-2026] A label is a reasonable thing to build when the dance floor is closed and the game is not.

Per the GTA wiki, "CircoLoco Records" is the trade name of an entity called Rockstar Records LLC, founded May 24, 2021, and RockstarINTEL uses the same name.[^clr-gtawiki-label][^clr-rsintel-crl010] No company filing was checked for this guide, so treat the entity name as reported.

### Does Rockstar own CircoLoco Records?

It is a partnership. Rolling Stone reported at launch that the independent label was a partnership between the two companies and would stay separate from both companies' businesses for now.[^clr-rollingstone-2021] Neither Rolling Stone nor Music Business Worldwide gave an ownership split.[^clr-rollingstone-2021][^clr-mbw-2021]

The launch quotes came from both sides. For Rockstar, founder Sam Houser and director of music Ivan Pavlovich; Pavlovich told Rolling Stone, "Music's been part of our DNA since day one."[^clr-rollingstone-2021] Houser's stated aim was to "find new ways to bring the very best underground music to the widest audience possible."[^clr-gamesradar-2021] For CircoLoco, Nick Benedetti spoke for the company, and the 2021 artists were picked jointly, all of them CircoLoco alumni.[^clr-rollingstone-2021]

In practice, the division of labor looks like this:

- **Rockstar's side:** placement in its games (the Media Player sticks, a CircoLoco tee in Los Santos Tuners) and the "CircoLoco / Rockstar Games presents" credit on releases.[^clr-gtawiki-label][^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-rsintel-category]
- **CircoLoco's side, per Beatport:** the A&R, the DC10 floor where tracks get tested, and the visual identity. Beatport describes Rockstar as partner and backer.[^clr-beatportal-2026]

### How does the label pick its records?

**A&R** (artists and repertoire) is the job of finding artists and choosing what gets released. Carbonaro told Beatport in 2026 that there is no fixed formula: signings go on instinct and on whether an act fits CircoLoco.[^clr-beatportal-2026] His test, in four words: "It has to feel honest."[^clr-beatportal-2026]

What that means in practice, per the same piece:[^clr-beatportal-2026]

- artists who shape a scene rather than fit into one
- a mix of new and established names
- tracks tested on the dance floor in front of the party's crowd
- a look that is part of the record: a blood-red palette that echoes the club, and surreal digital art for *Monday Dreamin'*

The catalog shows how that taste has moved. The 2021 compilation leans on long-time CircoLoco names such as Luciano, Carl Craig and Moodymann; from 2024 on the releases lean toward chart tech house and house, with Prospa, PAWSA, Jamback and Michael Bibi, and Prospa as the most frequent artist.[^clr-deejay-label][^clr-bandcamp-music][^clr-rsintel-category][^clr-weraveyou-2026-mystery] That last point is this guide's reading of the release list, not a statement from the label.

### Who made "Positive" on CircoLoco Records?

Jamback, a Dutch producer, alone. "Positive" is his CircoLoco Records debut, released November 7, 2025.[^clr-bandcamp-positive][^clr-crack-2025] Bandcamp, Crack Magazine, Chromatic Club and Beatport all credit Jamback with no co-artist, so if you remember a featured name on it, the sources do not.[^clr-bandcamp-positive][^clr-crack-2025][^clr-chromatic-2026][^clr-beatportal-2026] Crack reports the track was shaped by the loss of his mother.[^clr-crack-2025]

Then it parked at the top of Beatport. Beatport's own editorial reported in May 2026 that "Positive" spent 64 days at No. 1, the longest run in the store's history, passing FISHER's "Losing It" at 62 days in 2018.[^clr-beatportal-2026][^clr-chromatic-2026] As of November 2026, technoairlines' 12-month chart-points ranking puts "Positive" first in the label's catalog (reported).[^clr-technoairlines]

The catalog number is probably CLR021, but the only evidence is a press image filename, so it is marked inferred in the table above.[^clr-crack-2025]

### What is Prospa's "Don't Stop"?

"Don't Stop" is a Prospa single released on CircoLoco Records on April 11, 2025, catalog CLR015 (Discogs lists it as CLR015BP).[^clr-discogs-dontstop][^clr-rsintel-crl015] It builds on a hook from Somethin' For The People's 1997 song "My Love Is the Shhh!".[^clr-ravejungle-2025] RaveJungle ran its release-day story on April 12, 2025; Discogs and RockstarINTEL both say April 11.[^clr-ravejungle-2025][^clr-discogs-dontstop][^clr-rsintel-category]

Prospa are Harvey Blumler and Gosha Smith, a duo from Leeds.[^clr-crack-2026] By the time of "Don't Stop" they were already the label's busiest act, after "If You Want My Loving" (CRL 010) and "This Rhythm" (CRL 013); RaveJungle reported more than 7 million streams for "This Rhythm" in 2025.[^clr-rsintel-crl010][^clr-rsintel-crl013][^clr-ravejungle-2025] In technoairlines' November 2026 ranking, "Don't Stop" sits second in the catalog (reported).[^clr-technoairlines]

### What is "Free Your Mind" by Prospa and Cloonee?

Two things share the name. **"Free Your Mind"** is the title track with Cloonee, and Beatport-derived charts also credit Sybil.[^clr-crack-2026][^clr-technoairlines] ***Free Your Mind*** is Prospa's debut album, released on CircoLoco Records on June 5, 2026; deejay.de lists the 2 x 12-inch vinyl (CLR019V) for July 16, 2026.[^clr-crack-2026][^clr-deejay-fym]

The album features Josh Baker and RAHH on "You Don't Own Me" and Kosmo Kint on "Love Songs".[^clr-crack-2026] In May 2026, "Baby" and "Free Your Mind" held No. 1 and No. 2 on the Beatport Top 100, per Beatport.[^clr-beatportal-2026] The same month Beatport named CircoLoco Records its Label of the Month, which is where most of the A&R detail in this section comes from.[^clr-beatportal-2026] Technoairlines puts the title track fifth in the label's 12-month ranking (reported, November 2026).[^clr-technoairlines]

### How do I get the CircoLoco Records music in GTA Online?

Through the **Media Player**, the radio-wheel slot for collectible Media Sticks, added with Los Santos Tuners on July 20, 2021; [the GTA Online section](/online/) explains how it works.[^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer][^clr-gtabase-mediaplayer] Per Rockstar's update notes as reprinted by Worthplaying, *Monday Dreamin'* plays at the LS Car Meet, and four EP sticks are hidden around the city; the GTA wiki names them Black, Blue, Violet and Green.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer] Find all four and you unlock Seth Troxler's **CLR Launch Party** mix and a CircoLoco tee.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-07]

Beatport says Troxler built that mix by reworking the *Monday Dreamin'* stems, and that it debuted with Los Santos Tuners.[^clr-beatportal-2026] Sources disagree on its length: the Rockstar notes and GTA Base say it reworks all 20 album tracks, while the GTA wiki lists 16.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-gtabase-mediaplayer][^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

The NEZ EP was spread through the game before release. "You Wanna?" came as a Media Stick in The Contract (December 2021); ScHoolboy Q's "Let's Get It", produced by NEZ, premiered on Radio Los Santos; and "Freaks" with Moodymann and Gangsta Boo was heard first in the Los Santos Tuners trailer and sits in Kenny's Backyard Boogie mix.[^clr-sportskeeda-contract][^clr-worthplaying-2021-12][^clr-sportskeeda-2022] Rockstar said all three would be heard only in GTA Online until the EP came out, which it did on February 25, 2022.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-12][^clr-sportskeeda-2022]

That is where the confirmed trail ends. RockstarINTEL's release posts from CRL 004 through CRL 019 do not mention GTA Online, and no source found for this guide places a post-2022 CircoLoco Records release in the game.[^clr-rsintel-category]

### Is the Music Locker part of CircoLoco Records?

No source connects them. The Music Locker is the club under The Diamond Casino added with The Cayo Perico Heist in December 2020, with Moodymann, Keinemusik and Palms Trax as residents - five months before the label was announced.[^clr-djmag-2020] The overlap is artists, not paperwork: GamesRadar noted at the label's launch that GTA Online's nightclubs already featured CircoLoco alumni such as The Blessed Madonna, Dixon and Solomun, and Moodymann later turned up on the *Monday Dreamin'* Green EP.[^clr-gamesradar-2021][^clr-edmtunes-2021] Calling the Music Locker a CircoLoco Records venue would be an inference this guide does not make.

### Will GTA VI have a CircoLoco Records radio station?

Not as of Rockstar's own announcements. On August 28, 2026, We Rave You reported a leak: a clip said to come from an unfinished GTA VI build shows a station called "CircoLoco Records Radio".[^clr-weraveyou-2026-leak] We Rave You could not name the clip's source and said neither Rockstar nor CircoLoco had confirmed it.[^clr-weraveyou-2026-leak] GTAbase lists a "CircoLoco Records Radio" page for GTA VI as if it were settled, but cites no primary source for the station and names no tracks, so it adds no evidence.[^clr-gtabase-gta6]

Rockstar's own reveal of six stations on October 8, 2026 did not include a CircoLoco station, per GamesRadar.[^clr-gamesradar-2026] The [GTA VI section](/gta-6/) has the official list and the full leaked list. So, as of November 2026: leaked, not announced, not denied.
