## CircoLoco Records: Rockstar's label with a Monday party

**The short version:** CircoLoco Records is a record label run as a partnership between CircoLoco, the Monday party at DC10 in Ibiza, and Rockstar Games, announced on May 24, 2021.[^clr-mixmag-2021][^clr-rollingstone-2021] It opened with *Monday Dreamin'*, a 20-track compilation whose four EPs became hidden collectibles in GTA Online in July 2021, and since 2024 it has turned into a chart label for house and tech house.[^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer][^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-beatportal-2026] A "CircoLoco Records Radio" station for GTA VI exists only in a leak; Rockstar did not name one in its October 2026 station reveal.[^clr-weraveyou-2026-leak][^clr-gamesradar-2026]

### What is CircoLoco, and what is DC10?

**DC10** is a club on the Carretera de Salinas in Ibiza. It started in the 1990s as a music bar licensed for 80 people, opened by the brothers Deogracias and Antonio Lara Moreno.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10] In 1999 two Italian promoters, Antonio Carbonaro and Andrea Pelino, joined as partners and created **CircoLoco**, which began as a free Monday-morning after-party.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10] The venue now holds 1,500 across three rooms (La Terrace, The Main Room and The Open Air Garden), and ranked 19th in DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs in 2020.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10]

CircoLoco dates its own start to "The Monday Morning Sessions" in 1999.[^clr-nss-2021] Carbonaro calls it the longest-running electronic music brand among its peers, a claim nobody has measured.[^clr-nss-2021] He also founded No Soul For Sale, which began as the slogan for CircoLoco's 20th year and became its own platform.[^clr-nss-2021] The 25th anniversary in 2024 got a "DC10 Ibiza CircoLoco Greatest Hits" release.[^clr-wikipedia-dc10]

The Monday regulars include Seth Troxler, The Martinez Brothers, Tania Vulcano, Loco Dice and Luciano.[^clr-beatportal-2026] How a party like this runs as a business is its own subject, at https://lastcall.cold.fyi/.

### When did CircoLoco Records launch?

Rockstar Games and CircoLoco announced **CircoLoco Records** on May 24, 2021.[^clr-mixmag-2021][^clr-gamesradar-2021] The plan was a weekly run of EPs taken from the album *Monday Dreamin'*, with Seth Troxler's "Lumartes" already out as a single.[^clr-djmag-2021] The first EP was due June 4 and the full compilation July 9, 2021.[^clr-mixmag-2021][^clr-djmag-2021]

Carbonaro traces the idea to his friendship with Sam Houser and a joint CircoLoco and Rockstar event in Miami in October 2019; the label itself was put together during the pandemic.[^clr-nss-2021] It was a way to reach the community while clubs were shut for COVID-19.[^clr-beatportal-2026] A label is a reasonable thing to build when the dance floor is closed and the game is not.

The company behind the name is Rockstar Records LLC, founded May 24, 2021, with "CircoLoco Records" as its trade name - though that comes from fan and trade sites, not a company filing.[^clr-gtawiki-label][^clr-rsintel-crl010]

### Does Rockstar own CircoLoco Records?

It is a partnership. At launch the label was billed as independent and kept separate from both companies' other businesses for now.[^clr-rollingstone-2021] Nobody has published an ownership split.[^clr-rollingstone-2021][^clr-mbw-2021]

Rockstar fielded founder Sam Houser and director of music Ivan Pavlovich for the launch. "Music's been part of our DNA since day one," Pavlovich told Rolling Stone.[^clr-rollingstone-2021] Houser's stated aim was to "find new ways to bring the very best underground music to the widest audience possible."[^clr-gamesradar-2021] Nick Benedetti spoke for CircoLoco, and the 2021 artists were picked jointly, all of them CircoLoco alumni.[^clr-rollingstone-2021]

The work splits roughly like this:

- **Rockstar's side:** placement in its games (the Media Player sticks, a CircoLoco tee in Los Santos Tuners) and the "CircoLoco / Rockstar Games presents" credit on releases.[^clr-gtawiki-label][^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-rsintel-category]
- **CircoLoco's side:** the A&R, the DC10 floor where tracks get tested, and the visual identity, with Rockstar as partner and backer.[^clr-beatportal-2026]

### How does the label pick its records?

**A&R** (artists and repertoire) is the job of finding artists and choosing what gets released. Carbonaro says there is no fixed formula: signings go on instinct and on whether an act fits CircoLoco.[^clr-beatportal-2026] His test, in four words: "It has to feel honest."[^clr-beatportal-2026]

In practice, that means:[^clr-beatportal-2026]

- artists who shape a scene rather than fit into one
- a mix of new and established names
- tracks tested on the dance floor in front of the party's crowd
- a look that is part of the record: a blood-red palette that echoes the club, and surreal digital art for *Monday Dreamin'*

The catalog shows the taste moving. The 2021 compilation leans on long-time CircoLoco names such as Luciano, Carl Craig and Moodymann; from 2024 on the releases tilt toward chart tech house and house, with Prospa, PAWSA, Jamback and Michael Bibi, and Prospa turning up more than anyone.[^clr-deejay-label][^clr-bandcamp-music][^clr-rsintel-category][^clr-weraveyou-2026-mystery] That shift is plain in the release list; the label hasn't described it.

### Who made "Positive" on CircoLoco Records?

Jamback, a Dutch producer, alone. "Positive" is his CircoLoco Records debut, released November 7, 2025.[^clr-bandcamp-positive][^clr-crack-2025] Bandcamp, Crack Magazine, Chromatic Club and Beatport all credit him with no co-artist; if you remember a featured name on it, it isn't on the record.[^clr-bandcamp-positive][^clr-crack-2025][^clr-chromatic-2026][^clr-beatportal-2026] The track was shaped by the loss of his mother.[^clr-crack-2025]

Then it parked at the top of Beatport. By May 2026 "Positive" had spent 64 days at No. 1, the longest run in the store's history, passing FISHER's "Losing It" at 62 days in 2018.[^clr-beatportal-2026][^clr-chromatic-2026] Technoairlines' 12-month chart-points ranking, as of November 2026, puts it first in the label's catalog.[^clr-technoairlines]

Its catalog number is probably CLR021, though the only evidence is a press image filename.[^clr-crack-2025]

### What is Prospa's "Don't Stop"?

"Don't Stop" is a Prospa single released on CircoLoco Records on April 11, 2025, catalog CLR015 (Discogs lists it as CLR015BP).[^clr-discogs-dontstop][^clr-rsintel-crl015] It builds on a hook from Somethin' For The People's 1997 song "My Love Is the Shhh!".[^clr-ravejungle-2025] RaveJungle ran its release-day story on April 12, 2025; Discogs and RockstarINTEL both say April 11.[^clr-ravejungle-2025][^clr-discogs-dontstop][^clr-rsintel-category]

Prospa are Harvey Blumler and Gosha Smith, a duo from Leeds.[^clr-crack-2026] By the time of "Don't Stop" they were already the label's busiest act, after "If You Want My Loving" (CRL 010) and "This Rhythm" (CRL 013), which had passed 7 million streams by 2025.[^clr-rsintel-crl010][^clr-rsintel-crl013][^clr-ravejungle-2025] Technoairlines' November 2026 ranking has "Don't Stop" second in the catalog.[^clr-technoairlines]

### What is "Free Your Mind" by Prospa and Cloonee?

Two things share the name. **"Free Your Mind"** is the title track with Cloonee, and Beatport-derived charts also credit Sybil.[^clr-crack-2026][^clr-technoairlines] ***Free Your Mind*** is Prospa's debut album, released on CircoLoco Records on June 5, 2026; deejay.de lists the 2 x 12-inch vinyl (CLR019V) for July 16, 2026.[^clr-crack-2026][^clr-deejay-fym]

The album features Josh Baker and RAHH on "You Don't Own Me" and Kosmo Kint on "Love Songs".[^clr-crack-2026] In May 2026, "Baby" and "Free Your Mind" held No. 1 and No. 2 on the Beatport Top 100, and Beatport named CircoLoco Records its Label of the Month.[^clr-beatportal-2026] Technoairlines' November 2026 ranking puts the title track fifth in the label's 12-month chart.[^clr-technoairlines]

### How do I get the CircoLoco Records music in GTA Online?

Through the **Media Player**, the radio-wheel slot for collectible Media Sticks, added with Los Santos Tuners on July 20, 2021; [the GTA Online section](/online/) explains how it works.[^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer][^clr-gtabase-mediaplayer] *Monday Dreamin'* plays at the LS Car Meet, and four EP sticks - Black, Blue, Violet and Green - are hidden around the city.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer] Find all four and you unlock Seth Troxler's **CLR Launch Party** mix and a CircoLoco tee.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-07]

Troxler built that mix by reworking the *Monday Dreamin'* stems, and it debuted with Los Santos Tuners.[^clr-beatportal-2026] Rockstar's notes and GTA Base say it reworks all 20 album tracks; the fan wiki lists 16.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-07][^clr-gtabase-mediaplayer][^clr-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

The NEZ EP was spread through the game before release. "You Wanna?" came as a Media Stick in The Contract (December 2021); ScHoolboy Q's "Let's Get It", produced by NEZ, premiered on Radio Los Santos; and "Freaks" with Moodymann and Gangsta Boo was heard first in the Los Santos Tuners trailer and sits in Kenny's Backyard Boogie mix.[^clr-sportskeeda-contract][^clr-worthplaying-2021-12][^clr-sportskeeda-2022] Rockstar said all three would be heard only in GTA Online until the EP came out, which it did on February 25, 2022.[^clr-worthplaying-2021-12][^clr-sportskeeda-2022]

After that the trail goes cold. The release posts from CRL 004 through CRL 019 never mention GTA Online, and no later CircoLoco Records release has been documented in the game.[^clr-rsintel-category]

### Is the Music Locker part of CircoLoco Records?

Nothing on record ties them together. The Music Locker is the club under The Diamond Casino added with The Cayo Perico Heist in December 2020, with Moodymann, Keinemusik and Palms Trax as residents - five months before the label was announced.[^clr-djmag-2020] The overlap is artists, not paperwork: at the label's launch, GTA Online's nightclubs already featured CircoLoco alumni such as The Blessed Madonna, Dixon and Solomun, and Moodymann later turned up on the *Monday Dreamin'* Green EP.[^clr-gamesradar-2021][^clr-edmtunes-2021] Shared DJs don't make it a CircoLoco Records room.

### Does GTA VI have a CircoLoco Records radio station?

Yes. CircoLoco Records Radio is one of the 22 stations in the game at launch, playing house and techno.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-cultr-2026] The first songs logged on it are Skream's "Floral," Michael Bibi's "Rhythm of the Night" and Mind Against's "Let It Go"; GameRant adds "Deep N Sexy" by Rossi. and Demi.[^six-keengamer-all-2026][^six-gamerant-2026] No host has been named.

The station was a leak first. On August 28, 2026, We Rave You reported a clip, said to come from an unfinished build, showing the name; neither Rockstar nor CircoLoco confirmed it, and Rockstar's reveal of six stations on October 8 left it out.[^clr-weraveyou-2026-leak][^clr-gamesradar-2026] It took the game coming out to settle it. The full dial is in the [GTA VI section](/gta-6/), and the songs are on the [station's page](/stations/gta6-circoloco-records-radio/).
