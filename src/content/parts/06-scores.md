## The scores: music written for the games

**The short version:** For its first decade, Grand Theft Auto's original music was mostly in-house songs credited to fictional bands, played on the radio rather than under the action. GTA V (2013) brought a full original score, which Rockstar called its first fully collaborative score, built by Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, The Alchemist and Oh No as layered stems that react to play.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] This section also covers the scores for Red Dead Redemption, Red Dead Redemption 2, Max Payne 3 and GTA Online, and Justice's score for GTA VI.[^scr-stereogum-2026]

### Did early GTA have original music?

Yes, but it went on the radio. GTA 1 (1997) shipped with seven stations of entirely original music by DMA Design's own musicians.[^scr-pcgamer-2020] Craig Conner wrote the songs with rapper Jonathan Wilson, credited as "Robert De Negro", under invented acts such as Da Shootaz, Slumpussy and CCC.[^scr-timeextension-2022] Per Grand Theft Wiki, the main theme, "Gangster Friday", plays on no station at all.[^scr-gtwiki-gta1]

The habit lasted. Per the GTA wiki, only five of GTA 2's 30 songs were licensed, and the rest were original music.[^scr-gtawiki-gta2] GTA London kept four stations of Rockstar-made tracks, per Grand Theft Wiki.[^scr-gtwiki-london] GTA III and Liberty City Stories filled Head Radio and Lips 106 with fictional artists made for the game.[^scr-gtwiki-gta3][^scr-gtawiki-lcs] Conner also wrote original music for Manhunt (2003),[^fc-wiki-manhunt] mostly on a Korg Mono/Poly synth.[^scr-sos-2005] The in-house stations are covered at /early-radio/.

### Who composed the GTA V score?

Four acts: Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, The Alchemist and Oh No.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] Rockstar soundtrack supervisor Ivan Pavlovich called it the first fully collaborative score Rockstar had made, and described the 2013 package as "the largest soundtrack that we've done, and the largest score that we've done."[^scr-rollingstone-2013]

It was a relay more than a band. Per Wikipedia, Tangerine Dream's Edgar Froese went first: his first eight months on the job produced 62 hours of music, recorded with the band in Austria.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] Jackson, Rockstar's go-to composer since Red Dead Redemption,[^scr-mix-2019] first scored Trevor's missions, and The Alchemist and Oh No sampled and layered the material, per the same Wikipedia account.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] DJ Shadow mixed the result and matched it to gameplay.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror]

The team made more than 20 hours of music in all, per Wikipedia.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] Rolling Stone put it at roughly 20 movies' worth of score.[^scr-rollingstone-2013]

Two of the composers then got their own radio station. The Lab, a station built on their album *Welcome to Los Santos*, carries the record, which came out April 14, 2015 with the PC version of the game and at retail on April 21.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] Sources disagree on when the station arrived: PC Gamer says July 2015, others say April; /modern-radio/ has both versions.[^scr-pcgamer-2017]

Froese also put out his own version. *The Cinematographic Score - GTA 5* came out March 24, 2014 on Eastgate: 12 tracks, limited to 2,000 copies, per Wikipedia.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] Pressing runs that small are a crate-digger's problem; https://groove.cold.fyi/ covers how vinyl editions get made and priced.

### How does a dynamic score work in GTA V?

A **dynamic score** reacts to what the player is doing instead of playing a fixed track.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] GTA V's runs on Rockstar's interactive stem system.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] A **stem** is one layer of a cue - drums, bass, a synth line - that the game can bring in or drop out. Per Wikipedia, each GTA V mission used eight stems, with Pavlovich doing the mix-downs.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror]

The point is that the music can build or thin out with the action without restarting. The trade-off is volume: every layer has to work in combination with the others, which helps explain hour counts that read like box sets. Woody Jackson told Mix that the stem count grew from eight to 15 by Red Dead Redemption 2.[^scr-mix-2019]

### Who made the Red Dead Redemption soundtrack?

Bill Elm, a member of the band Friends of Dean Martinez, and Woody Jackson, a former member, scored Red Dead Redemption (2010).[^scr-wiki-rdr] Per Wikipedia, they made over 14 hours of music across 15 months, all recorded at 130 BPM in A minor, with Ennio Morricone's *Dollars Trilogy* as the touchstone.[^scr-wiki-rdr] Tommy Morgan played harmonica.[^scr-wiki-rdr]

The game also placed four vocal songs: José González's "Far Away", Jamie Lidell's "Compass", Ashtar Command's "Deadman's Gun" and William Elliott Whitmore's "Bury Me Not on the Lone Prairie".[^scr-wiki-rdr] "Far Away" won Best Song in a Game at the Spike Video Game Awards.[^scr-wiki-rdr] The album came out digitally May 18, 2010 and physically August 23, 2010, with David Holmes selecting its instrumental tracks, per Wikipedia.[^scr-wiki-rdr]

Credits for the album side are described differently in different places: IBTimes UK, drawing on a 2015 GamesTM interview, says Craig Conner produced Red Dead Redemption soundtracks too.[^scr-ibtimes-2015] The two accounts may cover different jobs; neither source spells out the split.

### Who made the Red Dead Redemption 2 soundtrack, and is D'Angelo on it?

Woody Jackson composed the score, and Daniel Lanois produced the vocal performances.[^scr-variety-2018] The singers were D'Angelo, Willie Nelson, Nas, Rhiannon Giddens and Josh Homme, with Arca, Colin Stetson, Senyawa and Duane Eddy among the other contributors.[^scr-variety-2018] D'Angelo's song "Unshaken" was his first new material since 2014's *Black Messiah*.[^scr-variety-2018] It reached number 6 on US R&B Digital Song Sales, per Wikipedia.[^scr-wiki-rdr2]

Willie Nelson's song is "Cruel Cruel World," and Josh Homme recorded a separate version of the same song, Stereogum reported in November 2018.[^gf-b-stereogum-2018]

Rockstar's own breakdown, as reported by Gaming Audio News in November 2018, adds Jeff Silverman on additional production and arrangements and lists additional scoring by Colin Stetson, David Ferguson, David Ralicke, Gabe Witcher, Mario Batkovic, Matt Sweeney, Rabih Beaini and Senyawa.[^scr-gan-2018]

The scale was larger than GTA V's. Jackson spent about five years on the game's music and recorded about 60 hours, of which only a fraction was used.[^scr-mix-2019] Per Wikipedia, over 110 musicians took part, and Pavlovich estimated a player hears about a third of the music in one playthrough.[^scr-wiki-rdr2]

Rockstar split the release in two. The vocal album came out digitally July 12, 2019, with CD and vinyl on September 20, 2019. The score album followed digitally on August 9, 2019, with a physical edition on April 10, 2020, per Wikipedia.[^scr-wiki-rdr2]

### Who did the Max Payne 3 soundtrack?

Los Angeles band HEALTH composed and recorded the Max Payne 3 Official Soundtrack.[^scr-gameinformer-2012] The single "Tears" went on iTunes for the May 15, 2012 console launch, and the full soundtrack followed digitally on May 23, 2012.[^scr-gameinformer-2012] HEALTH's John Famiglietti said Rockstar gave the band freedom to bring its own sound to the game.[^scr-gameinformer-2012]

It was an unusual hire: a working band, not a film composer, handed a whole game's score. HEALTH came back for GTA Online, appearing among the contributors to its 2023 original score album.[^scr-rockstarintel-2023]

For the game's 10th anniversary, Rockstar announced *Max Payne 3 - The Official Soundtrack (Anniversary Edition)* on May 13, 2022, in digital and limited-edition vinyl formats, with tracks made for the game that had not been released before.[^gf-b-shacknews-2022] No release date was given at the announcement.[^gf-b-shacknews-2022]

### Does GTA Online have its own score?

It does, though it arrives in pieces with updates rather than as one release. Per the GTA wiki, Los Santos Drug Wars (December 13, 2022) added a seven-track adaptive score: five tracks by Soulwax, some reworked from ideas cut from GTA V's Soulwax FM, and two by Nick Hook, each built from four to eight stems, with the stems the game mixes depending on gameplay events.[^scr-gtawiki-drugwars]

On December 15, 2023, around The Chop Shop update, Rockstar put *DāM-FunK Presents The Music of Grand Theft Auto Online (Original Score)* on streaming services: 15 tracks, 56 minutes 48 seconds.[^scr-rockstarintel-2023] Contributors include HEALTH, &ME, James Curd, Soulwax, Holy Fuck, Rampa and Show Me The Body.[^scr-rockstarintel-2023] DāM-FunK also has a mix on the in-game Media Player, per the GTA wiki.[^scr-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

The lineup reads like a club booking more than a composer roster, which fits the rest of GTA Online's music, covered at /online/.

### Who is composing the GTA VI score?

Justice. Rockstar named the duo as composers of GTA VI's original score on October 15, 2026, the same day it released the Launch Trailer.[^scr-stereogum-2026] That trailer's song, Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, is a track on *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, the separate 34-track record made with Atlantic Records (see /gta-6/), not the score.[^scr-stereogum-2026][^scr-mbw-2026]

The score has its own release track. On October 29, 2026, Justice released "Pusher", a song from the original score, and Game Informer reported that a 30-track score album is due December 11, 2026.[^scr-gameinformer-2026] The game itself is due November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.[^scr-gameinformer-2026]

Rockstar has described its plans as "the next evolution of the pioneering in-game radio stations, dynamic score, and musical touchpoints".[^scr-mbw-2026]

Complex reported on October 29, 2026 that Rockstar describes the score album as 30 main songs plus digital bonus tracks, and that "Pusher" features Gesaffelstein.[^gf-b-complex-2026] Featured artists like these are not credited co-composers.[^gf-b-complex-2026]

As of November 15, 2026, nothing official describes how Justice's music is wired into play, how long it runs, or who else composed it.
