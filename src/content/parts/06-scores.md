## The scores: music written for the games

**The short version:** For its first decade, Grand Theft Auto's original music was mostly in-house songs credited to fictional bands, played on the radio rather than under the action. GTA V (2013) brought a full original score, which Rockstar called its first fully collaborative score, built by Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, The Alchemist and Oh No as layered stems that react to play.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] Red Dead Redemption, Red Dead Redemption 2, Max Payne 3 and GTA Online all got scores of their own, and Justice is scoring GTA VI.[^scr-stereogum-2026]

### Did early GTA have original music?

Yes, but it went on the radio. GTA 1 (1997) shipped with seven stations of entirely original music by DMA Design's own musicians.[^scr-pcgamer-2020] Craig Conner wrote the songs with rapper Jonathan Wilson, credited as "Robert De Negro", under invented acts such as Da Shootaz, Slumpussy and CCC.[^scr-timeextension-2022] The main theme, "Gangster Friday", plays on no station at all.[^scr-gtwiki-gta1]

The habit lasted. Only five of GTA 2's 30 songs were licensed; the rest were written in-house.[^scr-gtawiki-gta2] GTA London kept four stations of Rockstar-made tracks.[^scr-gtwiki-london] GTA III and Liberty City Stories filled Head Radio and Lips 106 with fictional artists made for the game.[^scr-gtwiki-gta3][^scr-gtawiki-lcs] Conner also wrote original music for Manhunt (2003),[^fc-wiki-manhunt] mostly on a Korg Mono/Poly synth.[^scr-sos-2005] The in-house stations are covered at /early-radio/.

### Who composed the GTA V score?

Four acts: Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, The Alchemist and Oh No.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] Rockstar soundtrack supervisor Ivan Pavlovich called it the first fully collaborative score Rockstar had made, and described the 2013 package as "the largest soundtrack that we've done, and the largest score that we've done."[^scr-rollingstone-2013]

It was a relay more than a band. Tangerine Dream's Edgar Froese went first: his first eight months on the job produced 62 hours of music, recorded with the band in Austria.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] Jackson, Rockstar's go-to composer since Red Dead Redemption,[^scr-mix-2019] first scored Trevor's missions, then The Alchemist and Oh No sampled and layered the material.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] DJ Shadow mixed the result and matched it to gameplay.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror]

The team made more than 20 hours of music in all.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] Rolling Stone put it at roughly 20 movies' worth of score.[^scr-rollingstone-2013]

Two of the composers then got their own radio station. The Lab, built on their album *Welcome to Los Santos*, carries the record, which came out April 14, 2015 with the PC version of the game and at retail on April 21.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] PC Gamer dates the station to July 2015; other accounts say April, and /modern-radio/ has both.[^scr-pcgamer-2017]

Froese also put out his own version. *The Cinematographic Score - GTA 5* came out March 24, 2014 on Eastgate: 12 tracks, limited to 2,000 copies.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror] Pressing runs that small are a crate-digger's problem; https://groove.cold.fyi/ covers how vinyl editions get made and priced.

### How does a dynamic score work in GTA V?

A **dynamic score** reacts to what the player is doing instead of playing a fixed track.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] GTA V's runs on Rockstar's interactive stem system.[^scr-rollingstone-2013] A **stem** is one layer of a cue - drums, bass, a synth line - that the game can bring in or drop out. Each GTA V mission used eight stems, with Pavlovich doing the mix-downs.[^scr-wiki-gtav-mirror]

That lets the music build or thin out with the action without restarting. The cost is volume: every layer has to work in combination with the others, which helps explain hour counts that read like box sets. Woody Jackson told Mix that the stem count grew from eight to 15 by Red Dead Redemption 2.[^scr-mix-2019]

### Who made the Red Dead Redemption soundtrack?

Bill Elm, a member of the band Friends of Dean Martinez, and Woody Jackson, a former member, scored Red Dead Redemption (2010).[^scr-wiki-rdr] They made over 14 hours of music across 15 months, all recorded at 130 BPM in A minor, with Ennio Morricone's *Dollars Trilogy* as the touchstone.[^scr-wiki-rdr] Tommy Morgan played harmonica.[^scr-wiki-rdr]

The game also placed four vocal songs: José González's "Far Away", Jamie Lidell's "Compass", Ashtar Command's "Deadman's Gun" and William Elliott Whitmore's "Bury Me Not on the Lone Prairie".[^scr-wiki-rdr] "Far Away" won Best Song in a Game at the Spike Video Game Awards.[^scr-wiki-rdr] The album came out digitally May 18, 2010 and physically August 23, 2010, with David Holmes selecting its instrumental tracks.[^scr-wiki-rdr]

IBTimes UK, drawing on a 2015 GamesTM interview, says Craig Conner produced Red Dead Redemption soundtracks too.[^scr-ibtimes-2015] That may be a different job from Elm and Jackson's; nobody has spelled out the split.

### Who made the Red Dead Redemption 2 soundtrack, and is D'Angelo on it?

Woody Jackson composed the score, and Daniel Lanois produced the vocal performances.[^scr-variety-2018] The singers were D'Angelo, Willie Nelson, Nas, Rhiannon Giddens and Josh Homme, with Arca, Colin Stetson, Senyawa and Duane Eddy among the other contributors.[^scr-variety-2018] D'Angelo's song "Unshaken" was his first new material since 2014's *Black Messiah*.[^scr-variety-2018] It reached number 6 on US R&B Digital Song Sales.[^scr-wiki-rdr2]

Willie Nelson's song is "Cruel Cruel World," and Josh Homme recorded his own version of it.[^gf-b-stereogum-2018]

Rockstar's credits add Jeff Silverman on additional production and arrangements, and list additional scoring by Colin Stetson, David Ferguson, David Ralicke, Gabe Witcher, Mario Batkovic, Matt Sweeney, Rabih Beaini and Senyawa.[^scr-gan-2018]

The scale dwarfed GTA V's. Jackson spent about five years on the game's music and recorded about 60 hours, of which only a fraction was used.[^scr-mix-2019] Over 110 musicians took part, and Pavlovich estimated a player hears about a third of the music in one playthrough.[^scr-wiki-rdr2]

Rockstar split the release in two. The vocal album came out digitally July 12, 2019, with CD and vinyl on September 20, 2019. The score album followed digitally on August 9, 2019, with a physical edition on April 10, 2020.[^scr-wiki-rdr2]

### Who did the Max Payne 3 soundtrack?

Los Angeles band HEALTH composed and recorded the Max Payne 3 Official Soundtrack.[^scr-gameinformer-2012] The single "Tears" went on iTunes for the May 15, 2012 console launch, and the full soundtrack followed digitally on May 23, 2012.[^scr-gameinformer-2012] HEALTH's John Famiglietti said Rockstar gave the band freedom to bring its own sound to the game.[^scr-gameinformer-2012]

Handing a whole game's score to a working band, with no film composer in sight, was an unusual hire. HEALTH came back for GTA Online, appearing among the contributors to its 2023 original score album.[^scr-rockstarintel-2023]

For the game's 10th anniversary, Rockstar announced *Max Payne 3 - The Official Soundtrack (Anniversary Edition)* on May 13, 2022, in digital and limited-edition vinyl formats, with tracks made for the game that had never been released.[^gf-b-shacknews-2022] No release date came with the announcement.[^gf-b-shacknews-2022]

### Does GTA Online have its own score?

It does, though it arrives in pieces with updates rather than as one release. Los Santos Drug Wars (December 13, 2022) added a seven-track adaptive score: five tracks by Soulwax, some reworked from ideas cut from GTA V's Soulwax FM, and two by Nick Hook.[^scr-gtawiki-drugwars] Each is built from four to eight stems, and the game picks which to mix depending on what is happening.[^scr-gtawiki-drugwars]

On December 15, 2023, around The Chop Shop update, Rockstar put *DāM-FunK Presents The Music of Grand Theft Auto Online (Original Score)* on streaming services: 15 tracks, 56 minutes 48 seconds.[^scr-rockstarintel-2023] Contributors include HEALTH, &ME, James Curd, Soulwax, Holy Fuck, Rampa and Show Me The Body.[^scr-rockstarintel-2023] DāM-FunK also has a mix on the in-game Media Player.[^scr-gtawiki-mediaplayer]

The lineup reads like a club booking more than a composer roster, which fits the rest of GTA Online's music, covered at /online/.

### Who composed the GTA VI score?

Justice. Rockstar named the duo as composers of GTA VI's original score on October 15, 2026, the same day it released the Launch Trailer.[^scr-stereogum-2026] That trailer's song, Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, belongs to *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, the separate 34-track record made with Atlantic Records (see /gta-6/), not the score.[^scr-stereogum-2026][^scr-mbw-2026]

The score has its own release track. On October 29, 2026, Justice released "Pusher", a song from the original score, and a 30-track score album is due December 11, 2026.[^scr-gameinformer-2026] Rockstar counts that as 30 main songs plus digital bonus tracks, and "Pusher" features Gesaffelstein - a featured artist, not a credited co-composer.[^gf-b-complex-2026] The game itself came out on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.[^six-keengamer-all-2026]

Rockstar has described its plans as "the next evolution of the pioneering in-game radio stations, dynamic score, and musical touchpoints".[^scr-mbw-2026] It calls the score adaptive, with compositions "dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with the world."[^six-inven-score-2026] It has not said how long the score runs or who else wrote for it.
