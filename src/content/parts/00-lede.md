# The Radio Wheel

Steal a car in Grand Theft Auto and the first thing it does is play you a song. Hold the button and a wheel of stations fans out: punk, country, reggaeton, a call-in show about gardening, an ad for a soda you will never drink. For almost 30 years Rockstar Games has treated that wheel as a small media company inside the game, with music directors, licensing deals, celebrity DJs, writers, original scores and, lately, its own record label and club residencies.

This is an independent guide to how that works: who digs the records, how a station gets built, the [talk radio](/talk-radio/) fans still quote, the [fake bands and fake ads](/lore/), the [club era of GTA Online](/online/), [CircoLoco Records](/circoloco/), and [why songs quietly vanish](/licensing/) from games people already own. GTA V alone has sold nearly 235 million units, per Take-Two in November 2026, and it has outlived some of the music licenses on its own soundtrack.[^lic-kitguru-2026][^lic-taketwo-2026] [Grand Theft Auto VI](/gta-6/) is set for November 19, 2026.[^six-finviz-2026]

Below the sections sits the part built for digging: a page for every [station](/stations/) in every game, every [song](/songs/) this guide could source, the [songs that were pulled](/removed/) and when, every talk show and host.
