# The Radio Wheel

Steal a car in Grand Theft Auto and the first thing it does is play you a song. Hold the button and a wheel of stations fans out: punk, country, reggaeton, a call-in show about gardening, an ad for a soda you will never drink. For almost 30 years Rockstar Games has run that wheel like a small media company inside the game, with music directors, licensing deals, celebrity DJs, writers, original scores and, lately, its own record label and club residencies.

Here is how it works: who digs the records, how a station gets built, the [talk radio](/talk-radio/) fans still quote, the [fake bands and fake ads](/lore/), the [club era of GTA Online](/online/), [CircoLoco Records](/circoloco/), and [why songs quietly vanish](/licensing/) from games people already own. GTA V alone had sold nearly 235 million units by November 2026, and it has outlived some of the music licenses on its own soundtrack.[^lic-kitguru-2026][^lic-taketwo-2026] [Grand Theft Auto VI](/gta-6/) arrived on November 19, 2026, with 22 stations on the dial.[^six-keengamer-all-2026]

Further down is the part built for digging: a page for every [station](/stations/) in every game, every [song](/songs/) with a source behind it, the [songs that were pulled](/removed/) and when, every talk show and host. Every fact links to where it came from. When two sources tell it differently, you get both. Leaks are labelled as leaks.
