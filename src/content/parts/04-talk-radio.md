## Talk radio: Chatterbox to Chakra Attack

**The short version:** Talk radio exists in Grand Theft Auto because, by Dan Houser's own account, it was the cheapest way to fill hours of driving in GTA III (2001). Written mostly by Houser and Lazlow Jones, it became the series' sharpest satire: one call-in station in Liberty City, two in Vice City, a whole network of shows on San Andreas's WCTR, three political stations in GTA IV and a left/right split by county in GTA V. Lazlow, who played a steadily sadder version of himself, left Rockstar in 2020 after nine GTA games, in a month that depends on who you read.[^talk-gameranx-2025][^talk-wiki-lazlow][^talk-gtawiki-gta5stations][^talk-gtaboom-2024]

[[MODULE:talk]]

### Why does GTA have talk radio at all?

Houser has said the GTA III radio was a two-year project, and that Rockstar's Scotland team under Leslie Benzies spent the first year just getting the system to work.[^talk-gameranx-2025] With hours of driving to fill and no budget, Houser and Lazlow pitched talk radio as the cheap fix.[^talk-gameranx-2025] Houser later gave IGN the brief in one line: "how do we fill this with absolutely no money?"[^talk-gameranx-2025] Someone then suggested letting the game's own characters call in, which Houser said made the whole thing feel magical.[^talk-gameranx-2025]

The budget hack stuck. Music stations sell a mood; talk stations build a society. Each game, Lazlow told Kinda Funny Games, sets out to satirize a specific place and time, and the callers, sponsors and panel guests tell you what kind of city you are stealing cars in.[^talk-gamesradar-2024]

It pays off in plot too: WCTR's news reports the player's missions, and the station's content changes as the story advances, so some segments only play once.[^talk-gtawiki-wctr3d][^talk-gtwiki-wctr]

The talk dial, game by game:[^talk-fandom-chatterbox][^talk-gtawiki-kchat][^talk-gtawiki-vcpr][^talk-gtwiki-wctr][^talk-gtwiki-lcfr][^talk-gtawiki-vcs-stations][^talk-gtawiki-wktt][^talk-gtawiki-plr][^talk-gtwiki-integrity][^talk-gtawiki-cw][^talk-gtawiki-gta5stations]

| Game (year) | Talk stations | Count |
|---|---|---|
| GTA III (2001) | Chatterbox FM 109 | 1 |
| Vice City (2002) | K-Chat, VCPR | 2 |
| San Andreas (2004) | WCTR, eight programs including news | 1 |
| Liberty City Stories (2005) | LCFR, five shows | 1 |
| Vice City Stories (2006) | VCPR, five shows including two archive reruns | 1 |
| GTA IV (2008) | WKTT, PLR, Integrity 2.0 | 3 |
| Chinatown Wars (2009) | none | 0 |
| GTA V (2013) | WCTR, Blaine County Radio, three shows each | 2 |

Chinatown Wars skipped talk entirely. Its stations carry no voice tracks at all and are purely instrumental.[^talk-gtawiki-cw]

### What was Chatterbox FM in GTA III?

**Chatterbox FM** is GTA III's talk station and the template for everything after it: Lazlow, playing himself, taking calls from a city of cranks.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox] He plays the only sane man in the building, answering each caller with caustic sarcasm and a stock line about the call being a lesson to us all.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox] The station sits at 109, which is outside the real FM band of 87.5 to 108 MHz, so no real receiver could tune it (the real version of that dial lives at https://airwaves.cold.fyi/).[^talk-fandom-chatterbox]

The callers are Liberty City's oddest residents: the Survivalist caller, the SPANK caller, Freddy, the C.R.A.P. caller, Bob from Pike Creek, Barry Stark and Toni Cipriani.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox] Two longer interviews break up the calls: one with Reed Tucker, and one with Fernando Martinez, who uses the airtime to plug his marriage-rescue business, Fernando's New Beginnings.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox] The station belongs to Donald Love's Love Media, which runs self-congratulating public service announcements between segments.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox]

The targets are talk radio and its callers, consumerism, self-help, pharmaceutical ads, reality TV, conspiracy theories and anti-technology groups.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox] A caller also complains that a show called Gardening with Maurice has been taken off the air - a throwaway that pays off three years later in San Andreas.[^talk-gtawiki-wctr3d]

### What talk stations are in GTA Vice City?

Vice City (2002, set in 1986) doubled the talk count, and the two stations split the decade's noise down the middle.[^talk-gtawiki-kchat][^talk-gtawiki-vcpr]

**K-Chat** is the celebrity station, hosted by Amy Sheckenhausen (Leyna Weber).[^talk-gtawiki-kchat][^talk-gtwiki-vc] Her guests are a catalog of mid-1980s excess: Jezz Torrent of Love Fist, a fictional band, with his companion Mandy, feminist professor Michaela Carapadis, Pat "Mr. Zoo" Flannerdy, New Age priestess Gethsemanee Starhawk Moonmaker, ex-football player BJ Smith, actor Claude Maginot, and Thor, who sells self-help tapes.[^talk-gtawiki-kchat][^talk-gtwiki-vc] The satire lands on rock-star behavior, academic pretension, New Age and self-help fads, fitness celebrities and avant-garde art.[^talk-gtawiki-kchat]

**VCPR (Vice City Public Radio)** is a public-radio parody, and one of two stations playing in a stolen taxi.[^talk-gtawiki-vcpr] Its one show, **Pressing Issues**, is hosted by Maurice Chavez (Philip Anthony-Rodriguez), a short-tempered moderator who talks down to every guest he books.[^talk-gtawiki-vcpr][^talk-gtawiki-voiceactors] Station managers Jonathan Freeloader and Michelle Montanius run guilt-trip **pledge drives**, public radio's on-air fundraising appeals.[^talk-gtawiki-vcpr]

Pressing Issues runs three panels:[^talk-gtwiki-pressing]

| Panel | Guests |
|---|---|
| Public safety | Congressman Alex Shrub (Chris Lucas), liberal activist Callum Crayshaw (Sean Modica), Florida secessionist John F. Hickory (L.J. Gansen) |
| Morality | Televangelist Pastor Richards (David Green), anti-pop-culture mother Jan Brown (Maureen Silliman), naturist Barry Stark (Renaud Sebbane) |
| Perception and positive thinking | Goth Konstantinos Smith, self-help fraud Jeremy Robard (Peter Silvestro), Jenny Louise Crab (Mary Birdsong) |

The Robard bit is the kind of detail superfans quote: the initials of his self-help program spell out drug names.[^talk-gtwiki-pressing] K-Chat and VCPR are the two Vice City stations left off the official soundtrack box set.[^talk-gtawiki-kchat]

### What talk shows are on WCTR in GTA San Andreas?

**WCTR (West Coast Talk Radio)** in San Andreas (2004, set in 1992) is the biggest talk station in the series: a full schedule of shows, plus news, on one frequency.[^talk-gtwiki-wctr] The schedule:[^talk-gtwiki-wctr][^talk-gtawiki-wctr3d][^talk-gtwiki-sa][^talk-gtawiki-voiceactors][^talk-fandom-tightend]

| Show | Host (voice) | The joke |
|---|---|---|
| WCTR News | Lianne Forget (Sharon Washington), reporter Richard Burns (Wil Wheaton) | Doom-laden bulletins about the player's own missions |
| The Tight End Zone | Derrick Thackery (Peter Appel) | A sports call-in host who does not seem to know much about sports; the innuendo is the point |
| The Wild Traveler | James Pedeaston (Sam Tsoutsouvas) | Travelogue readings from a host so disliked that one episode gets no calls |
| Entertaining America | Billy Dexter (Peter Marx), then Lazlow | Celebrity interviews; the host is killed on air |
| Gardening with Maurice | Maurice (Andy Dick, see below) | Gardening tips that are not about gardening |
| I Say/You Say | Peyton Phillips (Paul Ames) and Mary Phillips (Jackie Hoffman) | A married couple's political debate: he leans communist, she is a greedy capitalist |
| Lonely Hearts | Christy MacIntyre (Sara Moon) | Relationship advice, with Fernando Martinez |
| Area 53 | Marvin Trill (Bob Sevra) | UFO and conspiracy calls; The Truth phones in |

**Entertaining America** has a story arc: Billy Dexter hosts until he is killed on air and Lazlow takes over the chair.[^talk-gtawiki-wctr3d][^talk-wiki-lazlow] The guest list across the run includes OG Loc and, later, Cris Formage, along with Jack Howitzer, and one wiki files Darius Fontaine under the show too.[^talk-gtawiki-wctr3d][^talk-wikigta-wctr]

**Area 53** spoofs the real paranormal call-in show Coast to Coast AM, and The Truth, from the main story, turns up on it.[^talk-gtwiki-wctr] The Tight End Zone runs on sponsorship from Logger Beer and Redwood Cigarettes.[^talk-fandom-tightend]

Two details rest on fan wikis alone. Fans credit Maurice to Andy Dick, and three wikis agree, but no press source confirms it.[^talk-wikigta-wctr][^talk-gtwiki-sa][^talk-gtawiki-voiceactors] And the wikis can't settle Fernando's job on Lonely Hearts: one calls him the improvised host, another says he hijacks the show.[^talk-gtwiki-fernando][^talk-gtawiki-wctr3d]

Lianne Forget signs off her bulletins by announcing that the end is near.[^talk-gtwiki-wctr]

### What talk radio is in Liberty City Stories and Vice City Stories?

**LCFR (Liberty City Free Radio)** in Liberty City Stories (2005, set in 1998) carries five shows:[^talk-gtwiki-lcfr][^talk-gtwiki-heartland]

- **Electron Zone Radio** - Bill (Michael Urichek) and Steve (Ptolemy Slocum) on the "Fruit OS" versus "TOS" war, sending up Apple, DOS, Gates and Jobs. Richard Burns of WCTR calls in.
- **Chatterbox** - Lazlow's call-in show, here as one slot on a schedule, with Ma Cipriani on the line. In story time this is the show that later gets its own frequency in GTA III.
- **Heartland Values with Nurse Bob** - Nurse Bob (Chuck Montgomery) hosts a talk show with a live in-studio audience, parodying Dr. Phil.
- **Breathing World** - Melissa Chowder (Ashley Albert) and guest Crow (Gregg Martin), plugging Crowfest '98, with possible nods to Ozzfest and Sting.
- **Coq O Vin** - French chef Richard Goblin (Mike Shapiro) prepares live animals on air.

**VCPR** returns in Vice City Stories (2006, set in 1984) with five shows.[^talk-gtawiki-vcpr][^talk-gtawiki-vcs-stations] Maurice Chavez is back on Pressing Issues, now with futurist Martin Graves, patriotic mother of 15 Bryony Craddock (Shelagh Ratner) and industrialist Forbes Waverly III (Melinda Wade).[^talk-gtwiki-pressing] **Bait and Switch** is a fishing show with Larry Joe and Bobbie Ray (Jim Florentine), sending up redneck Vietnam veterans;[^fc-gtawiki-florentine] **New World Order** is Dwayne Thorn's foreign-affairs show; and two archive reruns, The Time Ranger (1944) and Moorehead Rides Again (late 1940s), parody old radio serials.[^talk-gtawiki-vcpr] K-Chat, as far as anyone has documented, sits this one out.[^talk-gtawiki-vcs-stations]

Lazlow's Vice City Stories role is on V-Rock, a music station, and nobody agrees on his job title: Wikipedia makes him an intern, WikiGTA a co-host alongside Couzin Ed.[^talk-wiki-lazlow][^talk-wikigta-lazlow]

### What are WKTT, PLR and Integrity 2.0 in GTA IV?

GTA IV (2008) is where the talk radio turned openly political, with one station for each side of the aisle and one for Lazlow's ego.[^talk-gtawiki-wktt][^talk-gtawiki-plr][^talk-gtwiki-integrity]

**WKTT ("We Know The Truth")** is right-wing talk.[^talk-gtawiki-wktt] Its anchor is **The Richard Bastion Show**, with Jason Sudeikis as a Rush Limbaugh-style host.[^talk-gtawiki-wktt] Some of his callers were real people. On July 13, 2007, Rockstar opened the teaser site WKTTRadio.com with a New York phone number where fans could leave a message about what was wrong with Liberty City, America, liberals or their health.[^talk-digitaltrends-2007][^talk-gtawiki-wktt] Some of those calls ended up on Bastion's show.[^talk-gtawiki-wktt] The rest of the schedule: **Just or Unjust**, where Judge Grady replaces verdicts with audience-picked contests, and **Fizz!**, an Entertainment Tonight parody.[^talk-gtawiki-wktt] The episodes added two more: **The Martin Serious Show** in The Lost and Damned, a Howard Stern parody with Smithy the Sidekick, Lisa Lynn and Mark the Manager, and **Conspire** in The Ballad of Gay Tony, John Smith's Alex Jones-style conspiracy hour.[^talk-gtawiki-wktt]

**PLR (Public Liberty Radio)** is the public-radio answer:[^talk-gtawiki-plr]

- **The Séance** - psychic Beatrix Fontaine (Ilyana Kadushin), who keeps steering callers toward paying her.
- **Pacemaker** - health show hosted by Ryan McFallon (Bryan Tucker), with pharma rep Sheila Stafford (Rachel Allen), HMO chief Wilson Taylor Sr. (Bill Hader) and Mason Waylon (Rick Shapiro).
- **Intelligent Agenda** - Mike Riley's (Brian Sack) left-wing call-in, with actor Brandon Roberts, candidate John Hunter and child prodigy Zachary Tyler.

**Integrity 2.0** is a station of its own, not part of PLR, and the best single joke about Lazlow in the series.[^talk-gtwiki-integrity] He walks around Algonquin interviewing strangers: one runs away, one calls him an asshole, and he ends up assaulting a hot dog vendor.[^talk-gtwiki-integrity] The show is pre-recorded and loops because, in the fiction, he cannot afford live shows.[^talk-gtwiki-integrity] ZiT sponsors it in GTA IV; in the episodes the sponsor becomes his stepfather's funeral home, he gains a sidekick named Jorge, and the pair visit the gay club Hercules.[^talk-gtwiki-integrity]

### What is on WCTR and Blaine County Radio in GTA V?

GTA V (2013) splits the talk dial by geography. WCTR plays only in Los Santos County and Blaine County Radio only in Blaine County; drive across the county line and the signal drops, so the radio switches to the next station on the wheel.[^talk-gtawiki-gta5stations] The result is a political map you can hear, with WCTR leaning left and Blaine County Radio leaning right.[^talk-gtwiki-gta5][^talk-gtwiki-blaine]

**WCTR** has three shows:[^talk-gtabase-wctr]

- **Chakra Attack** - Dr. Ray De Angelo Harris (J.B. Smoove), with producer Cheryl Fawkes (Annie Lederman), on spirituality and relaxation.[^talk-pcgamer-smoove-2018][^talk-gtwiki-cheryl] The PS4 and Xbox One release added a second episode.[^talk-gtawiki-gta5stations]
- **Chattersphere** - Lazlow with Michelle Minx.[^talk-gtabase-wctr][^talk-wikigta-lazlow]
- **The Fernando Show** - Fernando Martinez (Frank Chavez) and his producer Jo, with Fernando trying to show a reformed side and interviewing Sue Murry.[^talk-gtwiki-fernando][^talk-gtabase-wctr]

**Blaine County Radio** parodies American conservative talk radio:[^talk-gtwiki-blaine]

- **Blaine County Radio Community Hour** - Ron Jakowski (David Mogentale) airs his conspiracy theories.[^talk-gtwiki-ron]
- **Beyond Insemination** - Duane Earl (Danny McBride), an animal husbandry expert who hates lawyers; his claim to have taught middle-school soccer nods to McBride's Eastbound & Down.[^talk-gtwiki-duane]
- **Bless Your Heart** - Bobby June in front of a live audience, covering Southern homemaking, couponing and recipes like deep-fried margarine, with candidate Jock Cranley and Samantha Muldoon as guests.[^talk-gtawiki-bless] The jokes run very dark, up to a son who died of polio because she refused to vaccinate him.[^talk-gtawiki-bless]

### Who is Lazlow, and when did he leave Rockstar?

**Lazlow Jones** is the one constant. He met one of Rockstar's founders while surfing, and in 2001 Lazlow's production company was hired for GTA III.[^talk-shortlist-2024] He has co-written the stations with Houser since then, and by 2024 called them writing, directing and producing partners of 23 years.[^talk-wiki-lazlow][^talk-shortlist-2024] Off mic he wrote and directed dialogue and voice actors, and worked on trailers, streams and the in-game animated shows.[^talk-pcgamer-2020]

He has appeared in every main-line GTA, and not only on talk stations: he was head DJ on Vice City's V-Rock and hosted GTA V's Fame or Shame TV show.[^talk-shortlist-2024][^talk-gtwiki-lazlow] GTA V was also the first time he appeared on screen as a character, in the missions "Fame or Shame", "Reuniting the Family" and "Meltdown".[^talk-gtwiki-lazlow]

The decline was deliberate. Lazlow told IGN at San Diego Comic-Con that Houser made the in-game Lazlow more pathetic with every game, because the joke depends on the gap between the man and the character.[^talk-gtaboom-2026] When he asked about his GTA V material, Houser told him, in GTA BOOM's paraphrase, that he would get to dance in his underwear.[^talk-gtaboom-2026]

The exit date depends on who you read:

| Source | Says |
|---|---|
| Wikipedia, PC Gamer | Left in April 2020, a month after Dan Houser, after nearly 20 years[^talk-wiki-lazlow][^talk-pcgamer-2020] |
| VGC, 2020-08-14 | Reported the departure in August 2020, from his LinkedIn[^talk-vgc-2020] |

Houser's own last day, according to a Take-Two filing, was March 11, 2020.[^talk-pcgamer-houser-2020] Lazlow gave no official reason, though on Instagram he said he had become his sister's primary caregiver.[^talk-pcgamer-2020] In a later IGN interview, he said he had reevaluated his priorities after her terminal cancer diagnosis, weeks after Red Dead Redemption 2 launched in 2018, and wanted to make a new IP; he counted nine GTA games from 2001 to 2020.[^talk-gtaboom-2024] He now works with Houser at Absurd Ventures, which the two co-founded.[^talk-wiki-lazlow][^talk-gamesradar-2025]

### Which talk radio characters keep coming back?

GTA's talk radio runs on a small repertory company, and the crossovers reward people who listen too closely.

**Fernando Martinez** (Frank Chavez) is the second most heard DJ of the GTA III and IV era games after Lazlow: a Chatterbox interview plugging Fernando's New Beginnings in GTA III, co-host on Emotion 98.3 in Vice City, improvised host of Lonely Hearts in San Andreas, Vice City FM in Episodes from Liberty City, and his own Fernando Show in GTA V.[^talk-gtwiki-fernando]

**The Cipriani family** calls Lazlow twice: Toni on GTA III's Chatterbox, and his mother, Ma Cipriani, on the Liberty City Stories version.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox][^talk-gtwiki-lcfr]

**Richard Burns** files reports for WCTR News in San Andreas, then calls into Electron Zone Radio in Liberty City Stories.[^talk-gtwiki-wctr][^talk-gtwiki-lcfr]

**Maurice** is probably two people: Maurice Chavez moderates Pressing Issues in both Vice City games, while San Andreas has Gardening with Maurice, the show a Chatterbox caller mourned in GTA III. Nothing ties them together beyond the name.[^talk-gtwiki-pressing][^talk-gtawiki-wctr3d][^talk-gtwiki-sa]

**Barry Stark** calls Chatterbox in GTA III and sits on the Vice City morality panel as a naturist.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox][^talk-gtwiki-pressing]

### How was GTA talk radio written and recorded?

The early method was a two-person, low-cost operation. Lazlow and Houser's first writing session was pizza and Diet Coke at Houser's apartment.[^talk-shortlist-2024] Houser set the big picture and the main characters; in the room, he threw out ideas and Lazlow typed, graduating from a legal pad to a laptop because he could not keep up.[^talk-shortlist-2024]

Chatterbox FM was recorded in Lazlow's home studio, the studio he used for his real show, Technofile.[^talk-shortlist-2024] He recorded the voices and the fake GTA III ads there and sent the files to Houser by FTP; Houser listened to them live over a phone line.[^talk-shortlist-2024] Lazlow produced GTA III's radio and commercials, and his company Radio Lazlow co-wrote and produced the Vice City stations.[^talk-fandom-chatterbox][^talk-gtwiki-lazlow]

The cast was whoever Lazlow knew. Most of the Chatterbox voices were friends, radio contacts and neighbors; neighborhood kids did voices too, and his own father is the caller who loves eating squirrels.[^talk-shortlist-2024] Callers read their lines to him over the phone, and the test for a bit was simple. "I knew we were golden if he would break up laughing," Lazlow told ShortList in 2024.[^talk-shortlist-2024]

By GTA IV, fans were supplying material through the WKTTRadio.com line, and Rockstar's audio team said in 2008 that Weazel News bulletins would update through the story.[^talk-digitaltrends-2007][^talk-gamesradar-2008]

For GTA V, former Rockstar audio staff Craig Conner and Will Morton, two of the three senior members of Rockstar North's audio team in 2008, gave AudioTechnology a rough picture of the process.[^gf-d-gamesradar-2008q][^gf-d-audiotech] The in-car radio's talkback shows, spoof DJs and phone-in guests sat alongside about 150,000 lines of in-game dialogue voiced by about 700 actors, recorded, Morton said, in three studios running at once, each with a Rockstar director.[^gf-d-audiotech] Conner called the DJ dialogue the biggest radio job, recorded in so many locations that one actor worked from home.[^gf-d-audiotech]

### What did critics say about GTA talk radio?

The critics split along the games' own line, local weirdness versus national politics.

- **PC Gamer, 2020.** Samuel Roberts named Chatterbox his favorite GTA talk station, weird residents calling Lazlow about nonsense, and found its target funnier and more specific than the later games' politicians and technology companies. Joe Donnelly named Chakra Attack the GTA V standout, an exception among skits that can feel forced.[^talk-pcgamer-best-2020][^talk-pcgamer-smoove-2018]
- **PopMatters, 2013.** G. Christopher Williams called the ads and talk shows central to the series' satire, found the targets inconsistent, and suggested the wall of media noise may be the message. He also noted that the broadcasts often contain misogynistic, homophobic and anti-religious statements.[^talk-popmatters-2013]
- **The Guardian (undated).** Aleks Krotoski called Lazlow's Chatterbox "Howard Stern-esque anarchy" and said a Chris Evans Virgin Radio stunt was "kid's play" by comparison.[^talk-guardian-krotoski]

### Will GTA VI have talk radio?

Yes, officially: on October 8, 2026, Rockstar's radio preview confirmed talk radio for GTA VI alongside six named stations, plus on-demand podcasts for Jason and Lucia playable in vehicles and on foot, a series first.[^talk-gamesradar-gta6-2026] No talk hosts were named in that coverage.[^talk-gamesradar-gta6-2026] Lazlow, asked in 2025 whether he would appear, said he doubted it.[^talk-gamesradar-2025] The rest of the GTA VI radio is covered in [GTA VI](/gta-6/).
