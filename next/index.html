What do the words mean?#

Glossary

106 terms

  • A&R Artists and repertoire: finding artists and choosing which records a label releases. At CircoLoco Records, it sits with the CircoLoco side. read more
  • Absurd Ventures The studio Dan Houser and Lazlow Jones co-founded after leaving Rockstar. read more
  • Adaptive score Score tracks split into four to eight stems that gameplay mixes live, as in GTA Online: Los Santos Drug Wars. read more
  • After Hours The July 2018 GTA Online update that let players buy and run nightclubs with four real resident DJs. read more
  • An Extended Look The roughly 26-minute GTA VI preview that premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026. read more
  • Artist-curated station A station picked and hosted by an outside artist, such as Flying Lotus's station in GTA V, built from unreleased music from his friends. read more
  • Blaine County Radio GTA V's rural conservative talk station, which only plays inside Blaine County. read more
  • BMG Interactive Bertelsmann Music Group's video game arm in the 1990s. It published the first Grand Theft Auto in 1997 and was sold to Take-Two in 1998. read more
  • Body & Soul A New York Sunday party; its promoter John Davis partnered on Rockstar Loft, and Andi Hanley was a warm-up DJ there. read more
  • Box set A multi-disc retail soundtrack with one disc per station: Vice City's seven CDs on Epic, San Andreas' eight discs on Interscope. read more
  • Buyout A flat one-time fee with no ongoing royalties. read more
  • Call-in A format where listeners phone the host; in GTA the callers are scripted, except some 2007 fan messages used on WKTT. read more
  • Celebrity host A real artist hosting as themselves (Iggy Pop) or voicing an invented DJ (Axl Rose as Tommy "The Nightmare" Smith). read more
  • Character podcast GTA VI's on-demand shows for Jason and Lucia, playable in vehicles and on foot, announced as a series first in October 2026. read more
  • Chatterbox FM GTA III's only talk station, at 109 on the dial (outside the real FM band), where Lazlow plays himself taking calls from Liberty City's cranks. read more
  • CircoLoco The Monday party at DC10 in Ibiza, created in 1999 by Antonio Carbonaro and Andrea Pelino as a free Monday-morning after-party. read more
  • CircoLoco Records A record label announced on May 24, 2021 as a joint venture between CircoLoco and Rockstar Games. read more
  • CLR / CRL The label's catalog prefix: CLR on records, retailers and Discogs; CRL in many RockstarINTEL headlines and on the GTA wiki. Same series. read more
  • CLR Launch Party Seth Troxler's mix unlocked by finding all four Monday Dreamin' EP media sticks. read more
  • Composition The copyright in the song itself, its melody and words, usually owned by a music publisher. Also called publishing. read more
  • DC10 The club on Ibiza's Carretera de Salinas where CircoLoco runs, opened in the 1990s as a bar licensed for 80 people; it holds 1,500 today. read more
  • De-mastering Reducing the heavy compression of modern masters so newer tracks sit beside older records on the same station. read more
  • Delisting Pulling a game from digital sale, often because its licenses have expired. read more
  • Director of Music Ivan Pavlovich's title at Rockstar, which he joined in 2004; he leads music curation across games and GTA Online updates. read more
  • DMA Design The Dundee studio that made the first Grand Theft Auto and later became Rockstar North. read more
  • Dynamic score Rockstar's term for music that changes with what happens in play, used in the GTA VI album announcement. read more
  • Episodes from Liberty City The 2009 standalone bundle of GTA IV's two episodes, The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony, with three exclusive stations. read more
  • Exclusive window The stretch when a track exists only in the game before it reaches streaming; about seven weeks for Dr. Dre's Contract songs. read more
  • Fictional artist A band or song written for the game and credited to an invented act, such as Love Fist on Vice City's V-Rock. read more
  • Friends of Dean Martinez The band Bill Elm and Woody Jackson came from before scoring Red Dead Redemption. read more
  • Gang station A GTA 2 station tied to one gang's turf, such as Funami FM for the Yakuza or KGBH for the Russian mafia. read more
  • Giegling The German collective showcased on CLR 005, the 2023 various-artists record Anyway. read more
  • GTA VI: The Album A 34-track original album made by Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records, announced September 17, 2026, for release with the game on November 19, 2026. read more
  • Hotfix A small, fast patch. GTA V's 2026 Moloko removal arrived in one. read more
  • Ident A short station-branding jingle or voice tag between songs, recorded after a station's tracks are chosen. read more
  • In-game world premiere Unreleased music first heard on a GTA station, such as The Voidz on Kult FM or Dr. Dre's tracks in The Contract. read more
  • In-house music Songs written by the developer's own musicians and credited to fictional bands, as on GTA 1's seven stations. read more
  • Independence FM The custom station in GTA IV on PC (and later Chinatown Wars on iOS and Android) that plays the player's own audio files. read more
  • Integrity 2.0 Lazlow's pre-recorded, looping street-interview station in GTA IV and its episodes. read more
  • K-Chat Vice City's celebrity interview and call-in station, hosted by Amy Sheckenhausen. read more
  • Kenny's Backyard Boogie Moodymann's media-stick mix from Los Santos Tuners, found in his character Kenny's car at the LS Car Meet. read more
  • Launch Trailer Rockstar's name for the GTA VI trailer released October 15, 2026; fans call it Trailer 3. read more
  • Lazlow (character) The in-game version of writer and host Lazlow Jones, written to be sadder and less like him with each game. read more
  • LCFR Liberty City Free Radio, the five-show talk station in Liberty City Stories, including a Chatterbox slot. read more
  • Leak Unofficial early release of information, here through footage, a Spotify glitch or social posts; unconfirmed until Rockstar or the label says so. read more
  • Leonida GTA VI's fictional state, home to Vice City. read more
  • License expiry Music rights are bought for a fixed term; when they lapse, songs are patched out, as happened to GTA IV from April 26, 2018. read more
  • Licensed track An existing commercial recording cleared for use in the game, the basis of Vice City's 1980s playlist. read more
  • Linear selector The older looping next and previous station control, which could only step to the adjacent station. read more
  • Liquid-filled vinyl A record with liquid sealed between clear layers; the GTA VI edition is two transparent magenta discs with blue liquid. read more
  • Los Santos Underground Radio The After Hours station (LSUR) that loops the four residents' mixes and selections; it opened with a Solomun mix. read more
  • Love Media Donald Love's in-game company, owner of Chatterbox FM. read more
  • Machinima A film made inside a game engine, such as "The Introduction" on the San Andreas soundtrack DVD. read more
  • Master The copyright in one specific recording of a song, usually owned by a label. read more
  • Master use license Permission from a label to use a specific recording. read more
  • Mastering (radio) Processing every track so all stations sit at the same level and character, as on a real broadcast chain. read more
  • Media Which uses a license covers, such as in-game playback per platform, trailers or store pages. read more
  • Media Player A radio-wheel slot added with Los Santos Tuners in July 2021 that plays collectible media sticks. read more
  • Media stick An in-world collectible USB drive that unlocks a mix or EP in the Media Player. read more
  • Mix-down Combining a cue's stems into a balanced mix; on GTA V, Ivan Pavlovich did the mix-downs for each mission's eight stems. read more
  • Monday Dreamin' The label's 20-track debut compilation, issued as Blue, Green, Violet and Black EPs in June 2021 and as an album on July 9, 2021. read more
  • Motion capture Recording a performer's movement with suits and cameras to animate their character; Rockstar captured each resident playing a live set. read more
  • Net bookings Take-Two's measure of sales booked in a period, including digital sales, and its main guidance figure. read more
  • Next-gen edition The 2014 release of GTA V for newer consoles, which added about 165 radio tracks, 22 of them songs cut from the 2013 release. read more
  • No Soul For Sale CircoLoco's 20th-year slogan, which Antonio Carbonaro turned into a separate platform. read more
  • On-demand podcasts In-game shows for Jason and Lucia that can be played at any time, a first for the GTA series in VI. read more
  • Option A pre-agreed right to extend a license to sequels, DLC or ports. read more
  • Original score Music composed for the game itself, separate from licensed radio; Justice are composing GTA VI's. read more
  • Parody brand An in-world fictional product that mocks a real category, such as Sprunk soda or Pißwasser beer. read more
  • Perpetual license A license with no end date. It costs more and is described as rare in games. read more
  • Pledge drive A public-radio on-air fundraising appeal, parodied by VCPR's station managers. read more
  • PLR Public Liberty Radio, GTA IV's public-radio parody, with The Seance, Pacemaker and Intelligent Agenda. read more
  • Pre-save A streaming-service option that adds an unreleased album to your library automatically on release day. read more
  • Pressing Issues Maurice Chavez's panel debate show on VCPR, with three-guest panels on topics such as public safety, morality and perception. read more
  • Radio host persona An artist voicing an in-game station's DJ character, such as Bad Bunny as El Martillo on Cocoteo FM. read more
  • Radio wheel The radial station picker introduced with GTA V and GTA Online: hold D-Pad Left (Q on PC) and hover to tune. read more
  • Rare groove Crate-dug, lesser-known funk and soul; the format of San Andreas' Master Sounds 98.3. read more
  • Reactive DJ lines DJ chatter that changes with time of day, weather or events in the game world, as Rockstar described for GTA IV in 2008. read more
  • Record A Studios The in-game studio at the center of The Contract's Dr. Dre storyline. read more
  • Resident DJ A real DJ recreated in GTA Online through motion capture, playing a custom mix in player-owned clubs. read more
  • Rights holder Anyone who owns a share of a song or recording; one track can have six or more. read more
  • Robert De Negro Stage name of rapper Jonathan Wilson, who refined and performed Craig Conner's in-house hip hop for GTA 1. read more
  • Rockstar Loft A New York club night Sam Houser and Terry Donovan started in 1999 with Body & Soul promoter John Davis; it wound down in 2000. read more
  • Rockstar North Rockstar's Scottish studio (Dundee, later Edinburgh), where GTA music and audio were overseen and mastered through the early games. read more
  • Rockstar Records LLC The company RockstarINTEL and the fan wiki name as owner of the CircoLoco Records trade name; no company filing confirming it has been published. read more
  • Self Radio GTA V's custom station for the player's own audio files, added with the PC version in April 2015. read more
  • Sensor Tower An analytics firm whose models estimate digital sales; its GTA VI pre-order figures are estimates, not company data. read more
  • Sold-in Units shipped to retailers plus digital sales, as Take-Two reports them; not the same as copies bought by players. read more
  • Soundtrack supervisor The person who sources and clears licensed tracks for a game; Pavlovich and Andi Hanley held the role on GTA IV. read more
  • Spoof ad A fake radio commercial for a parody brand, written from GTA III on by Dan Houser and Lazlow and produced by Lazlow. read more
  • Station host The voice between tracks; in GTA IV and GTA V usually a real musician or DJ playing a version of themselves, such as Iggy Pop on Liberty Rock Radio or Flying Lotus on FlyLo FM. read more
  • Station imaging The DJ links, station IDs and jokes between songs; the Vice City CDs kept them in. read more
  • Stem One separate layer of a music cue, such as drums or bass, that the game brings in or drops out. GTA V missions used eight; Red Dead Redemption 2 went up to 15. read more
  • Still Slipping Los Santos Joy Orbison's station from The Cayo Perico Heist, tied to a mission placing antennas around the map. read more
  • Sync license Permission from a music publisher to use a composition in a game or film. read more
  • Takeover playlist A temporary playlist on an existing station, such as the Dre Day set on West Coast Classics, which was folded into its regular rotation in January 2022. read more
  • Talk radio All-speech GTA stations of scripted shows, callers and ads, which began in GTA III partly as a way to fill airtime cheaply. read more
  • Talk station A GTA radio station with no music, only scripted shows, call-ins and ads, such as Chatterbox FM, VCPR, WCTR or Integrity 2.0. read more
  • Term How long a license lasts. Reported game cases run from seven to 10 years. read more
  • Territory The countries a license covers; worldwide costs more than a limited set. read more
  • The Music Locker An underground club beneath The Diamond Casino, added with The Cayo Perico Heist in December 2020, with Moodymann, Keinemusik and Palms Trax as residents. read more
  • VCPR Vice City Public Radio, a public-radio parody with guilt-trip pledge drives, home of Maurice Chavez's Pressing Issues in Vice City and Vice City Stories. read more
  • Vocal album vs score album Red Dead Redemption 2 released its Daniel Lanois-produced songs and Woody Jackson's score as two separate albums in 2019. read more
  • WCTR West Coast Talk Radio, the Los Santos talk station in San Andreas (a full schedule of shows plus news) and GTA V (three shows). read more
  • Weazel News GTA's parody news brand; in GTA IV its radio bulletins update as story missions are completed. read more
  • WKTT "We Know The Truth", GTA IV's right-wing talk station, home of The Richard Bastion Show. read more
