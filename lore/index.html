The short version: Between the songs sit fake ads for parody brands, bands that exist only in the game, real stars voicing invented DJs, and news bulletins about what you just did in a mission. Most of it was written by Dan Houser and Lazlow Jones from GTA III (2001) until Lazlow left Rockstar in 2020, and a good share of what fans can recite has never been confirmed outside the fan wikis.123,93

Where did GTA's fake radio ads come from?#

The idea is older than the jokes. Radio went into the first GTA (1997) to save Craig Conner's genre-hopping demos, but memory and budget kept DJs and ads out until GTA 2, where Michael Keillor, a film and TV director working at DMA Design, wrote spots for "3rd World Bank" and a "Zoom Zoom" nasal spray.5 PC Gamer credits those ads and DJs with setting the satirical tone the series kept.5 The early games are covered at /early-radio/.

GTA III is where the format matured. Its commercials were written by Houser and Lazlow and produced by Lazlow.39 Lazlow cut them in his home studio and sent them to Houser for review, and his test was blunt: "I knew we were golden if he would break up laughing."123

Each game, Lazlow told Kinda Funny Games, sets out to satirize one place at one moment in time.94 GamesRadar's 2010 roundup of favorite ads boiled the approach down to parodying the most twisted parts of real advertising.141

What are the best-known fake brands on GTA radio?#

A parody brand is an in-world product that mocks a real category, and a spoof ad is the radio commercial selling it. The San Andreas list alone runs to about 50 commercials: ads tied to the game, ads that link to radio segments, and parodies of products of the period.142

Is Love Fist a real band?#

No. Love Fist is a fictional rock band written for Vice City (2002), heard on V-Rock with "Dangerous Bastard" and "Fist Fury", both written for the game.104 Radio Espantoso, Vice City's Latin station, also mixes in made-up acts: Alpha Banditos, Tres Apenas como eso and Unaesta.104

A fictional artist is the oldest trick in GTA radio. Even the first game's main theme, "Gangster Friday", is credited to Craig Conner as the fictional band Slumpussy.33 GTA III stocked Head Radio and Lips 106 with invented acts such as Dil-Don't, Scatwerk, Fatamarse and Boyz 2 Girlz, with Conner and Stuart Ross writing the original tracks.39 Liberty City Stories (2005) brought both stations back with writer credits including Conner and Will Morton.54

Because no label owns it, in-house music is the one part of the radio no license can expire on. The rest is covered at /licensing/.

Who is Lazlow, and why does his character keep getting worse?#

Lazlow Jones is a real radio host who co-wrote and produced all the radio in Vice City and San Andreas and also plays a version of himself on air, from Chatterbox FM in GTA III to Chattersphere in GTA V.125 The joke is that the character fails upward and then just fails. Speaking to IGN at San Diego Comic-Con, Lazlow said Houser deliberately made the in-game Lazlow worse as a person and less like the real Lazlow with every game; for GTA V, Houser's answer to his script question included dancing in his underwear.126

He left Rockstar in 2020 after nine GTA games and joined Houser at Absurd Ventures.93 Asked in 2025 whether he would turn up in GTA VI, he said he doubted it.127 His full on-air run, his exit and the talk shows he wrote are covered at /talk-radio/.

Does GTA radio react to what you do in missions?#

In GTA IV, yes, more than in any game before it. Rockstar's audio team told GamesRadar in 2008 that the DJs had more AI than before and responded to time of day, weather and events in Liberty City, and that Weazel News delivered interactive bulletins as the story moved on.132 Weazel News is GTA's parody news brand, and its radio updates usually pair one mission-linked story with one or two unrelated ones.150

A few, with the trigger mission where there is one:150

  • An explosion in Bohan after "Rigged to Blow"
  • The deaths of the McReary brothers after "Blood Brothers"
  • Tom Goldberg's death after "Final Interview"
  • The attack on Bobby Jefferson's motorcade after "Entourage"
  • Pressure on Mayor Ochoa to reopen the bridges, then reports that he has
  • The Lost and Damned adds stories such as Arthur Stubbs's death and released stockbrokers tied to "Off Route"; The Ballad of Gay Tony adds Evan Moss's murder after "Frosting on the Cake" and an Annihilator explosion near Rotterdam Tower after "High Dive"

GTA Online ties music, rather than news, to missions. After Hours (2018) unlocked its four nightclub residents' mixes through setup missions,84 and The Contract (2021) built story missions around six new Dr. Dre tracks.89 Both are covered at /online/.

How does the radio wheel work, and how did station switching change?#

Station switching has been a design choice since the first game. On the PC version of GTA (1997) the stations are plain CD audio, so you can swap in your own CD.33 GTA 2 tied radio to territory: each area hears five of its 11 stations, including gang stations such as Funami FM for the Yakuza.35

Before GTA V, the control was a linear selector: a looping next and previous button that could only step to the neighboring station. The radio wheel, a radial picker, arrived with GTA V and GTA Online: hold D-Pad Left (Q on PC) and hover on a station for about a second to tune it.151 The 2021 Definitive Editions of GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas got a wheel too. Fans claim GTA VI goes back to a linear selector; Rockstar hasn't said so.151

In GTA Online the dial also gains the Media Player, a customizable station that only plays music from Media Sticks found around Los Santos.88 For how real stations get a signal into a car, the sister guide https://airwaves.cold.fyi/ covers the transmitters.

What Easter eggs are hidden in GTA radio?#

The radio rewards close listening and long memories.

  • Stations you can't tune. Billboards in GTA III advertise Liberty FM, WLLC "The Zone" and Liberty Soul FM, none of which exist on the dial.39
  • The host who never shows. On GTA Online's Kult FM, Julian Casablancas never reaches the booth, so producer Zachary (David Cross) fills in and calls back to Zero, the San Andreas RC shop owner. On Still Slipping, artists' answering-machine messages cut in between tracks.89
  • A robot DJ. GTA IV's ambient station, The Journey, is "hosted" by a computer voice made with Mac text-to-speech.66
  • A broken guest spot. Skepta's playlist on iFruit Radio has not played since the Los Santos Drug Wars update, leaving only Danny Brown's half.65
  • Cheat codes in a CD box. Each disc in the 2002 Vice City box set unlocked a cheat on vicecityradio.com.44
  • The station that never aired (rumor). Fans say Kavinsky was reported in early 2012 as host of a planned GTA V station called "Nightride FM". It never appeared and the original report hasn't turned up, so file it under rumor.115

GTA VI keeps the format and adds to it. On October 8, 2026, Rockstar previewed six stations and confirmed talk radio plus on-demand podcasts for Jason and Lucia, playable in vehicles and on foot, which it called a series first.139 GamesRadar's report named no hosts; others on the same announcement did, from Bad Bunny on Cocoteo FM to Robyn on Flash FM; neither names the podcast hosts or any of the writers.152,153 The full station list is at /gta-6/. Lazlow's line about the studio's priorities still fits: "Comedy is a key part of our DNA."123

Quick answers

Is Love Fist a real band?
No. Love Fist is a fictional rock band written for GTA Vice City (2002). Its songs "Dangerous Bastard" and "Fist Fury" play on V-Rock and were made for the game.
Is Axl Rose really in GTA San Andreas?
Yes. Axl Rose voices K-DST's DJ, Tommy "The Nightmare" Smith. Chuck D, George Clinton and Sly and Robbie also voice San Andreas DJs.
What radio station is Karl Lagerfeld on in GTA IV?
K109 The Studio, GTA IV's disco station. His casting as a DJ made fashion news in 2008.
Does the GTA IV radio news react to your missions?
Yes. Weazel News bulletins change as the story moves, reporting things like the McReary brothers' deaths after "Blood Brothers". Rockstar's audio team described the system in 2008.
Why does Lazlow's character get worse in every GTA?
Lazlow told IGN at San Diego Comic-Con that Dan Houser deliberately made the in-game Lazlow sadder and less like him each game. In GTA V, Houser told him he would get to dance in his underwear.
Is Lazlow in GTA 6?
Lazlow left Rockstar in 2020 and joined Dan Houser at Absurd Ventures. Asked in 2025 whether he would appear in GTA VI, he said he doubted it.
